**House rule:** read the seven novels. Insert a novella if you have it. Do not postpone *Empire of Silence* until you own every *Tales of the Sun Eater* volume. *The Murdered Sun* has been described as a non-canonical early version of book one; it is not a prequel and not an on-ramp.

This is not Dune, even when a blurb rhymes. You do not need Herbert first.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with book seven because it is the ending.
- Treating the novellas as required homework.
- Opening a Cosmere-style spreadsheet of every short.

## FAQ

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No. Start at *Empire of Silence* (2018). The novels carry the confession.

**Is the series finished?**  
The seven novels are published as a close.

**Can I start with Howling Dark?**  
No.
