The official site says the books can be read in any order, then lists them in the order they were written. For a first read, written order is the honest path: you meet Reacher the way Child introduced him. Do not start with a prequel because it is earlier in his army life. Those books assume you already know the man who got off the bus.

Short version of the order question: [Jack Reacher: publication or chronological?](/questions/jack-reacher-publication-or-chronological/). This page is the numbered list.

Andrew Child co-authors from *The Sentinel* (2020) onward. That is a handoff, not a new series and not a new book one. *Chain Reaction* is announced for October 2026. It is not on the shelf yet. Do not wait for it before starting.

## The “any order” claim, with exceptions

Most Reacher novels are built as a man arriving in a new town. That is why publishers say you can start anywhere. It is half true.

The exceptions are the point of a reading-order page:

- **Prequels in the middle of the list.** *The Enemy*, *The Affair*, *Night School*, and *The Secret* jump backward. Publication order keeps them from becoming accidental book ones.
- **A four-book chain.** *61 Hours*, *Worth Dying For*, *A Wanted Man*, and *Never Go Back* follow one leftover job. Read those four in order even if you hop around elsewhere.
- **Recurring names.** Neagley, the 110th, and a few family facts accumulate. You can follow a standalone plot without them. You will not follow why the plot is expensive.

**House rule:** publication order for a first pass. Chronological charts that begin with *The Enemy* or *The Secret* are reread toys.

## Shorts and the screen

**No Middle Name** (2017) is a short-story collection, including “Too Much Time.” It is not a numbered novel. Optional cleanup after you already like the voice.

The Prime series is a cousin. Season one draws on *Killing Floor*; later seasons jump. Season order is not book order. The Tom Cruise films are a different cousin.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Enemy* or *The Affair* because a “chronological Reacher” list put them first.
- Treating *One Shot* as book one because you saw a film.
- Skipping the 61 Hours chain and wondering why *Never Go Back* feels mid-conversation.
- Waiting for *Chain Reaction* (announced 2026) before opening *Killing Floor*.
- Searching only “Child” and picking up a later hardcover as an on-ramp.

## FAQ

**Jack Reacher books in order — publication or chronological?**  
Publication. Start with *Killing Floor*. Chronological prequels are mid-run books, not book one. Short version: [publication or chronological?](/questions/jack-reacher-publication-or-chronological/).

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
For a first pass, yes. For a reread, hop if you want — except the four-book chain and the prequels you should not use as doors.

**Can I start with The Enemy or The Affair?**  
Not for a first read. They jump backward. They assume you already know Reacher.

**Which title is book 4?**  
*Running Blind* in the US, *The Visitor* in the UK. Same book.

**When did Andrew Child take over?**  
Co-author from *The Sentinel* (2020). Still the same numbered run.

**Is Exit Strategy a novella?**  
Penguin has called the 2025 book a Reacher story in a shorter jacket. It is still the latest published volume in the written-order list, not a starting point.

**Is the Prime show a substitute?**  
No. It is an adaptation that jumps. Season order is not book order.
