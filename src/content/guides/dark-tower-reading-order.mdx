**Contested placement, labeled:** King has said this one stands on its own and also sits between books 4 and 5. For a first read, either:

- read the seven, then *Keyhole*, or
- insert *Keyhole* after *Wizard and Glass* if you already have it.

Do not start with it. Do not postpone *The Gunslinger* until you own it.

## Related King books — optional

Charts that require *The Stand*, *’Salem’s Lot*, *Insomnia*, *Hearts in Atlantis*, and *Everything’s Eventual* before Roland are doing Cosmere-spreadsheet energy in Maine. Those books reward a later reread. They are not Dark Tower 0.

**House rule:** read the Tower. If a later volume makes you curious about a specific other King title, go read that title then. Do not build a 20-book prerequisite list.

The 2017 film is a cousin that compresses. It is not a table of contents.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with the 2017 movie and then opening *Wolves of the Calla*.
- Reading *The Stand* first “so the Tower makes sense.”
- Skipping *The Gunslinger* because someone said the revised edition is required and your library has the old one. Either text is book one; the revised is what most new copies are.
- Treating *Keyhole* as book one because it is friendlier.

## FAQ

**Revised Gunslinger or original?**  
Either is book one. New printings are usually the 2003 revision.

**Do I need The Stand first?**  
No.

**Where does Keyhole go?**  
Between 4 and 5, or after the seven. Not first.

**Is the series finished?**  
The seven plus *Keyhole* are the published Tower path.
