**House rule:** read 1–12. *Threshold* is optional after you want more of the same friends. Wight’s other series (*Traveler’s Gate*, *Elder Empire*) are other shelves, not Cradle 0.

Book one is thinner on purpose. That is not a reason to start on *Blackflame*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting mid-series because someone said “it gets good at book three.”
- Treating *Threshold* as an on-ramp.
- Mixing in *Dungeon Crawler Carl* as if they share a ranking system.
