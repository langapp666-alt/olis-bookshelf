*We Solve Murders* starts a different Osman series. It is not Thursday Murder Club 6.

The Netflix film is a cousin of book one. It is not a reason to skip to book five.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with the newest hardcover.
- Treating *We Solve Murders* as a continuation.
- Using the film as a table of contents for the later novels.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
For a first pass, yes. The cases are separate; the people are not.

**Is We Solve Murders book 6?**  
No.

**Film first?**  
The film is a cousin of book one.
