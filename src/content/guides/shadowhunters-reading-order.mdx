Several trilogies (and one six-book run) share a world. Clare’s FAQ is short: most people read Mortal Instruments, then Infernal Devices, then Dark Artifices, then Last Hours, then the later Wicked Powers books when they exist.

Publication order after *City of Glass* interleaves Infernal Devices with the last three Mortal Instruments novels. That is a documented second path, not a reason to start in 1878.

**Contested order, labeled:**

- **Mortal Instruments (all six), Infernal Devices, Dark Artifices, Last Hours.** Clare’s “most people read like this” list. This house’s first recommendation if you want the whole shelf.
- **Mortal Instruments 1–3, then Infernal Devices interleaved with Mortal Instruments 4–6.** Publication after *City of Glass*. Allowed. Fun if you like watching the two lines arrive together. Not required.
- **Clockwork Angel first.** Allowed if you only want the Victorian trilogy. Then read Mortal Instruments before Dark Artifices. Last Hours is a sequel of sorts to Infernal Devices; Clare says it does not spoil the modern books.

*The Wicked Powers* is the later modern close Clare names after Last Hours. It is not on the shelf as a finished trilogy on this page. When those books exist, they are not a new book one.

## Extras — after Mortal Instruments

Clare: *Tales from the Shadowhunter Academy*, *The Eldest Curses*, and *The Bane Chronicles* should be read after Mortal Instruments. Read Infernal Devices before *Ghosts of the Shadow Market*.

**House rule:** novels by cycle first. Insert a collection if you have it. Do not start the Chronicles on *The Red Scrolls of Magic* because Magnus is on the cover.

The *Shadowhunters* television series rearranges people and years. It is not a book order.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Lady Midnight* because Emma is the new hardcover.
- Starting with *Clockwork Angel* and then jumping to *Queen of Air and Darkness*.
- Using the show as a table of contents.
- Treating *The Bane Chronicles* as book one of the franchise.
- Stopping after *City of Glass* and calling the series finished without knowing three more Clary novels exist.

## FAQ

**Infernal Devices or Mortal Instruments first?**  
Mortal Instruments if you want Clare’s usual path. Infernal Devices if you only want 1878. Dark Artifices last among the modern books.

**Do I need the extras?**  
No.

**Is Last Hours a prequel I should read first?**  
No. It follows Infernal Devices.

**TV order?**  
Not a book order.
