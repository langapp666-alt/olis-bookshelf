A finished run. Hearne’s own chronology page is the source for where the shorts go. Publication order of the novels is the first-read path. *Besieged* is a collection he calls vital even though it is not a conventional novel. It still is not book one.

*Ink & Sigil* is a later neighboring series. After *Scourged* if you want it. Not Iron Druid 0.

Hearne numbers *Besieged* as book 9 on his chronology page: ten stories, some from Atticus’s past, some after *Staked*, bridging into *Scourged*. Retailer lists sometimes put *Scourged* before *Besieged*. Use his page, not the sort order.

**House rule:** novels 1–8, then *Besieged*, then *Scourged*. If you only have the novels, read 1–8 and *Scourged* and know you skipped a bridge he considers load-bearing.

## Common mistakes

- Starting at *Dead Beat*—wrong series. That is Dresden.
- Starting with *Scourged* because it is the last book and “the payoff.”
- Reading *Besieged* first because short stories look like a sampler.
- Treating *Ink & Sigil* as Iron Druid book one.
- Using a retailer list that files *Besieged* after *Scourged*.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read Besieged?**  
Hearne says it is vital as a bridge. It is still not an on-ramp. If you skip it, go *Staked* then *Scourged* and accept the gap.

**Can I start with Grimoire of the Lamb?**  
You can. You will meet Atticus without the series’ first case. This house starts at *Hounded*.

**Is the series finished?**  
The Chronicles end at *Scourged*. *Ink & Sigil* continues the neighborhood.

**Where do the Oberon mysteries go?**  
After *Staked*, as extras. Not Iron Druid 8.5 required homework.
