Short version of the door: [Where to start The First Law](/questions/where-to-start-first-law/). This page is the full world map.

Abercrombie’s site groups the world the way the books were written: First Law trilogy, then three standalones in that world, then *Sharp Ends*, then the Age of Madness trilogy. Publication order is chronology for the novels that matter. That is not an accident.

*The Devils* (2025) is a new series. It is not First Law book 11. *The Shattered Sea* is a different trilogy. Leave both off this spine.

## The three standalones

They are standalone in the sense that each has a new main job. They are not standalone in the sense that they forget the trilogy. Age of Madness later assumes you know what these books did to the map.

The stories sit all over the timeline. Some are earlier than the trilogy. That does not make the collection book 0. Read it after *Red Country*, or skip it. A later pamphlet, *The Great Change (And Other Lies)* (2023), is more Age of Madness extra. Same rule: after the novels it sits beside, not as homework.

## The Age of Madness

A second trilogy, same world, later generation. Publication order. For the later three as their own page, see [Age of Madness](/guides/age-of-madness-reading-order/).

If you only wanted one finished grimdark trilogy, you can stop after *Last Argument of Kings*. That is a complete story. It is not the whole First Law world Abercrombie kept writing.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *A Little Hatred* because a shop called it a new series. It is a sequel generation.
- Starting with *Best Served Cold* because “standalone” looked safer.
- Treating the standalones as optional flavor text before Age of Madness.
- Sliding *The Devils* or *Shattered Sea* into the numbered column.
- Reading *Sharp Ends* first because some stories are earlier.

## FAQ

**Can I read Age of Madness first?**  
You can. You will be standing in a family argument you did not attend.

**Are the standalones required?**  
Required for the *world* as Abercrombie continued it. Not required if you only wanted the original trilogy.

**Do I need Sharp Ends?**  
No.

**Is The Devils First Law?**  
No.

**Which book is the shortest on-ramp?**  
*The Blade Itself*. There is not a gentler door.
