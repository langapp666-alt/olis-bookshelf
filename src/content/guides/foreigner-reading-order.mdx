Long first-contact diplomacy, sold as encapsulated trilogy arcs. Cherryh’s own site: start at the beginning if you can; arc-first is allowed. Publication order across the arcs is the path that keeps Bren’s career from arriving as footnotes.

The opening of *Foreigner* includes early colonial history before Bren’s present. Cherryh has said to be patient with those chapters. They are not a separate prequel novel.

**Contested skip, labeled:** Cherryh allows the first book of a later arc as an entry. **House rule:** *Foreigner* first. Arc-first is for readers who already know they want a later political problem, not for “where do I start Foreigner.”

Self-published short prequels (*Deliberations*, *Invitations*) are optional extras. Not book one.

Alliance-Union, Chanur, and Finisterre are other Cherryh shelves. They are not Foreigner.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Precursor* or *Destroyer* because “each trilogy stands alone.”
- Starting with *Defiance* because it is the newest.
- Skipping the slow opening of *Foreigner* and calling the series unfinished history.
- Mixing Chanur or Downbelow Station into the Foreigner numbered list.

## FAQ

**Can I start at a later arc?**  
Cherryh says yes. This house still starts at *Foreigner*.

**Do I need the short prequels?**  
No.

**Is Defiance a good on-ramp?**  
No. It assumes decades of Bren.

**Is the series finished?**  
Arc 8 is incomplete as of this page. Arcs 1–7 are complete three-book runs.
