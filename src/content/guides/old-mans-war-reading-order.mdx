**House rule:** 1–3, then decide on *Zoe’s Tale*. Then the two later books if you want more Colonial Union. *Zoe’s Tale* is not a hidden book one and not required to follow the later volumes.

*The Collapsing Empire* is the Interdependency. Different door. *Redshirts* is a standalone. Neither is Old Man’s War 7.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Human Division* because the episodes look skippable.
- Treating *Zoe’s Tale* as book one because it is younger.
- Mixing in Interdependency titles.

## FAQ

**Do I need Zoe’s Tale?**  
No. It retells *The Last Colony* from another shoulder.

**Is the series finished?**  
The published sequence through *The End of All Things* is a pause you can treat as an ending.

**Collapsing Empire first?**  
No. Different series.
