**House rule:** read *Hyperion* and *The Fall of Hyperion* as one work in two volumes. Then decide if you want Endymion. You can stop after the first pair. You cannot start on the second pair.

Related Simmons shorts exist. They are not a fifth novel and not an on-ramp.

## Common mistakes

- Stopping after *Hyperion* because the structure feels complete. *Fall* is the rest of the sentence.
- Starting with *Endymion* because a list called it “more accessible.”
- Treating the four as standalone pilgrimages.
