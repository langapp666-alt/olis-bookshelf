Three novels issued months apart, then a novella, then a much later Kylar book. Publication order is the first-read path. The trilogy was written as one job. Do not hop.

[Lightbringer](/guides/lightbringer-reading-order/) is a different Weeks shelf. It is not Night Angel 0, and Night Angel is not Lightbringer homework. If you like the writer more than this particular assassin, that five is the usual next Weeks door.

## Perfect Shadow — later, not first

**Contested order, labeled:** chronology puts *Perfect Shadow* before *The Way of Shadows*. Retail and some “complete Night Angel” lists file it as book 0. **House rule:** after *Beyond the Shadows*. It is a reward for already knowing Durzo. Taken first, it answers the trilogy in advance.

*I, Night Angel* is a short Weeks extra around the same shelf. Optional. Not a second novella you must finish before book two.

## Night Angel Nemesis — after, not instead

*Nemesis* is the start of a later Kylar sequence, not a reboot. It assumes the settlement of *Beyond the Shadows*. Do not start here because it is the recent hardcover.

## Path to Lightbringer

Weeks wrote Lightbringer between the original Night Angel three and *Nemesis*. The five Lightbringer novels are a finished, separate argument. You do not need Night Angel first. You do not need Lightbringer to finish Kylar.

**House rule:** trilogy, then *Perfect Shadow* if you want Durzo’s earlier name, then *Nemesis* if you want more Kylar. If the first three were not for you, skip the extras and use the [Lightbringer guide](/guides/lightbringer-reading-order/) as a different Weeks door.

The graphic novel of *The Way of Shadows* is an adaptation. It is not Night Angel 0.5.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Perfect Shadow* because it is shorter, earlier, or labeled 0.5.
- Starting with *Night Angel Nemesis* because it is the new one.
- Treating Lightbringer as a sequel, or Night Angel as Lightbringer preamble.
- Stopping after *The Way of Shadows* because someone said book one is “just the apprentice years.” The three are one arc.

## FAQ

**Perfect Shadow first?**  
No. After the trilogy. It spoils if you use it as a door.

**Do I need Night Angel before Lightbringer?**  
No.

**Is Nemesis a fourth Night Angel novel?**  
It is a later Kylar book, start of a new sequence. Read it after the trilogy, not instead of it.

**Can I start at book two?**  
No.
