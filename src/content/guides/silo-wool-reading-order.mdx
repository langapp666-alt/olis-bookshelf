The Apple TV+ series is called *Silo*. The books were called *Wool* first. Same underground argument. If your paperback says *Wool Omnibus*, you are holding book one in the form Howey later collected.

Howey’s line is the house line: *Wool* introduces the silo, *Shift* tells the making, *Dust* the undoing. Chronological charts that open with *Shift* are a reread toy. *Shift* is written for people who already have *Wool*’s questions.

## The original Wool parts

*Wool* began as a short and grew into five linked parts (*Wool*, *Proper Gauge*, *Casting Off*, *The Unraveling*, *The Stranded*). If you already own those five separately, read them in numbered order; together they *are* the novel *Wool*. If you own the omnibus, do not also buy the five parts thinking they are sequels.

*Shift* likewise collected *First Shift*, *Second Shift*, and *Third Shift*. The collected novel is the intended book.

## Shorts and other Howey

Three Silo shorts — “In the Air,” “In the Mountain,” “In the Woods” — have been bundled with later editions. They are optional texture after the trilogy, not a fourth novel.

*Sand*, *Beacon 23*, and the Molly Fyde books are other Howey shelves. They are not Silo 4–6. The show is a cousin that rearranges. Show order is not book order.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Shift* because it is “historically first.”
- Reading only the first *Wool* novella and wondering why the story feels unfinished.
- Treating the TV title *Silo* as a different book one.
- Waiting for a new silo novel before opening *Wool*.

## FAQ

**Wool or Silo?**  
*Wool* is the first novel. *Silo* is the series / show name.

**Should I read Shift first?**  
No. Howey lists it second.

**Do I need the five original Wool parts?**  
Only if you do not have the collected *Wool*.

**Is the show a substitute?**  
No. It jumps and invents.
