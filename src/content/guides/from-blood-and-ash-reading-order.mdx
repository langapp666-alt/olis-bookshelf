Two related runs: the Blood and Ash novels, and the Flesh and Fire prequels. Publication of the main series is the first-read path. The prequels assume you already want this world. For the four Sera novels and the official weave, see [Flesh and Fire](/guides/flesh-and-fire-reading-order/). Short version of the interleave: [Flesh and Fire before or after Blood and Ash?](/questions/flesh-and-fire-before-blood-and-ash/).

**Contested order, labeled:** some readers insert Flesh and Fire after FBAA book 3 or 4, when the main series starts leaning on that earlier age. Others wait until they finish the published Poppy books. **House rule:** do not start with *A Shadow in the Ember*. If you want the crossover texture earlier, the least damaging insert is after *The Crown of Gilded Bones*, not before *From Blood and Ash*.

Novellas and extra scenes exist. Optional. Not a hidden book one.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with Flesh and Fire because it is chronological.
- Starting with *A Soul of Ash and Blood* because it looks like a beginning.
- Mixing in the *Covenant* or *Lux* shelves as if they were the same series.

## FAQ

**Flesh and Fire first?**  
Not for a first read.

**Is the series finished?**  
No. Read what is published in order.

**Is Soul of Ash and Blood skippable?**  
You can skip it and lose a retell. Do not start with it.
