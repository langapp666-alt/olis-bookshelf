Five novels and four novellas. Brett published a binge-read order that weaves the novellas between the novels. The novels alone still carry the series. UK/US title split on book one is the other common trap.

*The Desert Spear* can be read without book one per Brett — he does not advise it. **House rule:** *The Warded Man* / *The Painted Man* first.

**House rule:** novels first if you want the shortest complete path. If you want Brett’s maximum-effect binge, insert the novellas where his guide places them: after *Warded Man* (*Brayan's Gold*, *Great Bazaar*), after *Daylight War* (*Messenger's Legacy*), and *Barren* with or after *The Core*.

Do not start the series on a novella because it is chronologically earlier.

## Common mistakes

- Buying *The Painted Man* and *The Warded Man* as two different book ones.
- Starting with *The Desert Spear* because the new POV looks like a soft reboot.
- Treating the novellas as required homework before book one.
- Mixing *The Nightfall Saga* (later Brett world) into the Demon Cycle numbered list without checking.

## FAQ

**Warded Man or Painted Man?**  
Same novel. US vs UK/AU title.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No. Recommended by Brett for a binge; not required to finish the five.

**Can I start at Desert Spear?**  
Brett allows it and does not advise it. This house starts at *Warded Man*.

**Is the series finished?**  
The Demon Cycle novels are complete. Later Brett books in neighboring worlds are separate doors.
