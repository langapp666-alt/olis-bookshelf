Tahir’s site and the BOOM/Archaia line treat these as standalone early chapters, not a fifth numbered novel. A later graphic volume may exist. It will not be book one of the prose.

**House rule:** read the four. Add a graphic if you want Elias or Laia earlier. Do not open *A Thief Among the Trees* because it is “before.”

*All My Rage* is a different Tahir book.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *A Thief Among the Trees* because it is earlier at Blackcliff.
- Starting with *A Sky Beyond the Storm* because it is the last argument people remember.
- Treating the graphics as required homework before *A Torch Against the Night*.
- Expecting a fifth prose novel. The story closes in book four.
