Nearly forty novels. Two honest doors. Publication order introduces Drizzt the way 1988 readers met him. Chronological / Legend numbering starts in Menzoberranzan with *Homeland*. This page is an **entry map** through the first six, then a labeled handoff. It is not a full Forgotten Realms spreadsheet.

**Contested order, labeled:** shops and omnibus spines number *Homeland* as book one. That path is fine and widely recommended. **House rule:** *The Crystal Shard* first if you asked for reading order without specifying chronology. After Icewind Dale, read the Dark Elf three as origin, then continue with *The Legacy*. If you prefer origin-first, do Dark Elf → Icewind Dale, then the same onward list.

Paths of Darkness, Sellswords (Jarlaxle/Entreri side path), Hunter’s Blades, Transitions, Neverwinter, and later trilogies follow in publication order. Sellswords can wait; it is companion-heavy, not a new Drizzt on-ramp.

**House rule:** finish Icewind Dale + Dark Elf (in either order), then *The Legacy* onward. Do not jump to *Gauntlgrym* because a film poster put that title in front of you.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Homeland* because it says Legend of Drizzt #1, then being surprised Icewind Dale was written first.
- Starting with *The Crystal Shard* and never reading *Homeland* — fine if you only wanted the companions’ first adventure; incomplete if you wanted his origin.
- Starting mid-series on Hunter’s Blades or Neverwinter because those covers are in print.
- Mixing every Forgotten Realms novel into one Drizzt column.

## FAQ

**Homeland or Crystal Shard first?**  
Chronology: *Homeland*. Publication / this house default: *Crystal Shard*. Both are labeled doors.

**Do I need Sellswords?**  
No. Optional side path for Jarlaxle and Entreri.

**Is this all of Forgotten Realms?**  
No. Drizzt is one spine. Other Realms series are separate doors.

**How many books are there?**  
Dozens. Use the first six to decide whether you want the rest.
