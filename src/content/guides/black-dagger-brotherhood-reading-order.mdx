A long vampire-warrior run. Publication order, as Ward lists it, is the first-read path. The cases and pairings accumulate. So do the politics.

Ward’s page interleaves the Legacy novels and marks an Insider’s Guide. This house keeps the Brotherhood novels in her sequence and puts Legacy and the extras in labeled rooms. A first read can be Brotherhood novels only.

*The Black Dagger Brotherhood: An Insider’s Guide* sits after *Lover Enshrined* on Ward’s list. It is a companion, not novel 7. A later anniversary guide exists. Neither is an on-ramp.

*Lover Enthroned* is an announced later Brotherhood novel. It is not a starting point.

**House rule:** read Brotherhood in her novel order. Insert a Legacy book if you have it and her list puts it after a Brotherhood title you already read. Do not start the franchise on *Blood Kiss*.

## Other rooms

- **Black Dagger Prison Camp** (*The Jackal* and on) — neighboring. After you like the world, not instead of *Dark Lover*.
- **In-world novellas** (*Father Mine*, *Dearest Ivie*, *Prisoner of Night*, the winter books) — extras. Out of the numbered novel column.
- **Fallen Angels**, **The Bourbon Kings**, and later Ward fantasy — different shelves.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The King* because Wrath is the one you heard about.
- Starting with *Blood Kiss* because Legacy looks shorter.
- Treating an Insider’s Guide as novel 7.
- Using a retailer “book 24” sort as a table of contents.

## FAQ

**Do I need Legacy?**  
No. The Brotherhood novels carry the argument. Start at *Dark Lover* (2002).

**Do I need the Insider’s Guide?**  
No.

**Can I start at The King?**  
You can. You will spend several books standing on a porch you never walked up.

**Is Lover Enthroned a starting point?**  
No.
