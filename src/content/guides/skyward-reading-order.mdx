A finished four-novel YA SF run with three novellas in the middle. Publication order, with the shorts in their advertised gaps, is the path.

For Cosmere doors, use the [Cosmere starter](/guides/cosmere-starter-reading-order/), [Mistborn](/guides/mistborn-era-1-2-reading-order/), or [Stormlight](/guides/stormlight-archive-reading-order/).

**House rule:**

1. *Skyward*
2. *Starsight*
3. *Sunreach* and *ReDawn* if you have them
4. *Cytonic*
5. *Evershore* if you have it
6. *Defiant*

If your library lacks the novellas, keep going. Do not stall a four-book series on three shorts. They are also collected as *Skyward Flight*.

## Common mistakes

- Treating Skyward as Cosmere homework.
- Starting with *Defiant*.
- Starting with *Sunreach* because it is shorter.
- Opening a Cosmere spreadsheet because the author matches.

## What to read next

1. **[Red Rising](/guides/red-rising-reading-order/) (Pierce Brown)** — If you want another climb out of a stacked sky, colder.
2. **[Mistborn Era 1](/guides/mistborn-era-1-2-reading-order/) (Brandon Sanderson)** — If you want his other finished trilogy door, which *is* Cosmere.
3. **[The Reckoners](/guides/reckoners-reading-order/) (Brandon Sanderson)** — If you want his other non-Cosmere YA SF door: a finished trilogy, with a later Texas book that is not book one.
4. **[Old Man’s War](/guides/old-mans-war-reading-order/) (John Scalzi)** — If you want another approachable military-SF first book.
5. **Stop after Defiant.** The four novels are a complete visit.

## FAQ

**Is Skyward Cosmere?**  
No.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No. They help.

**Is the series finished?**  
The four novels plus the three novellas are a published close.
