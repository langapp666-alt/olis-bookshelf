The North American spine is often sold as Abhorsen. Same books. Publication order is the first-read path: meet the Charter, the bells, and the Wall the way Nix introduced them.

Boxed sets and “complete Old Kingdom” lists that open with *Clariel* are doing chronology. That is a reread toy.

Read *Lirael* and *Abhorsen* as one job. Stopping between them is how people lose the thread.

*The Creature in the Case* was a 2005 World Book Day volume, then collected in *Across the Wall*. It is a Nicholas Sayre story set just after the trilogy. **House rule:** if you want it, after *Abhorsen* and before *Goldenhand*. It is not optional homework, and it is not book one.

**Contested order, labeled:**

- **Publication, with the novella before Goldenhand.** This house’s first recommendation.
- **Prequels last.** Allowed. *Clariel* and *Terciel and Elinor* are richer once you already know what the Abhorsen line costs.
- **Clariel first because it is earliest.** Do not. It hands you a name the original three are built to meet later.

*To Hold the Bridge* is another Old Kingdom short, collected in a 2015 volume of the same name. Optional. Not a third prequel door.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Clariel* because a chronological chart numbered it 1.
- Starting with *Terciel and Elinor* because it looks like a parents-and-origin story, therefore book one.
- Skipping *The Creature in the Case* and then wondering why *Goldenhand* assumes a Nicholas you have not seen since the trilogy — or treating the novella as a required fourth novel.
- Mixing in *Shade’s Children* or the *Keys to the Kingdom* shelf as if they were Old Kingdom.

## FAQ

**Clariel first?**  
No. *Sabriel* (1995) is book one. *Clariel* is a later-published prequel. Read it after the original three.

**Do I need The Creature in the Case?**  
No. If you want it, after *Abhorsen* and before *Goldenhand*. It is a novella, also sold as *Nicholas Sayre and the Creature in the Case*.

**Is Terciel and Elinor book one?**  
No. It is a prequel about Sabriel’s parents. Start with *Sabriel*.

**Abhorsen or Old Kingdom?**  
Same series. North American printings often use Abhorsen on the spine.
