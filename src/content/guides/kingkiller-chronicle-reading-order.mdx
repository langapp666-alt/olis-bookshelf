Two novels published. A third announced for a long time. This page will not invent a date. Side novellas are extras about other rooms in the same building. They are not a prequel path that unlocks book one.

Read them in this order. Waiting for book three before opening book one is how people never start. The incomplete state is real; it is not a reason to skip to a novella.

**House rule:** novels first. *Slow Regard* is optional aftercare with a very particular voice. *Narrow Road* is a later expansion of a short. Neither replaces *The Doors of Stone*, and neither is book 0.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Slow Regard* because it is shorter.
- Treating the series as abandoned and therefore not starting.
- Searching “Kingkiller” and picking up a commemorative or art book as novel 3.
- Reading a spoiler-heavy theory wiki between books one and two.

## FAQ

**What is the Kingkiller Chronicle reading order?**  
Start with *The Name of the Wind* (2007), then *The Wise Man’s Fear* (2011). Novellas are optional aftercare. There is no honest book-three placement until a third novel exists.

**Is there a book three?**  
Announced. Not a reading-order problem until it is a book.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No.

**Can I start with Narrow Road?**  
No.
