  ACOTAR reading order: start with *A Court of Thorns and Roses* (2015), then publication order. *A Court of Silver Flames* is not a new series. You do not need Throne of Glass first. Do not start with a Crescent City novel.
</StartHere>

ACOTAR is one courtly argument in publication order. The novella is a pause after book three, not a new book one. Further ACOTAR novels have been announced for 2026–2027; they are not on the shelf as you start, and they are not a reason to wait.

Short version of the door: [Where to start ACOTAR](/questions/where-to-start-acotar/). Maas desk choice: [ACOTAR or Throne of Glass first?](/questions/acotar-or-throne-of-glass/). After this run: [What to read after ACOTAR](/questions/what-to-read-after-acotar/).

For Throne of Glass and the crossover problem, see [TOG](/guides/throne-of-glass-reading-order/) and [Crescent City](/guides/crescent-city-reading-order/). TOG and ACOTAR do not spoil each other. Crescent City book two onward does.

**House rule:** 1–3, then the novella, then *Silver Flames*. You can skip *Frost and Starlight* if your library does not have it; you will miss a pause, not a secret war. Do not skip from book one to *Silver Flames* because a clip said Nesta is the point.

Announced later ACOTAR volumes are sequels, not prequels. When they exist, they go on the end of this table.

## Maasverse, kept in its place

You do not need Throne of Glass before ACOTAR. You do not need ACOTAR before Throne of Glass. You **do** want both substantially finished before Crescent City’s second novel if you care about named crossovers. TOG’s own trap is the prequel collection: [Should I read Assassin’s Blade first?](/questions/assassins-blade-first/).

This page will not list a 20-title “Maas chronological order.” Those charts go stale and turn book one into a syllabus.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *A Court of Silver Flames*.
- Starting with *House of Earth and Blood* because a friend said “the crossover is the real series.”
- Treating *Frost and Starlight* as optional filler you read first because it is short.
- Waiting for ACOTAR 6 before opening book one.

## FAQ

**Where to start ACOTAR?**  
*A Court of Thorns and Roses* (2015). Then publication order.

**ACOTAR reading order — which book is first?**  
Book one is *A Court of Thorns and Roses*. Not *Silver Flames*. Not a Crescent City novel. Not *The Assassin’s Blade*.

**Can I start with Silver Flames?**  
No. It is Nesta’s novel. It assumes the first three books and the novella pause.

**Do I need Frost and Starlight?**  
No. It helps. It is not a secret novel. Read it after book three if you have it.

**Do I need Throne of Glass first?**  
No. They do not spoil each other. Short version: [ACOTAR or Throne of Glass first?](/questions/acotar-or-throne-of-glass/).

**Is the series finished?**  
The published run through *Silver Flames* is a pause. More novels are announced. Start anyway.
