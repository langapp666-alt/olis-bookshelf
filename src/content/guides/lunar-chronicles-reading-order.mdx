Four novels plus a prequel novel. Meyer has given a recommended order more than once. Publication of the four is *Cinder* through *Winter*. She inserts *Fairest* after *Cress*. Chronological charts put *Fairest* first. That path hands you Levana before Cinder earns her.

**Contested order, labeled:**

- **Cinder, Scarlet, Cress, Fairest, Winter.** Meyer’s FAQ. This house’s first recommendation.
- **Cinder through Winter, Fairest after.** Allowed if you want the four first and the prequel as aftercare.
- **Fairest first** because it is earlier. Meyer’s blog preferred publication/as-written. This house does not start there.

## Shorts and extras

*Stars Above* is the short-story collection. Meyer’s later FAQ puts it after the novels (and after the *Wires and Nerve* graphic volumes if you want those). Use the collection rather than chasing every early digital short.

*Wires and Nerve* (two graphic volumes) continues Iko. Optional. Not Cinder 2, and not an on-ramp.

*Heartless* is Meyer’s Wonderland book. It is not Lunar Chronicles 6.

**House rule:** *Cinder* through *Winter* with *Fairest* after *Cress*. *Stars Above* if you want the extras in one object.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Fairest* because a chronology chart put it first.
- Starting with *Winter* because it is the Snow White book.
- Treating *Stars Above* as novel 5.
- Mixing in *Heartless* as a prequel.

## FAQ

**What is the Lunar Chronicles reading order?**  
Start with *Cinder* (2012), then *Scarlet*, *Cress*, *Fairest*, *Winter* — Meyer’s recommended order. Do not start with *Fairest* because a chronology chart put it first.

**Do I need Fairest?**  
Meyer puts it in the recommended novel line after *Cress*. You can finish *Winter* without it. You will understand Levana less.

**Stars Above first?**  
No. Collection after the novels.

**Graphic novels required?**  
No.
