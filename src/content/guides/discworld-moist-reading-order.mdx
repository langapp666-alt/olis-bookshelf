Three novels. Publication order. A con man is given institutions and told to make them work. That is the whole street.

This is not Watch #9. Vimes can appear. The job is still Moist’s. For the Watch spine see [City Watch](/guides/discworld-city-watch-reading-order/). For early Discworld tourism see [Rincewind](/guides/discworld-rincewind-reading-order/).

Some maps call these the Industrial Revolution books and quietly add *The Truth* or *Monstrous Regiment*. Those are neighboring Ankh-Morpork novels. They are not Moist 0 and Moist 4.

Read them in this order. *Raising Steam* is later Pratchett and a wider city book. It is still third, not a standalone railway novel you open because you like trains.

## Optional neighbors

- **Watch first, then Moist**, if you want the city after Vimes has already forced it to grow up. Not required.
- **The Truth**, later, if you want the newspaper. Not Moist homework.
- **Full Discworld publication order** if you are collecting the whole Disc. That is a different project.

The BBC *Going Postal* film is a cousin of book one. It is not a reason to skip *Making Money*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Raising Steam* because the railway is in the shop window.
- Treating Moist as the last three Watch novels.
- Stuffing *The Truth* into the numbered column.
- Opening a 41-book chart and postponing the post office.

## FAQ

**Is Moist a good Discworld starting point?**  
Yes. It is one of the later, cleaner doors. The city already exists; the books will teach you the rest.

**Do I need the Watch first?**  
No. Cameos are cheaper if you have read Vimes. They are not homework.

**Is Raising Steam required?**  
It is the third novel. The first two already make a finished pair if you stop. This house still lists all three.

**Whole Discworld?**  
Not on this page.
