Three Bennett shelves that shops file under one author sort. The Divine Cities is a finished three. The Founders Trilogy is a finished three in another world. The Shadow of the Leviathan is a third series, detective-shaped, and not book 4 of either trilogy. Pick the door you meant.

*Mr. Shivers*, *The Company Man*, *The Troupe*, *American Elsewhere*, and *Vigilance* are standalones. They are not book zero of any of these.

The publisher lists *A Trade of Blood* as the third book of the series that began with *The Tainted Cup*. Read them in this order if this is the shelf you wanted. Do not open it to finish Founders.

**House rule:** name the series, then start at its book one. Divine Cities → *City of Stairs*. Founders → *Foundryside*. Leviathan → *The Tainted Cup*. Stop at the end of the trilogy you actually wanted.

## Common mistakes

- Reading *Foundryside* as Divine Cities book 4.
- Reading *The Tainted Cup* as Founders book 4 because it is the newest Bennett hardcover.
- Starting at *Locklands* or *A Trade of Blood* because the earlier books look older.
- Treating *Mr. Shivers* as a shared-universe prologue.

## FAQ

**Do I need the Divine Cities before Founders?**  
No. Different worlds. Either first.

**Is The Tainted Cup part of Founders?**  
No. It opens The Shadow of the Leviathan.

**Are the Divine Cities finished?**  
Yes. Three novels, ending at *City of Miracles*.

**Are Founders finished?**  
Yes. Three novels, ending at *Locklands*.

**Where do the early Bennett novels go?**  
Nowhere on these lists. They are standalones.
