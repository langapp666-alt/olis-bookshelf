Nine main novels. Publication order is the first-read path. The Solaria shop window also holds novellas, a boys’-POV companion, and neighboring series. Those are extras and other streets. They are not a new book one.

Fan “complete universe” charts interleave *Ruthless Boys* and *Darkmore Penitentiary* through the middle of Zodiac Academy. That is a completionist project. It is not the on-ramp.

Read them in this order. *The Reckoning* is book three of this series. It is not Lightbringer, and it is not a standalone.

A later festive book, *On the Cursed Day of Christmas*, has been announced after *Restless Stars*. It is not an on-ramp. Do not invent it as required homework before book one.

## Neighboring Solaria series

*Ruthless Boys of the Zodiac* (beginning *Dark Fae*) and *Darkmore Penitentiary* share the setting. They are designed so you *can* visit them. They are not Zodiac Academy 2.5, and they are a poor place to meet the Vegas for the first time.

**House rule:** read the nine. If you want more Solaria, pick a neighboring series after you already like the school. Do not open a universe spreadsheet and postpone *The Awakening*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Dark Fae* because the cover looks like book one of the franchise.
- Starting with *Origins of an Academy Bully* because it is “0.5.”
- Starting with *Sorrow and Starlight* because it is the thick one people argue about.
- Treating *The Awakening as Told by the Boys* as a replacement for book one.

## FAQ

**Do I need Ruthless Boys first?**  
No. Start at *The Awakening* (2019).

**Origins first because it is a prequel?**  
Not for a first read.

**Is the series finished?**  
Nine main novels are published. Later Solaria books may exist. They will not be *The Awakening*.

**Which cover says Zodiac Academy versus The Awakening?**  
Same book one. Search either, plus the authors’ names.
