Short version of the fork: [Touch or Game first?](/questions/hades-persephone-touch-or-game/). This page is the numbered path plus the tandem note.

Two overlapping spines used to be sold as “Hades x Persephone” (Persephone) and “Hades Saga” (Hades). The author’s current numbering puts them in one column. That is the useful object: Persephone’s book, then Hades’s book for the same stretch, then the next Persephone, and so on.

You can read all four Touch novels first and the Game books later. You will repeat events. You will not be lost. Starting with *A Game of Fate* is how people meet Hades’s interior before the series has introduced the bargain.

**House rule:** *A Touch of Darkness* first. Then either follow the numbered seven above, or finish the Touch novels and add the Game books after the matching Touch volume. Do not start with Fate.

St. Clair has said the books can be read in more than one order, and has been emphatic that *A Game of Gods* should come before *A Touch of Chaos*. That is the one sequence this house will not reverse.

## Tandem — reread, not homework

The author site hosts a chapter-by-chapter tandem guide for the first six books, aimed at hardcore readers and recommended on a second pass. That is the right label. Tandem means you already know both voices and want them in the same sitting. It is not how a first-time reader should meet New Greece.

If you tandem anyway: Touch chapter, then the matching Fate chapters, as the PDF lays out. Do not invent a braid from forum posts.

## Neighboring shelves

*When Stars Come Out* is the same author’s other myth door (Asterion / Ariadne). Optional later. Not Hades 8.

*King of Battle and Blood* is a different St. Clair vampire shelf. Leave it off this spine.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *A Game of Fate* because it is Hades’s book, or because a set numbered it 1.
- Skipping *A Game of Gods* and going straight to *A Touch of Chaos*.
- Treating the tandem PDF as required first-read apparatus.
- Mixing *King of Battle and Blood* into the New Greece column.

## FAQ

**Touch or Game first?**  
*A Touch of Darkness*. *A Game of Fate* is book 2 on the author’s numbering.

**Do I have to tandem-read?**  
No. The tandem guide is a second-read option.

**Do I need A Game of Gods before A Touch of Chaos?**  
Yes. That is the sequence St. Clair flags.

**Are the Game books skippable?**  
You can follow Persephone without them. You will miss Hades’s interior. They are not an alternate book one.
