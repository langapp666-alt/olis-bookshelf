**Contested order, labeled:** Hobb has said Liveship can be read without Farseer. That is true as a ship trilogy. Tawny Man and Fitz and the Fool assume Farseer. Rain Wilds assume Liveship. **House path:** Farseer → Liveship → Tawny Man → Rain Wilds → Fitz and the Fool.

You can skip Rain Wilds and still follow Fitz. You will understand less of the later dragon weather.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Fool’s Assassin* because the title rhymes with book one.
- Reading Rain Wilds first because you like dragons.
- Stopping after Farseer and then opening Fitz and the Fool years later without Tawny Man.
- Mixing in *Soldier Son*. Different Hobb shelf.

## FAQ

**Can I start with Liveship?**  
Yes, as a ship trilogy. Then go back to Farseer before Tawny Man. Start at *Assassin’s Apprentice* (1995).

**Do I need Rain Wilds?**  
Not for Fitz’s earlier books. Yes if you want the later dragon half.

**Is it finished?**  
Fitz’s published run ends in *Assassin’s Fate*.
