Read them in this order. BookTok lists that begin at *The Atlas Paradox* are how people meet five names and none of the rules.

If you already own the 2020 text, you do not need to buy the Tor edition to continue. If you are buying book one now, the revised edition is the one in print.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Atlas Complex* because it is the finale on a table.
- Starting with *The Atlas Paradox* because the revised *Six* was not in the shop that week.
- Treating *Sacred Hospitality* as required homework.
- Mixing in Blake standalones as secret Society prequels.

## FAQ

**Is the trilogy complete?**  
Yes.

**2020 or 2022 for book one?**  
Same novel: *The Atlas Six*. Self-published 2020; Tor’s revised edition is 2022. Either text is book one.

**Do I need Sacred Hospitality?**  
No.
