Do not start with *Changes*. Do not start with *Peace Talks*. Do not start with a graphic novel adaptation of book one. The comics are a different script of a story you should meet as a novel.

Butcher’s author page still listed the novels through *Battle Ground* when last opened and had not yet added *Twelve Months*. Penguin’s listing is the current book 18: hardcover and ebook 20 January 2026.

*Peace Talks* and *Battle Ground* were carved from one longer project. Read them in that order, back to back if you can. They are not interchangeable “late Dresden.”

The series is not finished. Butcher has described a long run of case books plus a later apocalyptic trilogy. *Mirror Mirror* is an announced later title, not a substitute for book 18, and not a starting point.

Keep these out of the numbered novel column.

A first read can be novels only. If you want the shorts, use the two collections rather than chasing every anthology appearance. Lists titled “Side Jobs reading order” go stale; the collections are the stable objects.

Graphic novels and Dynamite comics adapt or sidestep early cases. They are not a shortcut around *Storm Front*, and they are a poor place to meet the later political books.

## Common mistakes

- Starting at *Dead Beat* because “the series gets good there.” You will understand the plot. You will not understand why the plot is expensive.
- Reading *Peace Talks* without immediately reading *Battle Ground*.
- Treating *Side Jobs* as book 12.5 required homework.
- Using the TV series as a table of contents. It is a cousin from an earlier stretch of the books.
- Waiting for the author’s site to list *Twelve Months* before counting it as book 18.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read the shorts?**  
No. The novels carry the argument. The collections are extras.

**Can I skip the first two books?**  
You can. You will spend the next several novels standing on a porch you never walked up.

**Are Peace Talks and Battle Ground one story?**  
Close enough that you should treat them as a pair. Publication order, no gap if you can help it.

**Is Twelve Months a starting point?**  
No. It follows *Battle Ground*.

**Where do the graphic novels go?**  
Optional reread of early cases. Not an on-ramp.
