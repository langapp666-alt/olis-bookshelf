Two finished trilogies. Publication order across the universe is the path that keeps the second three from stepping on the first ending.

McClellan’s site presents Powder Mage and Gods of Blood and Powder as neighboring trilogies, plus short fiction. The shorts jump around in time. They are not a third trilogy, and they are not book one.

**Contested order, labeled:** *Sins of Empire* was built so you *can* enter there. Retail copy will tell you so. This house still reads the first trilogy first if you want the whole shelf. The second trilogy talks around a war you have not watched yet if you start at *Sins*.

**House rule:** *Promise of Blood* through *The Autumn Republic*, then the Landfall three if you want more of the universe.

*The Mad Lancers* is a novella that sits near the second trilogy. Optional. Not Sins of Empire 0.

## Short fiction — optional, not an on-ramp

McClellan has said the shorts are mostly self-contained and were not written in chronological order. He suggests reading *Forsworn* and *Servant of the Crown* as a pair, in that order.

**House rule:** novels first. If you want the shorts, use his short-fiction page rather than a fan “0.1–0.9” column. Do not start the universe on *Forsworn* because it is earlier.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Sins of Empire* because it says “Book One” and looks like a new series.
- Starting with *The Autumn Republic* because someone said the first book is “just the coup.”
- Treating the novellas as a prequel trilogy you must finish before *Promise of Blood*.
- Mixing in *Glass Immortals* as Powder Mage 7.

## FAQ

**Can I start at Sins of Empire?**  
McClellan allows it as a universe jump. For a first Powder Mage visit, start with *Promise of Blood*.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No.

**Is the first trilogy complete?**  
Yes.

**Is Glass Immortals the same world?**  
No. Different McClellan shelf.
