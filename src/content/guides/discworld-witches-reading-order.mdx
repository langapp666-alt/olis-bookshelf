Official Discworld reading-order pages treat the Witches as their own line. Publication order inside the line is the first read.

**Contested start, labeled:** *Equal Rites* is first and slightly aside — it is where the line begins, and it is not yet the full coven comedy some readers wanted. *Wyrd Sisters* is the other honest door. This house still lists *Equal Rites* first. If you bounce off it, *Wyrd Sisters* is the retry, not *A Hat Full of Sky*.

## Tiffany Aching — a later path

Tiffany is witches from another angle: younger, later, a different county.

**House rule:** read the six coven novels, then Tiffany, if you came for Granny and Nanny. If you came for a shorter, younger Discworld, *The Wee Free Men* is a real door — it is still not *Mort* and not *Guards! Guards!*.

*The Sea and Little Fishes* is a short. Optional. Not a novel.

## Common mistakes

- Opening a 41-book Discworld list and postponing *Equal Rites*.
- Starting with *The Shepherd’s Crown*.
- Mixing Witches with Watch in publication order across the whole Disc and calling it a witches path.
- Treating *Equal Rites* as skippable filler because later books are funnier.

## FAQ

**Equal Rites or Wyrd Sisters first?**  
*Equal Rites* is first. *Wyrd Sisters* is the other door.

**Do I need the Watch books?**  
No. They share a world, not a homework list.

**Is Tiffany required?**  
No. It is a later, complete argument.

**Whole Discworld?**  
Not on this page.
