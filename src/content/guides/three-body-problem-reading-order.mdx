## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Death’s End* because it is the “big” one.
- Treating *Redemption of Time* as Liu’s fourth novel and an on-ramp.
- Using the show as a table of contents.
- Waiting for another adaptation before opening book one.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read all three?**  
If you want the designed ending, yes.

**Is Redemption of Time required?**  
No.

**Netflix first?**  
The show is a cousin. The novels still start at *The Three-Body Problem*.
