## Starting point

Start with **Gideon the Ninth**. Muir’s own series page says the sequence begins there, continues with *Harrow the Ninth* and *Nona the Ninth*, and concludes with *Alecto the Ninth* (forthcoming).

Do not start with *Nona*. Do not start with the Sixth House short. Do not start with *Harrow* because someone told you it is “the real book.” *Harrow* spends capital you only have if you have already been in Canaan House.

This is a four-book argument. Three novels are out. The fourth is not. Short fiction fills gaps; it does not replace a novel.

## The novels

Publication order is the only honest first read. Chronology is not a separate on-ramp here; the later books are doing things *to* the earlier ones.

### Nona is not optional

*Nona the Ninth* looks, from the outside, like a detour: new name, new daily life, a city that is not the Mithraeum. It is still book three of four. Skipping it to “get to Alecto” is skipping the book that has to happen before the last one can.

If you bounced off *Harrow*, that is a taste problem, not a license to jump. There is no gentler middle volume.

## Short fiction

Muir’s site lists three shorts. None belong in the numbered novel column.

| Title | Where it lived | Story placement | First-read? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Mysterious Study of Doctor Sex | Reactor / Tor.com, 2020 | Years before *Gideon*; Palamedes and Camilla | Optional. After *Gideon* is plenty. |
| As Yet Unsent | *Harrow* paperback / ebook; also Tor.com | Between *Harrow* and *Nona* | Optional. Read after *Harrow* if you have the later printing. |
| The Unwanted Guest | *Nona* paperback | During *Nona*; one-act play | Optional. After *Nona*. |

Some later *Harrow* printings also include a short “Blood of Eden Memorandum for Record.” Treat it as extra material in that edition, not as a missing novel.

**House rule:** if your hardcover of *Harrow* or *Nona* lacks the extra, you have not failed the series. Check the paperback or the Reactor copies later.

Do not read the Sixth House prequel first. It is a locked-room school story that assumes you already care who those two are going to become.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Harrow* or *Nona* because the titles sound sequential and you picked the one in stock.
- Treating *Nona* as a novella or a palate-cleanser you can skip.
- Reading *The Mysterious Study of Doctor Sex* as book 0.5 homework.
- Waiting for *Alecto* before starting. The first three novels are already a sequence.
- Using a “complete” list that quietly includes every extra memorandum as a numbered book.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read the shorts?**  
No. *As Yet Unsent* is the one most worth slotting after *Harrow* if you have it. The others are texture.

**Can I skip Harrow?**  
You can stop. You cannot skip it and still be reading this series.

**Is Nona a side book?**  
No. Muir lists it as the continuation.

**When is Alecto the Ninth?**  
Forthcoming, with no date on the author’s series page when last opened. Do not invent one.

**Which printing do I need?**  
Any *Gideon* is fine. For the shorts, prefer later paperbacks of *Harrow* and *Nona*, or the Reactor texts.
