  It is book one of The Faithful and the Fallen (2012). Then *Valour*, *Ruin*, *Wrath*. Of Blood and Bone is the same Banished Lands, later generation. *The Shadow of the Gods* is Bloodsworn — a different Gwynne world. It is not Banished Lands 8.
</StartHere>

Two complete series share the Banished Lands. A third Gwynne trilogy does not. Retail “complete Gwynne” lists flatten all three into one numbered column. That is the trap.

Gwynne has said you do not *need* Faithful and the Fallen before Of Blood and Bone: the later trilogy carries its own history. He has also said the first series gives that history weight. **House rule:** *Malice* first if you want the Banished Lands. Do not start on *A Time of Dread* because the covers are newer. Do not start on Bloodsworn because it is the book people are currently holding.

Norse-myth epic. No Corban, no Banished Lands map. You can read Bloodsworn without any Banished Lands book. You can read Banished Lands without any Bloodsworn book. They are neighboring Gwynne shelves, not a prequel and sequel.

If Bloodsworn is the series you actually wanted, start with *The Shadow of the Gods*. Do not “prepare” for it with *Malice*.

A further Gwynne world (*Wolfheart*) is a later, separate door. Same rule: not Banished Lands 11.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *A Time of Dread* because it is “book one” of a trilogy.
- Starting with *The Shadow of the Gods* because it is the current Gwynne, then wondering where the Banished Lands went.
- Starting with *Wrath* or *A Time of Courage*.
- Filing all ten Gwynne novels as one series in publication order and calling that the Banished Lands.

## FAQ

**Do I need Faithful and the Fallen before Of Blood and Bone?**  
Gwynne says no, strictly. This house still reads *Malice* first for a Banished Lands visit.

**Is Bloodsworn Banished Lands?**  
No. Separate world. Start it only if that is the series you want.

**Where do I start John Gwynne?**  
*Malice* for the Banished Lands. *The Shadow of the Gods* for Bloodsworn. Pick one. They are not a sequence.

**Is the Banished Lands finished?**  
Both Banished Lands series are complete. Bloodsworn is a different complete three.
