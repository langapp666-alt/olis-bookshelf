Broken Earth is a finished trilogy. Publication order is the only path. There is no prequel novel hiding in front of *The Fifth Season*.

Each book continues the same argument. Lists that call any of them standalone are doing cover-copy work, not reading-order work.

## Shorts and extras

Jemisin has published related short fiction around this world. Those pieces are optional and are not a fourth novel. Do not postpone book one until you have hunted every magazine appearance.

There is no contested chronology that moves *The Stone Sky* first. If a chart tries, ignore it.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with book three because of awards lists.
- Waiting for a prequel that is not the product.
- Treating the trilogy as three disconnected “seasons.”
- Opening a spoiler-heavy explainer before chapter one. The first book’s structure is the point.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
Yes. Start at *The Fifth Season* (2015).

**Is the trilogy complete?**  
Yes.

**Are there prequels?**  
Not as a first-read novel. Start with *The Fifth Season*.

**Which edition?**  
Any complete printing of book one.
