Fleming published one Bond book a year from *Casino Royale* to his death. The official James Bond Library lists those fourteen titles in the order they appeared. That is the first-read path.

The movies are a cousin franchise. They skip, remix, and rename. They are not a table of contents for the novels.

## Fleming’s Bond, in publication order

Twelve novels and two short-story collections. The collections sit where they were published, not in a later “shorts appendix.”

Publication order is the Bond order. *On Her Majesty’s Secret Service*, *You Only Live Twice*, and *The Man with the Golden Gun* spend what the earlier books earned. Starting on a later “best film” title will not tell you why those three are expensive.

*The Spy Who Loved Me* is still a Fleming Bond book. It is not an optional extra because the film reused the title and almost nothing else.

## Films are not a reading order

Eon productions begin with *Dr. No* and then wander. Connery, Moore, Craig, and the rest are different arguments about the same name. Use a film list if you want the films. Do not use it to decide which novel is book one.

## Continuation novels — later, and contested

After Fleming, other writers were hired to keep the name in print. They are **not** Fleming 15. The official library treats them as a later shelf.

John Gardner, Raymond Benson, Sebastian Faulks, Jeffery Deaver, William Boyd, and others also wrote Bonds. If you want that later shelf, finish Fleming first, then pick one authorized continuation and treat it as itself. This page will not invent a 40-book “complete 007” that exists only to host links.

Young Bond and the spin-off comics are other doors. They are not on-ramps to *Casino Royale*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Goldfinger* or *Casino Royale* the film and calling that the novel order.
- Starting with *Colonel Sun* or a Horowitz Bond because the spine says James Bond.
- Skipping *For Your Eyes Only* and then treating the later shorts as missing novels.
- Waiting to “watch them in order” before opening book one.

## FAQ

**What is the James Bond reading order?**  
Start with *Casino Royale* (1953), then Fleming’s twelve novels and two collections in publication order. Films and continuation novels stay off book one.

**Do I need the short-story collections?**  
For a first Fleming pass, read them where they sit. They are short. They are not secret novels.

**Is the Craig *Casino Royale* a substitute for book one?**  
No. It is an adaptation of the first novel’s job, not a license to skip the book.

**Which continuation first if I insist?**  
*Colonel Sun* is the first after Fleming. Horowitz is a later authorized voice. Neither is book one.

**Publication or the films’ “Bond chronology”?**  
Publication. The films do not agree with each other.
