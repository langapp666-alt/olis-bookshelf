Valdemar is many trilogies across a long in-world history. Lackey wrote them as separate three-book jobs. The vocabulary — Heralds, Companions, Gifts — is taught in *Arrows of the Queen*. Later books assume it. Chronology starts with the Mage Wars, which Lackey wrote seventh, for people who already live in this country.

This page is a starter path. It is not the 40-title spreadsheet.

This is the trilogy people mean when they say Lackey’s best Valdemar. It is darker than Talia’s books. Internal chronology puts it centuries before Arrows. **House rule:** after you already know what a Herald-Mage is supposed to be. Starting here is allowed in the sense that the three stand as a tragedy. It is a rough first impression of a world whose actual on-ramp is kinder.

## What the chronological list is doing

Modern editions often print a timeline that begins with the Mage Wars (*The Black Gryphon* and sequels), then Founding, Collegium Chronicles (*Foundation* and sequels), *Brightly Burning*, Last Herald-Mage, and only then Talia.

That list is a map of history. It is not a reading order. Those earlier-set books were written for readers who already have the Companions in their bones.

**House rule:** do not start with *The Black Gryphon*, *Foundation*, *Brightly Burning*, or *Magic’s Pawn* because a chart numbered them 1. After the Arrows trilogy (and after Mage Winds/Storms if you stayed), pick one earlier trilogy as a reread-in-the-past. You do not owe the spreadsheet.

The Darian / Owlbooks, Herald Spy, Family Spies, and later Collegium books are further rooms. Same rule: after you like the house, not as preparation for *Arrows of the Queen*.

## Common mistakes

- Opening the chronological contents page and starting a thousand years before Talia.
- Starting with *Magic’s Pawn* because it is the famous one, or because a TV announcement named Vanyel.
- Starting with *Foundation* because it says Collegium and looks like school-year book one.
- Treating *By the Sword* as Valdemar 0.

## FAQ

**Where do I start Valdemar?**  
*Arrows of the Queen* (1987). Not the chronological list.

**Can I start with Last Herald-Mage?**  
You can. This house does not, for a first visit. It is darker, earlier, and assumes the Herald vocabulary.

**Do I need the Mage Wars first?**  
No. They are set earliest and written later. After you already like Valdemar.

**Is there one official order?**  
Lackey wrote trilogies. Publication of the Arrows three is the door. Chronology is a table of contents, not an on-ramp.
