Four novels make the Cycle. They are a finished argument. Everything after them is a return to the same country, not a new book one.

Paolini’s own pages are blunt: *Murtagh* is a direct sequel to the original four and to *The Fork, the Witch, and the Worm*. It is set about a year after *Inheritance*. That is a later door. It is not Eragon 0.

**House rule:**

1. *Eragon* through *Inheritance*
2. *The Fork, the Witch, and the Worm* if you want more of the same country
3. *Murtagh* if you want the later novel

Publisher copy has called *Murtagh* a possible first visit. This house does not. The book spends the Cycle’s ending. Read the four first.

*The Book of Remembrance* is a later Kickstarter collection of World of Eragon stories. Optional extra. Not Inheritance 5, and not a substitute for *Eragon*.

*To Sleep in a Sea of Stars* is Fractalverse science fiction. It is not a secret dragon prequel.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Murtagh* because a jacket called it an entry.
- Starting with *Inheritance* because it is the title of the Cycle.
- Treating the 2006 film as a table of contents.
- Mixing in the coloring book, almanac, or illustrated edition as a fifth novel.

## FAQ

**Do I need Murtagh?**  
No. The four end.

**Fork before Murtagh?**  
That is the author’s advertised sequence. Neither is required.

**Is Eragon Cosmere?**  
No.

**Movie first?**  
No. The film is a cousin of book one, and a poor map of the rest.
