Five novels. Cass’s 2015 series note listed them in publication order: America’s three, then Eadlyn’s two. That is still the first-read path.

Novellas collected in *Happily Ever After* are extras. Chronology charts put *The Queen* and *The Prince* before book one. That path hands you the palace before America walks in.

**Contested order, labeled:**

- **The five novels, then *Happily Ever After*.** This house’s first recommendation.
- **Novellas after the book they sit beside** (*The Prince* after *The Selection*, *The Guard* after *The Elite*). Allowed if you already have the pieces. Still not a reason to start on Maxon.
- **The Queen or The Prince first** because they are earlier. Cass published them as companions. This house does not start there.

*The Siren* is a different Cass book.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Heir* because Eadlyn’s covers look newer.
- Starting with *The Prince* because it is “0.5.”
- Treating *Happily Ever After* as a sixth novel you need before *The Crown*.
- Expecting a sixth prose novel. Cass closed Illéa in *The Crown*.

## FAQ

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No.

**Can I start at The Heir?**  
You can. You will spend two books standing on a contest you never watched. This house does not.

**Is Happily Ever After a novel?**  
No. It is a companion collection.
