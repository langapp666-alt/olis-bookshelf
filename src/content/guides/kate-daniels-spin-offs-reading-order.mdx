The Kate Daniels novels are Atlanta, Kate’s point of view, *Magic Bites* through *Magic Triumphs*. Andrea, Hugh, and Julie each have a book that looks like a fresh series if you meet it in a shop. The authors’ release schedule puts those books in the middle or after the end. This page is that placement. It does not reprint all ten Kate novels.

Hidden Legacy, Innkeeper, and The Edge are different Andrews shelves.

**Read the years with the notes, not as a sort.** *Blood Heir* was published in 2021. The authors’ chronological schedule still places it after *Magic Tides* and *Magic Claims* (both 2023). Publication order and their story order disagree on that one book. **House rule:** follow the author schedule for Julie. Do not read *Blood Heir* between *Magic Triumphs* and *Magic Tides* just because the copyright page is earlier. And do not start on it.

*Gunmetal Magic* and *Iron and Magic* match publication and the schedule. *Gunmetal Magic* is not Kate 5.5 you must own, and it is a poor place to meet the world. *Iron and Magic* is Hugh after you already know him from the Kate novels. The schedule marks it as his book one. There was no second Hugh novel on that list when this page was checked.

## Novellas that look like extra book ones

*Magic Mourns* is an Andrea novella. The schedule puts it after *Magic Strikes* and before *Magic Bleeds*. It is not a start.

*Magic Gifts* is a Kate novella. In print it was packaged with *Gunmetal Magic*. Buying *Gunmetal Magic* does not make Andrea’s novel into Kate book one, and it does not replace *Magic Bites*.

Other shorts (*Magic Dreams*, *Magic Steals*, *Magic Stars*, *Sanctuary*, and the pieces in *Small Magics*) interleave on the author list. A first read can ignore them. Insert one when the schedule puts it after a book you have already read.

*The King of Fire* is a Julie short that also appears with *Blood Heir*. It is not a prequel door.

## What “after Magic Slays” means

The [Kate Daniels guide](/guides/kate-daniels-reading-order/) has the ten Atlanta novels and the Wilmington pair in Kate’s order. Use it for the spine. Use this page when the book in your hand is not narrated by Kate.

**House rule:** *Magic Bites* first. *Gunmetal Magic* only after *Magic Slays*. *Iron and Magic* only after *Magic Binds*. *Blood Heir* only after *Magic Claims*. None of those three is required to finish Kate’s ten.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Gunmetal Magic* because Andrea is on the cover and the number 1 belongs to her book, not to the world.
- Starting with *Blood Heir* because Julie’s hardcover looks like a reboot.
- Starting with *Iron and Magic* because Hugh’s book says book one.
- Reading *Blood Heir* in 2021 publication order, ahead of Wilmington, against the author schedule.
- Treating Hidden Legacy (*Burn for Me*) as Kate book 11.

## FAQ

**Can I start with Gunmetal Magic?**  
No. Andrea’s novel assumes *Magic Slays* already happened. Start at *Magic Bites*.

**Where does Blood Heir go?**  
After *Magic Triumphs*, *Magic Tides*, and *Magic Claims*, on the author schedule. Not first. Not between *Magic Triumphs* and *Magic Tides*.

**Where does Iron and Magic go?**  
After *Magic Binds* and before *Magic Triumphs*. Hugh’s book one is not the world’s book one.

**Do I need the spin-offs to finish Kate’s series?**  
No. *Magic Triumphs* ends the Atlanta run without them.

**Is Hidden Legacy the same world?**  
No.
