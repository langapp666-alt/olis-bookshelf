One war, three couple pairs. Publication order is the only path that keeps the later pairs from spending the earlier endings. Jensen’s site and the Del Rey series page list them in one line.

**House rule:** read 1–2, then 3–4, then 5–6. Each pair looks like a new series on a shelf. It is not.

Del Rey reprints added material in later editions. Same novels. *A Fate Inked in Blood* is a different Jensen shelf.

Penguin listed *The Tempest Blade* for 14 April 2026 when this page was checked. If that date moves, the placement does not: after *The Twisted Throne*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Inadequate Heir* because Keris is the new hardcover.
- Starting with *The Twisted Throne* because Ahnna is the new pair.
- Treating book three as Bridge Kingdom 1 of a spin-off you can read first.
- Mixing in *A Fate Inked in Blood* as book 7.

## FAQ

**Can I start at book three?**  
No. The second pair spends the first war.

**Do I need all six?**  
No. The first two end a couple. The war does not.

**Is Tempest Blade out?**  
Check the publisher series page. Placement is after *The Twisted Throne* either way.
