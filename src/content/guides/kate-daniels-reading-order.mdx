Ten novels make Kate’s Atlanta run. They are a finished argument. *Magic Tides* and *Magic Claims* are a later-city continuation. They spend the tenth book’s ending.

Andrews’s Kate page lists the novels from her point of view and then the Wilmington Years. Their “start here” page is cheerful about skipping the first two. This house still starts at *Magic Bites*. You can take their joke after you have a sample. You cannot take it as book one.

**House rule:** read the ten. If you want the later city, *Magic Tides* then *Magic Claims*. Do not open *Magic Tides* because it looks like a new series.

A later Wilmington book may exist after these two. It will not be Kate 0.

## Spin-offs — neighboring, not on-ramps

*Gunmetal Magic*, *Iron and Magic*, and *Blood Heir* are other points of view in the same world. Andrews’s release list shows where they sit. They are not Kate 5.5 required homework, and they are a poor place to meet Kate for the first time.

Novellas (*Magic Mourns*, *Magic Dreams*, *Magic Stars*, and others) interleave. A first read can be the ten novels. Insert a short if you have it and the authors’ list puts it after a book you already read.

*Hidden Legacy* and *Innkeeper* are different Andrews shelves.

## Common mistakes

- Starting at *Magic Strikes* because the start-here page said the first two are “meh.”
- Starting with *Magic Tides* because it is a new city.
- Starting with *Blood Heir* because Julie is on the new hardcover.
- Treating *Gunmetal Magic* as Kate 6.

## FAQ

**Should I skip the first two because the authors said to?**  
You can. This house does not. *Magic Bites* is still book one.

**Do I need the Wilmington books?**  
No. Atlanta ends at *Magic Triumphs*.

**Do I need the spin-offs?**  
No.

**Is Hidden Legacy the same world?**  
No.
