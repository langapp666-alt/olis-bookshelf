Urban fantasy as a family business. Narrators change by branch: Verity, then Alex, back to Verity, then Antimony, Sarah, Alice, Mary. Shops sometimes sell a later branch as if it were a new series. It is not.

McGuire’s FAQ: publication order is a good idea; shorts enhance but are not required. **House rule:** novels in publication order. Shorts optional after you already like the Prices.

October Daye and Wayward Children are other McGuire shelves. See those guides. They are not InCryptid prequels.

**Branch trap, labeled:** *Half-Off Ragnarok* feels like a soft reset because Alex is a new narrator. It still assumes Verity’s first two. Same for Antimony, Sarah, and Alice: new voice, old consequences.

## Short fiction — optional

Free and anthology shorts fill earlier generations (Healys, Alice, and others). McGuire treats them as optional. Do not start the series on a short because it is chronologically earlier.

*Sparrow Hill Road* shares a universe tone without being InCryptid homework.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Half-Off Ragnarok* or *Magic for Nothing* because a “complete Price family” widget lists a later narrator first.
- Starting with *Spelunking Through Hell* because Alice’s covers are current.
- Treating InCryptid as October Daye 20.
- Treating the shorts as a required prequel column before *Discount Armageddon*.

## FAQ

**Do I need publication order?**  
McGuire says it is a good idea. This house agrees.

**Can I read only Verity?**  
You can stop after her books. You will miss later family consequences.

**Do I need the short stories?**  
No.

**Is Installment Immortality a starting point?**  
No.
