Read them in this order. Book two and three arrived as sequels, not as interchangeable “Russian folklore” standalones.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Winter of the Witch* because it is the last hardcover.
- Treating *The Warm Hands of Ghosts* as a Winternight continuation.
- Mixing in a folklore anthology as book 0.

## FAQ

**Is the trilogy complete?**  
Yes.

**Is there a later Winternight novel?**  
Not on Arden’s trilogy page. *The Warm Hands of Ghosts* is a different book.

**Do I need Russian folklore first?**  
No. The novels teach their own table.
