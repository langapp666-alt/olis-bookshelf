Eleven novels. Publication order is the first-read path. Mac’s first five close an argument. Dani’s book and the later Dublin novels continue the same street.

Moning’s Fever series page lists the novels and *Fever Moon*. The graphic is a sideline. It is not Darkfever 0.

**House rule:** read the eleven. *Fever Moon* if you want the graphic. Highlander time-travel novels are a different Moning shelf, even when a later Fever book nods at them.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Iced* because Dani is on the later covers.
- Starting with *Shadowfever* because that is the thick one people argue about.
- Treating *Fever Moon* as novel 6.
- Mixing a Highlander book into the numbered Fever column.

## FAQ

**Can I stop after Shadowfever?**  
Yes. That closes Mac’s first arc. Start at *Darkfever* (2006).

**Do I need Fever Moon?**  
No.

**Do I need the Highlander books?**  
No.
