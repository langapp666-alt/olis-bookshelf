  Two honest doors. *Shadow and Bone* (2012) starts the Ravka trilogy. *Six of Crows* (2015) starts the Ketterdam duology. Do not start with *King of Scars*. Do not start with the Netflix season order.

The Grishaverse is one setting with three main published runs. Publication order across the universe is the path that keeps later books from stepping on earlier endings. The Crows books were designed so you *can* enter there. *King of Scars* was not.

**Contested order, labeled:**

- **Trilogy first, then Crows, then King of Scars.** Publication order. This house’s first recommendation if you want the whole shelf.
- **Crows first.** Allowed. You will meet Ravka from the outside. Then read the original trilogy before *King of Scars*.
- **Netflix order.** The show mixes plots. It is not a book order and it is a reason people open *Six of Crows* thinking it is *Shadow and Bone* book 4.

*The Language of Thorns* and *The Lives of Saints* are extras. Optional after you like the setting. *Ninth House* is a different Bardugo series.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *King of Scars* because Nikolai is the one you liked on screen.
- Treating *Six of Crows* as Shadow and Bone book 4.
- Using the show as a table of contents.
- Skipping *Crooked Kingdom*.

## FAQ

**Crows or Shadow and Bone first?**  
Either door. *King of Scars* last among these six. Start at *Shadow and Bone* (2012).

**Do I need the extras?**  
No.

**Is the Grishaverse finished?**  
The three runs above are published complete. Later stories may exist; they are not a new book one.
