Short version of the door: [Where to start the Vorkosigan Saga](/questions/where-to-start-vorkosigan/). This page is the full table.

The argument is publication order versus internal chronology. Bujold favors chronology, with a few books held until they pay off. This house follows her: Cordelia first, then Miles, *Falling Free* later.

Each novel was written to stand alone. That is true and insufficient. Later books are richer if you already know who paid for the earlier ones.

**House rule:** *Shards*, *Barrayar*, then *The Warrior’s Apprentice*, and do not skip ahead to *Memory*. *Brothers in Arms* before *Mirror Dance*. *Komarr* before *A Civil Campaign*.

**Contested order, labeled:**

- **Cordelia, then Miles, Falling Free late.** Bujold’s favored path. This house’s first recommendation.
- **Miles first, at The Warrior’s Apprentice.** Allowed. You will meet Cordelia as history. Still read *Shards* and *Barrayar* before you are done.
- **Publication order.** *Shards*, *Warrior’s Apprentice*, *Ethan*, *Falling Free*, then a scramble in which *Barrayar* arrives fifth among the novels. Documented. Not kinder.
- **Falling Free first** because it is earliest. Bujold and most readers tell you not to. It does not share the cast.

## Novellas — out of the numbered novel column

Bujold’s guide is specific about duplication.

- **The Mountains of Mourning**, **Labyrinth**, and **The Borders of Infinity** are the three novellas collected as *Borders of Infinity*. Read that collection after *The Warrior’s Apprentice* and before *Brothers in Arms* if you want the sampler she describes. Do not also buy the three as if they were extra novels.
- **Weatherman** is an out-take from the start of *The Vor Game*. If you have the novel, you likely do not need it.
- **Winterfair Gifts** sits after *A Civil Campaign*.
- **The Flowers of Vashnoi** sits after *Captain Vorpatril’s Alliance*.

A first read can be novels only. The collection is the stable object for the early shorts.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Falling Free* because a chronology chart put it first.
- Starting with *Komarr* because it is “the start of the second career.”
- Reading *Mirror Dance* before *Brothers in Arms*.
- Buying *Borders of Infinity* and the three novellas separately and wondering why the stories repeat.
- Opening a sixteen-book spreadsheet and postponing *Shards of Honor*.

## FAQ

**Shards or Warrior’s Apprentice first?**  
*Shards* if you want Bujold’s spoiler-sensitive door. *Warrior’s Apprentice* if you want Miles immediately. Not *Falling Free*.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No. The novels carry the argument. *Borders of Infinity* is the tidy extra.

**Is Ethan of Athos required?**  
No. It is a side novel. It is richer later.

**Where does Cordelia’s Honor go?**  
It *is* *Shards* plus *Barrayar*. Do not read it and the two novels as four books.
