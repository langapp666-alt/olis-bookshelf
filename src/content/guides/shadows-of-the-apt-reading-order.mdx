A finished ten. Publication order is the reading order. There is no chronological prequel posing as book one. The trap is the rest of the author: later series, a different fantasy trilogy, and mid-list reprints that look current.

See [Children of Time](/guides/children-of-time-reading-order/) and [The Final Architecture](/guides/final-architecture-reading-order/) if those were the Tchaikovsky shelves you meant. Echoes of the Fall (*The Tiger and the Wolf* first) is another fantasy trilogy. It is not Apt book 11.

Read them in this order. The cast spreads. Later books assume the earlier wars already happened.

Apt short-story collections exist. They are optional after you already know the ten. They are not book one and they are not required to finish *Seal of the Worm*.

**House rule:** *Empire in Black and Gold* through *Seal of the Worm*. Stop earlier if you want; do not start later.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Children of Time* because it is the Tchaikovsky book a shop puts forward.
- Starting with *Shards of Earth* for the same reason.
- Starting with *The Tiger and the Wolf* because it is also Tchaikovsky fantasy.
- Jumping to *The Air War* or *Seal of the Worm* because a reprint looks new.
- Treating an Apt short-story collection as book 11 you must read before the ending.

## FAQ

**Is this the same series as Children of Time?**  
No.

**Is this the same series as Shards of Earth?**  
No. That trilogy is The Final Architecture.

**Can I start at book 5?**  
Not for a first read. The ten are one argument.

**Are the shorts required?**  
No.

**Is the series finished?**  
The ten novels are finished. Short stories sit beside them. They do not open a new book one.
