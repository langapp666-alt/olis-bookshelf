Four novels make Mare’s run. They are a finished argument. The novellas and *Broken Throne* are extras that look like a fifth book on a shelf.

Aveyard’s series page presents a complete epic of four volumes. *Broken Throne* is the companion collection: the two earlier novellas plus later pieces after *War Storm*.

**Contested order, labeled:** chronological charts put *Queen Song* and *Steel Scars* before *Red Queen*. Those two are origin texture. They assume you already care who Cal and Farley are. Publication puts them after book one (*Cruel Crown* arrived as a companion to *Glass Sword*).

**House rule:**

1. *Red Queen* through *War Storm*
2. *Broken Throne* if you want the extras in one object

If you already have *Cruel Crown*, you do not need to buy those two novellas twice. *Broken Throne* still holds later pieces *Cruel Crown* does not.

Aveyard’s later *Realm Breaker* books are a different series.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Queen Song* because it is “earlier.”
- Treating *Broken Throne* as novel five.
- Buying *Cruel Crown* and *Broken Throne* and reading the same two novellas twice without noticing.
- Starting with *War Storm* because it is the war book.

## FAQ

**Do I need Broken Throne?**  
No. The four end.

**Cruel Crown or Broken Throne?**  
*Broken Throne* is the fuller collection. *Cruel Crown* is only the first two novellas.

**Should I read Queen Song first?**  
Not for a first read.

**Is Realm Breaker Red Queen 5?**  
No.
