Five novels published in the main sequence. Later volumes have been discussed for years. This page will not invent a date for *The Winds of Winter*. The HBO / MAX show is a cousin that outran the books; show order is not a reason to skip to *A Dance with Dragons*.

Publication order is the only first-read path. *Feast* and *Dance* overlap in time and split cast. Read *Feast* then *Dance*. Combined-order fan charts exist for a reread. They are not required, and they are easy to spoil yourself with.

**Contested order, labeled:** chronological charts put Dunk and Egg and *Fire & Blood* first. That is a historian’s reread. For a first read it hands you names the novels are designed to introduce as the point.

*The Rise of the Dragon* is an illustrated companion, not a novel. *The World of Ice & Fire* is a reference book. Neither is book 0.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Fire & Blood* because of *House of the Dragon*.
- Starting with *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* because the adaptation is newer.
- Reading *Dance* before *Feast*.
- Waiting for *Winds* before opening *A Game of Thrones*.

## FAQ

**Show first?**  
The show diverges and finishes. The novels still start at *A Game of Thrones*.

**Feast or Dance first?**  
*Feast*, then *Dance*.

**Is Fire & Blood required?**  
No.

**Winds of Winter?**  
Not a book you can put in this table yet.
