Ten novels from 1984 to 2000, then a long silence, then extras and a new arc. Cook’s Company books were met as a chronicle that moves forward. Later volumes that fill earlier gaps assume you already know who survived.

Retail and some wikis will show *Port of Shadows* as book 1.5. That is chronology. It is not a first-read mercy.

Read them in this order. *The Silver Spike* follows leftover names from the first three while Croaker’s column goes south. It is not optional preamble to *Shadow Games*, and it is not a door.

Some readers skip *The Silver Spike* and go south immediately. Allowed. Do not skip *Shadows Linger*.

## Port of Shadows and On the Long Run — labeled

*Port of Shadows* folds in earlier shorts (“Tides Elba,” “Smelling Danger,” “Bone Candy”). You do not need those separately.

*On the Long Run* is a later arc of short stories (*Chasing Midnight*, *Cranky Bitch*, and others) set in the gap after *Shadows Linger*. They revisit Company texture the 2018 novel already spent. **House rule:** novels through *Soldiers Live* first. *Port of Shadows* next if you want the lost-history extra. The Long Run shorts after that, or not at all.

## Lies Weeping — new arc, after the original ten

<OrderTable
  author="Glen Cook"

This is the start of a later Company arc, not a reboot and not a prequel. Read it after *Soldiers Live*. *Port of Shadows* can sit before it as extra, or you can go straight from the original ending into the new books. Do not start the Company here because the cover is recent.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Port of Shadows* because a timeline put it between books 1 and 2.
- Reading the Long Run shorts as a hidden prequel trilogy.
- Starting with *The Silver Spike* or *Bleak Seasons* because a later omnibus labeled a new “book of.”
- Mixing in Cook’s *Dread Empire* as Black Company 0.

## FAQ

**Port of Shadows after book one?**  
Not on a first read. After *Soldiers Live*, even though it sits earlier in Company time.

**Do I need On the Long Run?**  
No. Shorts. After *Port of Shadows* if you want them.

**Is The Silver Spike required?**  
It is fourth in publication. You can skip it and still follow Croaker south. It is not book one of the South.

**Is Lies Weeping a new series?**  
It is a new Company arc after the original ten. Not a door.
