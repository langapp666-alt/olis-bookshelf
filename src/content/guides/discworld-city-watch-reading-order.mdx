You do not need the Witches or Death books first. Those are other streets. The Watch books assume a city that already exists; they do not assume you have read every prior Discworld novel.

If someone told you “Discworld can be read in any order,” they are half right about the *series* and wrong about *this* street. The Watch novels are a character ladder. Vimes, Carrot, Angua, and the institution of the Watch change on purpose.

These eight are the spine. Cameos in other Discworld novels (*The Truth*, *Monstrous Regiment*, *Raising Steam*, and others) are optional later rereads, not Watch #9.

## Theatre of Cruelty is optional

**Theatre of Cruelty** is a short Watch story, not a ninth novel.

- In story terms it sits after *Guards! Guards!* and before *Men at Arms*.
- It has lived in authorized collections such as *A Blink of the Screen*, not on the standard novel spine.
- Availability is uneven. Some audiobook groupings invent a “1.5.” That is a catalog convenience, not a Pratchett novel number.
- You can finish all eight novels without it and still have read the City Watch series.

**House rule:** mention it, do not number it, and do not send people to unofficial copies.

*Where’s My Cow?* is a picture book inside the Watch’s world. It is not Watch #6.5.

## Optional paths

- **Watch only.** *Guards! Guards!* through *Snuff*. This is the guide.
- **Watch, then other Ankh-Morpork industrial novels** (*Going Postal*, *Making Money*, *Raising Steam*) if you want the city after Vimes has already forced it to grow up.
- **[Witches](/guides/discworld-witches-reading-order/)** or **[Death](/guides/discworld-death-reading-order/)** if you want the other short Discworld streets. Tiffany sits on the Witches page.
- **Full Discworld publication order** if you are collecting the whole Disc. That is a different project. Use the publisher’s Discworld list for the 41-book shelf, not this page.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Colour of Magic* because it is “book one.” You can. It is a different tone and a different cast.
- Reading *Night Watch* first because people call it the best. It is a later book.
- Stuffing every Vimes cameo into the numbered table.
- Treating *Theatre of Cruelty* as required homework.
- Using a fan “complete Watch” list that quietly includes the picture book.

## What to read next

1. **[Rivers of London](/guides/rivers-of-london-reading-order/) (Ben Aaronovitch)** — If you want a working police force in a city that is older and stranger than its paperwork. Magic as an extra jurisdiction, not a chosen-one diploma.
2. **The Goblin Emperor (Katherine Addison)** — If the pleasure was watching an honest person learn an institution from the inside, without needing another dragon.
3. **[The Craft Sequence](/guides/craft-sequence-reading-order/) (Max Gladstone)** — If Ankh-Morpork’s legal and economic improvising was the hook: gods, contracts, and cities as machines.
4. **[The First Law](/guides/first-law-reading-order/) (Joe Abercrombie)** — If you want another long fantasy street that is really about people trapped inside an institution, with less civic optimism.
5. **[Jack Reacher](/guides/jack-reacher-reading-order/) (Lee Child)** — If you want the copper’s stare without the dragons: cases that look standalone until they are not.

## FAQ

**Is City Watch a good Discworld starting point?**  
Yes. It is one of the usual good ones, alongside [Witches](/guides/discworld-witches-reading-order/) and [Death](/guides/discworld-death-reading-order/). This site starts here because the Watch is a true sequence.

**Do I need Theatre of Cruelty?**  
No.

**What about the Watch in other novels?**  
Let them surprise you after *Snuff*, or after you start wandering the rest of the Disc.

**Should I read by theme or by publication across all Discworld?**  
For the Watch, publication. For the whole Disc, use a publisher map. We are not turning this page into a 41-book chart.
