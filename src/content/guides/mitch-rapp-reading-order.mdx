Short version of the door: [Start Mitch Rapp with American Assassin?](/questions/mitch-rapp-american-assassin-first/). This page is the numbered publication list.

Flynn wrote ten Rapp novels, then two young-Rapp prequels, then *The Last Man*. Kyle Mills continued from *The Survivor*. Don Bentley took over at *Capture or Kill*. Those are handoffs, not new series and not new book ones.

The official saga page leads with chronology: *American Assassin*, *Kill Shot*, *Denied Access*, then *Transfer of Power*. That is a reread map and a retailer habit. It is how people skip the book that actually invented the job.

*Term Limits* (1997) is Flynn’s first novel. It is the same Washington weather. Rapp is not the protagonist. It is not Mitch Rapp book one.

*Double Tap* is the 2026 young-Rapp volume. Do not wait for it before opening *Transfer of Power*. It is another origin-era book, not a door.

## Chronology, labeled

Young Rapp, in story time: *American Assassin*, *Kill Shot*, *Denied Access*, then (when it lands) *Double Tap*, then *Transfer of Power* and the rest.

**Contested order, labeled:** the estate site and a lot of “complete Rapp” widgets start at *American Assassin*. The 2017 film does the same. That path is coherent on a reread. For a first read it turns Flynn’s invention of the character into a flash-forward. **House rule:** publication. The prequels stay in the numbered list where they were published.

*Capture or Kill* is a Bentley novel that sits earlier in Rapp’s calendar than its 2024 jacket. Still read it where it was published, or after you already like the voice. It is not an on-ramp.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *American Assassin* because the movie did, or because a chronological list numbered it 1.
- Starting with *Denied Access* or *Kill Shot* because they look like origins.
- Treating *Term Limits* as Mitch Rapp book one.
- Skipping from Flynn to Bentley and missing that Mills wrote a decade in the middle.
- Searching only “Rapp” and picking up the newest hardcover as book one.

## FAQ

**American Assassin first?**  
Not for a first read. It is a 2010 prequel. Start with *Transfer of Power*.

**Do I need Term Limits?**  
No. Same author, different book.

**When did Kyle Mills take over?**  
*The Survivor* (2015). Don Bentley from *Capture or Kill* (2024). Still the same numbered run.

**Is Denied Access book one?**  
No. It continues the young-Rapp stretch after *Kill Shot*. Publication places it twenty-fourth.
