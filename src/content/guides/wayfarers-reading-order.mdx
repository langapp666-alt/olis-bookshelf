*Monk & Robot* is a different Chambers series. It is not Wayfarers 5.

**House rule:** read book one. Continue in publication order if you want more of the universe. Hopping after book one is allowed; starting on book three because it is “the found-family one” still costs you the dialect.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Galaxy, and the Ground Within* because it is quieter.
- Treating *Monk & Robot* as a continuation.
- Skipping book one because someone said the series is “all standalones.”

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
Book one first. After that, publication order is kindest. Start at *The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet* (2014).

**Is the series finished?**  
The four novels are a published set.
