Short version of the fork: [Practical Magic: publication or chronological?](/questions/practical-magic-publication-or-chronological/). This page is both numberings, labeled.

Four novels. Two honest paths. Hoffman’s own site says it is your choice: written order, or story-time order that begins in the 1600s. Amazon and many “complete series” widgets prefer chronology, which makes *Magic Lessons* look like book one.

**House rule:** publication first. Chronology is allowed, and it is not a trap on the scale of a spoiler-heavy assassin origin. It is still a different door: you meet Maria, then Franny and Jet, then Sally and Gillian. Publication lets the 1995 novel be a novel, not episode three.

*The Invisible Hour* (2023) is a separate Hoffman book. It is not Practical Magic 5.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Magic Lessons* because Amazon numbered the family tree as a tetralogy from Maria forward.
- Starting with *The Book of Magic* because it is the last hardcover.
- Treating *The Invisible Hour* as Practical Magic 5.
- Waiting to “do them in order” until you have decided which order, and then reading none of them.

## FAQ

**Publication or chronological?**  
Hoffman allows both. This house starts with *Practical Magic* (1995). Chronology begins with *Magic Lessons*.

**Why does Amazon start with Magic Lessons?**  
That is story time. It is not a first-read mercy, and it is not a command.

**Is the series finished?**  
The four Owens novels are a complete visit.

**Do I need the film first?**  
No. The book is the door.
