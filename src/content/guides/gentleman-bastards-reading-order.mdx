Read them in this order. Book two changes cities; it is still book two. Book three is written for people who already know the earlier costs.

*The Thorn of Emberlain* and later announced titles are not on this table until they are books you can hold. Do not wait for them before opening *Lies*.

Short fiction exists around this world. Optional. Not a prequel on-ramp.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with book three because a blurb mentioned Sabetha.
- Treating book two as a standalone pirate novel.
- Postponing book one until the series is “done.”
- Using a film-or-show rumor as a reading order.

## FAQ

**Is the series finished?**  
Not as originally sketched. Three novels are published. Start at *The Lies of Locke Lamora* (2006).

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
Yes.

**Standalone?**  
*Lies* can be visited alone. You will be leaving a conversation mid-sentence if you continue later.
