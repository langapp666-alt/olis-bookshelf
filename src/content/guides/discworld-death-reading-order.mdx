Death cameos elsewhere. Those cameos are not secret Death novels. *The Colour of Magic* is not book zero of this path.

The Sky / Hallmark *Hogfather* film is a cousin. Watch it after the book if you want the joke in the right order.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Hogfather*.
- Opening the whole Discworld list because Death walked through *Guards! Guards!*.
- Treating *Soul Music* as a standalone because of the title.
- Waiting to “read Discworld in order” from book one of the entire series.

## FAQ

**Do I need earlier Discworld first?**  
No.

**Is Thief of Time the end of Death?**  
It is the end of this novel line. Death continues to appear. That is not a sequel list.

**Hogfather first because of Christmas?**  
No.
