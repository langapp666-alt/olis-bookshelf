The contested Roots of Chaos question is publication versus chronology. Shannon’s cycle page lists *Priory* third in time and first in print. This house starts where the cycle started: the 2019 novel. Later volumes can stand as their own visits; that does not make the long prequel book one.

Short version of the door: [Where to start Roots of Chaos](/questions/where-to-start-roots-of-chaos/). Same argument, smaller closet: [Narnia, publication or chronological?](/questions/narnia-publication-or-chronological/). This page is the three objects, labeled.

Three published prose objects so far. *A Day of Fallen Night* is a full-length prequel five centuries earlier. *Among the Burning Flowers* is a shorter direct prequel to *Priory*, and Shannon names it as a possible start if you want a faster read. For a first pass, still open *Priory*.

## Chronology — labeled, not the on-ramp

**Contested order, labeled:**

1. *A Day of Fallen Night*
2. *Among the Burning Flowers*
3. *The Priory of the Orange Tree*

That path keeps future knowledge out of the Grief of Ages. It also hands you the longest, most political book first. Shannon has noted that *Priory* is the easier layer of knowledge if you want one.

**House rule:**

1. *The Priory of the Orange Tree*
2. *A Day of Fallen Night* if you want more of the cycle
3. *Among the Burning Flowers* if you want the short bridge back toward *Priory*

If you bounced off *Priory*’s length and want the short door Shannon names, *Among the Burning Flowers* is allowed. Then read *Priory* before treating the cycle as done. Do not open *A Day of Fallen Night* as “the real book one.”

*The Bone Season* is a different Shannon series.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *A Day of Fallen Night* because a chronology chart put it first.
- Starting with *Among the Burning Flowers* and stopping there.
- Treating *Priory* as optional preamble to the prequels.
- Mixing in *The Bone Season* as Roots of Chaos 0.

## FAQ

**Where to start Roots of Chaos?**  
*The Priory of the Orange Tree* (2019). Publication first.

**Roots of Chaos reading order — publication or chronological?**  
Publication for a first read. Chronology — *Fallen Night*, then *Burning Flowers*, then *Priory* — is a later visit.

**Can I read A Day of Fallen Night first?**  
Shannon designed them to work as standalones. Chronology and publication still change what you know when. This house starts at *Priory*.

**Is Among the Burning Flowers a novel?**  
It is a shorter, illustrated prequel. Not *Priory* 2, and not a substitute for *Priory*.

**Do I need The Bone Season first?**  
No.

**Is the cycle finished?**  
Three published visits. Shannon has described a longer cycle. Later books will not be a new *Priory*.
