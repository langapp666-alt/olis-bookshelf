## Novellas

McGuire publishes novellas between many of the novels (*Full of Briars*, *Of Things Forgotten*, and others). **House rule:** read the novels in order. Insert a novella if you have it and it is labeled as sitting after the book you just finished. Do not start the series on a novella because it is shorter. Do not postpone *Rosemary and Rue* until you have hunted every chapbook.

*InCryptid* and *Wayward Children* are other McGuire shelves. See [Wayward Children](/guides/wayward-children-reading-order/). They are not Toby prequels.

## Common mistakes

- Starting on book eight because someone said the series “gets good later.”
- Mixing Wayward Children into this table.
- Reading *The Innocent Sleep* first because it is a companion.
- Using a wiki chronology that starts with a short about someone else’s war.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read the novellas?**  
No. They help. They are not secret novels.

**Is the series finished?**  
No. Start anyway.

**Sleep No More or The Innocent Sleep first?**  
*Sleep No More*, then *The Innocent Sleep*.
