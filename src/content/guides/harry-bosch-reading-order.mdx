Connelly’s series page is the list this page follows: Bosch novels in published order. Haller, Ballard, and McEvoy are parallel shelves that share some books. A “complete Connelly chronology” that braids every cameo is a different project. For a first Bosch visit, stay on the Bosch list and step sideways only at the two doors that change what a later Bosch novel assumes.

The Amazon series *Bosch* and *Bosch: Legacy* are cousins. Episode order is not book order.

A further Bosch novel, *The Hollow*, is announced for November 2026. It was not out when this page was checked. It is not a new door.

Read them in this order. The cases accumulate, and so do the people who later share the books.

**Before *The Narrows*:** read *The Poet* (1996). Connelly marks *The Narrows* as its sequel.

**Before *A Darkness More Than Night*, if you want McCaleb’s own start:** *Blood Work* (1998) is his book. You can still read the Bosch list without it. You will meet him mid-path.

**Before *Dark Sacred Night*, if you came for Ballard:** *The Late Show* (2017) is her book one. From *Dark Sacred Night* forward, the Bosch list is also her list.

**House path if you only want Bosch:** *The Black Echo* straight through the Bosch table. Detour to *The Poet* before *The Narrows*. Detour to *The Late Show* before *Dark Sacred Night* if Ballard is why you are here.

**House path if you want both Bosch and Haller:** Bosch published order, and read each Haller novel when its year falls between Bosch books, starting Haller at *The Lincoln Lawyer*. Do not open Bosch on *The Brass Verdict*.

*Nightshade* (2025) and *Ironwood* (2026) are the Catalina books (Detective Stilwell). *Ironwood* also uses Renée Ballard. They are not Bosch novels.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Late Show* or *The Waiting* because the recent covers feature Ballard.
- Starting with *The Lincoln Lawyer* because of the Netflix series, then treating Haller books as Bosch 1 through 8.
- Starting with *Nine Dragons* or *The Crossing* because a “Bosch and Haller” sticker looked like a fresh start.
- Skipping *The Poet* and opening *The Narrows* cold.
- Using the TV season list as the novel table of contents.

## FAQ

**Where do I start Harry Bosch?**  
*The Black Echo* (1992).

**Publication or chronological?**  
For Bosch, the author’s published order is the chronology that matters. There is no prequel novel to put first.

**Do I need The Lincoln Lawyer first?**  
No. Read it before the Haller books. It is not Bosch book one.

**Do I need The Late Show before Dark Sacred Night?**  
If you want Ballard’s introduction, yes. If you are only following Bosch, you can meet her when the Bosch list reaches *Dark Sacred Night*.

**What about The Poet?**  
Read it before *The Narrows*. It is McEvoy book one.

**Is The Waiting the last book?**  
It is the latest published Bosch novel on the author list when this page was checked. *The Hollow* was announced and not used as a starting point.
