Short version of the First Law door: [Where to start The First Law](/questions/where-to-start-first-law/). This page is the later generation only.

Three novels. Publication order. They assume the original trilogy’s weather. The standalones between the two trilogies (*Best Served Cold*, *The Heroes*, *Red Country*) are useful and not strictly required for every name in Age of Madness — but skipping the original three is how people get lost.

**House path:**

1. First Law trilogy (*The Blade Itself*, *Before They Are Hanged*, *Last Argument of Kings*)
2. The three standalones if you want the full conversation
3. Age of Madness

**Contested skip, labeled:** you *can* open *A Little Hatred* cold. You will spend the trilogy paying a tax the original books were designed to collect. This house does not recommend it.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *A Little Hatred* because the covers look newer.
- Starting with *The Wisdom of Crowds*.
- Treating Age of Madness as a reboot.

## What to read next

1. **[The First Law](/guides/first-law-reading-order/) (Joe Abercrombie)** — If you have not read it. That is the actual door.
2. **[The First Law](/guides/first-law-reading-order/) standalones, then this trilogy.** If you already finished the original three.
3. **[Gentleman Bastards](/guides/gentleman-bastards-reading-order/) (Scott Lynch)** — If you want another grim crew, unfinished.
4. **[The Poppy War](/guides/poppy-war-reading-order/) (R. F. Kuang)** — If you want another finished grim war trilogy.
5. **Stop after The Wisdom of Crowds.** This later trilogy is complete.

## FAQ

**Can I start here?**  
You can. You should not.

**Do I need the standalones?**  
They help. The original trilogy helps more.

**Is Age of Madness finished?**  
Yes.
