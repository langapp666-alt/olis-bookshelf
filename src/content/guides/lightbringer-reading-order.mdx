Five novels. Publication order is the only path. The series was discussed at different projected lengths while it was being written. The published object is five. Older forum posts that stop at book three are leftovers, not a door.

*The Night Angel* books are a different Weeks shelf. They are not Lightbringer 0.

Read them in this order. There is no honest mid-series start.

*Gunner’s Apprentice* is an optional short Weeks released around *The Broken Eye*. If you want it, after *The Blinding Knife*. It is not book 2.5 required homework.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Broken Eye* because someone remembered Lightbringer as a trilogy.
- Starting with *The Burning White* because it is the last hardcover.
- Treating *Night Angel* as a prequel.
- Searching “Lightbringer” and picking up a later Weeks project as book one.

## FAQ

**Is Lightbringer finished?**  
Yes. *The Burning White* is the fifth and last novel.

**Do I need Gunner’s Apprentice?**  
No.

**Do I need Night Angel first?**  
No.

**Can I start at book three?**  
No.
