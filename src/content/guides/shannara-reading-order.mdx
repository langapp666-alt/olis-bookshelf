  It is book one in print (1977). Then *The Elfstones of Shannara*, then *The Wishsong of Shannara*. Many readers enter at *Elfstones* instead. Do not start on a Genesis of Shannara prequel or a Fall of Shannara finale book.
</StartHere>

A long Four Lands shelf with more than one honest door. Publication order for the original trilogy is the house first path. Retail “complete Shannara” lists flatten prequels, Heritage novels, and later cycles into one numbered column. That is the trap.

This page is an **entry map**, not a thirty-book spreadsheet. Finish the original three. Decide later whether you want Heritage, Voyage, High Druid, or the later cycles.

**Contested door, labeled:** a long-standing fan recommendation starts at *Elfstones* and treats *Sword* as optional later. Brooks has also published suggested new-reader paths that do not begin on *Sword*. **House rule for this site:** *Sword* → *Elfstones* → *Wishsong* if you asked for Shannara reading order. *Elfstones* first is allowed if you already know you want that door — then read *Wishsong*, and only circle back to *Sword* if you care about the earliest Ohmsford.

Stop after *Wishsong* if you only wanted the classic trilogy. Heritage assumes that world already happened.

Later cycles (Voyage of the Jerle Shannara, High Druid, Legends of Shannara, Defenders, Fall of Shannara) continue in publication order after Heritage. They are not required for a first visit.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Armageddon's Children* because a “new reader” list puts the Genesis books first.
- Starting with *The Elfstones of Shannara* and never learning that *Sword* exists — fine if intentional; confusing if you wanted the print door.
- Starting with *The Scions of Shannara* because the covers look current.
- Treating the TV *Shannara Chronicles* episode order as the novel table of contents.

## FAQ

**Sword or Elfstones first?**  
This house: *Sword* first. *Elfstones* first is a labeled alternate door, not the default.

**Do I need First King of Shannara?**  
No. Optional after the original three.

**Where do the Genesis books go?**  
Deep past. Not the on-ramp for “Shannara reading order.”

**Is the series finished?**  
Many cycles are complete. New Four Lands work can still appear; this page stays an entry map.
