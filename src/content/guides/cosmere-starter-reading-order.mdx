This is **not** a complete Cosmere reading order. Those lists argue with each other, go stale, and turn *The Final Empire* into homework. We already have focused guides for the two doors most readers mean:

- [Mistborn Era 1 and 2](/guides/mistborn-era-1-2-reading-order/)
- [The Stormlight Archive](/guides/stormlight-archive-reading-order/)

Use those for the internal order, novellas, and *Secret History*. This page only answers “which planet first?”

**House rule:**

1. Choose Mistborn if you want a finished trilogy before anyone mentions a second era.
2. Choose Stormlight if you want the big series Sanderson is in the middle of, and you accept a long first book.
3. Do not read both first chapters “to decide” and then neither.

Sanderson also lists *Elantris*, *Warbreaker*, and *The Emperor’s Soul* as possible starts. They are real doors. They are not required before Mistborn or Stormlight.

*Arcanum Unbounded* is a collection, not Cosmere book 2. *White Sand* is another shelf. *Skyward* is not Cosmere. See the [Skyward guide](/guides/skyward-reading-order/).

## Common mistakes

- Opening a 40-title spreadsheet and postponing *The Final Empire*.
- Reading *Warbreaker* first because a forum said you “have to” before *Words of Radiance*.
- Starting Stormlight novellas first because they are shorter.
- Treating the secret projects as Cosmere book one.

## FAQ

**Do I need the whole Cosmere?**  
No. Sanderson is explicit: series are sequential inside themselves. Tidbits are optional.

**Mistborn or Stormlight first?**  
Either. This house slightly prefers Mistborn Era 1 if you want a complete argument sooner.

**Is Skyward Cosmere?**  
No.

**Where does Secret History go?**  
On the Mistborn page, not here.
