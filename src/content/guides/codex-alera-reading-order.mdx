## Common mistakes

- Starting with *First Lord's Fury* because the title sounds like an origin.
- Starting with *Academ's Fury* after someone said book one is “slow.”
- Mixing Dresden novels into the Alera numbered list.
- Waiting for a seventh novel that is not part of this finished six.
