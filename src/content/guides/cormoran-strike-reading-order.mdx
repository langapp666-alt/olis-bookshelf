## Starting point

Start with **The Cuckoo’s Calling**. It is the first novel and the first time you meet Cormoran Strike and Robin Ellacott as a working partnership that does not yet know it is one.

There is no prequel on-ramp. There is no “begin with the best case.” Later books spend capital earned in the earlier ones: the agency, the press, the families, and the leftover damage from each investigation.

Robert Galbraith is the name on the spine. Use it when you search. The author’s official series page lists the books in publication order. That is the only honest first read.

*The Hallmarked Man* is book 8 (2 September 2025). It is not a starting point.

**Sleep Tight, Evangeline** was announced on 13 February 2026 as book 9. No publication date was posted when last checked. Do not invent one, and do not wait for it before starting the series.

The novels get longer. That is a feature of the cases, not a reason to jump in at book five because a review called it the richest.

## Television

The BBC/HBO *Strike* adaptations are cousins. They are not a table of contents. If you watched first, still start with *The Cuckoo’s Calling*. Casting and compression are not a reading order.

There are no canonical novellas sitting between the novels the way Rivers of London or Dresden shorts do. That is part of why this series is simpler than it looks: eight novels, publication order, one announced ninth.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Troubled Blood* or *The Running Grave* because they are the ones in the window display.
- Treating the TV series as a substitute for the early books.
- Searching only “Galbraith” and picking up a later hardcover as book one.
- Waiting for book 9 before opening book 1. The first eight are already a sequence.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
Yes, if you care about Strike and Robin as a firm. Each case is a case. The partnership is the series.

**Is The Hallmarked Man a standalone?**  
It is a novel with a complete case. It is still book 8.

**When is Sleep Tight, Evangeline?**  
Announced February 2026. Date not posted on the official book page when last opened.

**Does the TV show spoil the books?**  
It follows early cases closely enough that you should treat seasons as adaptations, not as a free first chapter.

**Are there shorts I am missing?**  
Nothing that belongs on this table. The official page is novels.
