- Postponing book one until the trilogy is “done.”

## FAQ

**The Will of the Many books in order?**  
*The Will of the Many* (2023), then *The Strength of the Few*. Publication order. The trilogy is unfinished. Licanius is a different series, not a prequel.

**Is the series finished?**  
No. Start at *The Will of the Many* (2023). Two novels are published; *The Strength of the Few* is book two.

**Do I need Licanius first?**  
No.
