Wayward Children is a set of short novels about doors and the school that catches the children who come back. Publication order is the first-read path. Chronological charts that start with a later origin novella miss how the school is introduced.

Odd-numbered volumes tend to continue the school; even-numbered volumes often tell a single child’s earlier door. That pattern is a critic’s convenience, not a rule you must enforce. Read them as published.

**House rule:** start with *Every Heart a Doorway*. After that, publication order. You can skip an origin volume if a particular door is not for you; you will understand less of the later school books that lean on that child.

Do not start with *In an Absent Dream* because it is beloved. Do not start with the newest hardcover because it is newest.

## What you can skip

Nothing is required after book one in the bookstore sense. The series is designed as a conversation. Skipping all the origin books gives you the school without the earlier lives. Skipping the school books and reading only origins gives you fairy tales without the hallway they share.

This page does not rank doors.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with a later origin because it is “first for that character.”
- Treating the series as 11 unrelated novellas you can shuffle.
- Waiting for a final volume before reading book one. The series is ongoing.
- Mixing these with October Daye or InCryptid. Different McGuire shelves.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read every volume?**  
No. Start with book one. After that, skip an origin if you must.

**Is there a chronological order?**  
There are charts. They are reread toys. Publication first.

**Is the series finished?**  
No. *Through Gates of Garnet and Gold* is not advertised here as a finale.

**October Daye first?**  
Different series. Different door.
