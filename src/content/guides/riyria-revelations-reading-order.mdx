Sullivan wrote Revelations first, then went backward for Chronicles, then sideways for *Legends of the First Empire*. Publication of Revelations first is the house path. Chronological charts that start in the deep past are a reread project.

If you have the original six paperbacks, read them in that internal order: *The Crown Conspiracy*, *Avempartha*, *Nyphron Rising*, *The Emerald Storm*, *Wintertide*, *Percepliquis*. The omnibuses are the same six.

**Contested order, labeled:** Sullivan has said new readers can start with Chronicles if they want the duo younger. That is allowed. It is not kinder if you came for the original heist energy. **House rule:** *Theft of Swords* first. Chronicles after, if you want more Hadrian and Royce.

*Legends of the First Empire* and *Rise and Fall* are deeper history. After Revelations, not before. They will happily spoil the fun of discovering this world in *Theft of Swords*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Crown Tower* because it is “first.”
- Starting with *Age of Myth* (Legends) because a complete-chronology chart said so.
- Buying only *Percepliquis* because the title sounds like an ending you can sneak up on.

## FAQ

**Omnibus or original six?**  
Same story. *Theft of Swords* is the Orbit omnibus; *The Crown Conspiracy* (2008) is the first original novel inside it. Omnibus is how most shops sell it now.

**Chronicles first?**  
Allowed. Not the house first read.

**Do I need Legends of the First Empire?**  
No. Not before Revelations.
