People still call this a trilogy. The first three are a finished Diana-and-Matthew argument. Harkness then kept going. Publication order is the first-read path.

Her FAQ, checked 2026-09-13: *A Discovery of Witches*, *Shadow of Night*, *The Book of Life*, *Time’s Convert*, *The Black Bird Oracle*. She is editing a later book, *The Falcon and the Rose*. That will not be an on-ramp.

**House rule:** read the three. If you want more of the family, *Time’s Convert*, then *The Black Bird Oracle*. Do not open book five because it is the new hardcover.

*The World of All Souls* is a companion to the first three. Optional. Not All Souls 3.5. *The Serpent’s Mirror* is a later project Harkness has mentioned; it is not a substitute for book one.

The television series *A Discovery of Witches* covers the original trilogy in cousin form. It is not a reason to skip to *Time’s Convert*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Black Bird Oracle* because it is newest.
- Starting with *Time’s Convert* because Marcus is the part you heard about.
- Treating the show as a table of contents for books four and five.
- Searching “Discovery of Witches” and picking up the companion as novel 4.

## FAQ

**Do I need Time’s Convert?**  
No. The trilogy ends. Harkness counts it as book four.

**Is Black Bird Oracle a starting point?**  
No.

**Will there be more?**  
Harkness says yes, and that the story she has imagined is not halfway done. Later books still follow *A Discovery of Witches*.

**Show first?**  
The show is a cousin of the first three. Start at the novel if you want the books.
