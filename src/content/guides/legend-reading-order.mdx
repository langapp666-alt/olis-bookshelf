A finished trilogy, then a later fourth. Publication order is the first-read path. Chronological charts put a prequel novella first. That novella assumes you already know the wanted posters.

Lu’s site calls *Rebel* book 4 in the Legend series. Penguin lists four books. The original three still end as a visit.

**Contested order, labeled:** chronological lists put *Life Before Legend* first. It is origin texture. It does not rebuild the Republic. **House rule:** *Legend*, *Prodigy*, *Champion*, then *Rebel* if you want the later generation. Insert the novella after the three if you have it.

*Life After Legend* pieces are short epilogue extras, not novels. Optional after *Champion*. Not a reason to skip *Rebel*, and not a reason to start there.

*Warcross* and *The Young Elites* are different Lu shelves.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Rebel* because it says book 4 and Eden is on the cover.
- Starting with *Life Before Legend* because it is “before.”
- Treating the original three as optional preamble to *Rebel*.
- Mixing in *Warcross* as Legend 5.

## FAQ

**Do I need Rebel?**  
No. The trilogy ends. Lu counts *Rebel* as book 4.

**Life Before Legend first?**  
Not for a first read.

**Is Warcross the same world?**  
No.
