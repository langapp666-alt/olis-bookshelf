Short version of the interleave: [Flesh and Fire before or after Blood and Ash?](/questions/flesh-and-fire-before-blood-and-ash/). This page is the four Sera novels plus the official weave.

Armentrout’s FAQ is unusually clear: Flesh and Fire is not a fully separate prequel. The two series are meant to be read together in release order. Reading all four Ember books first spoils mysteries the Poppy novels are still carrying.

**House rule:** do not start with *A Shadow in the Ember*. If you only want Poppy, use the FBAA page and stay on that numbered six. If you want the world, follow the weave above. The least damaging first insert is still after *The Crown of Gilded Bones*.

*Visions of Flesh and Blood* is a world compendium, written with Rayvn Salvador. Armentrout says it does not have to be read as part of the series, and it should not be read before *A Fire in the Flesh*.

Novellas and extra scenes exist. Optional. Not Ember 0.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with Flesh and Fire because it is chronological, or because Sera’s covers look like a new romantasy door.
- Reading all four Ember books before *From Blood and Ash*.
- Starting with *A Soul of Ash and Blood* or *Born of Blood and Ash* because the titles sound like origins.
- Mixing in *Covenant*, *Lux*, or *Fall of Ruin and Wrath* as if they were Blood and Ash world books.

## What to read next

1. **[From Blood and Ash](/guides/from-blood-and-ash-reading-order/) (Jennifer L. Armentrout)** — If you landed here first. That is the actual door.
2. **[ACOTAR](/guides/acotar-reading-order/) (Sarah J. Maas)** — If you want another romantasy with a novella that looks like a novel.
3. **[Empyrean](/guides/empyrean-reading-order/) (Rebecca Yarros)** — If you want another unfinished romantasy with a strict first book.
4. **[Zodiac Academy](/guides/zodiac-academy-reading-order/) (Caroline Peckham and Susanne Valenti)** — If you want another long dark romantasy whose neighboring series looks like book one.
5. **Stop after Born of Blood and Ash.** The four Ember novels are a complete Sera visit. Poppy’s later books are a different appetite.

## FAQ

**Flesh and Fire first?**  
Not for a first read. Armentrout’s FAQ wants release order across both series.

**Where does A Shadow in the Ember go?**  
After *The Crown of Gilded Bones*, before *The War of Two Queens*, on the official list.

**Do I need Visions of Flesh and Blood?**  
No. If you want it, after *A Fire in the Flesh*.

**Is Flesh and Fire finished?**  
The four Sera novels are complete. The shared world is not.
