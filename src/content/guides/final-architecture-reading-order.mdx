A finished space-opera three. Publication order is the only path. Retail “Tchaikovsky complete” lists sometimes park *Shards of Earth* next to *Children of Memory* as if one were the other’s prequel. They are neighboring shelves.

See [Children of Time](/guides/children-of-time-reading-order/) if that is the trilogy you actually wanted. [Shadows of the Apt](/guides/shadows-of-the-apt-reading-order/) is the ten-book fantasy series — *Empire in Black and Gold* first, not Architects and not this trilogy.

Read them in this order. There is no contested start and no chronological prequel.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Children of Time* because a shop sorted both under “Tchaikovsky space opera.”
- Starting with *Lords of Uncreation*.
- Waiting for a novella bridge that is not required to finish the three.

## What to read next

1. **[Children of Time](/guides/children-of-time-reading-order/) (Adrian Tchaikovsky)** — If you want his other finished SF trilogy (spiders, not Architects).
2. **[Shadows of the Apt](/guides/shadows-of-the-apt-reading-order/) (Adrian Tchaikovsky)** — If you wanted the ten-book fantasy run. *Empire in Black and Gold* is that door.
3. **[The Expanse](/guides/the-expanse-reading-order/) (James S. A. Corey)** — If multi-faction space politics was the hook.
4. **[Revelation Space](/guides/revelation-space-reading-order/) (Alastair Reynolds)** — If deep-time threat and crew-scale SF were the pleasure.
5. **Stop after Lords of Uncreation.** The three are complete.
