This page is a **Stormlight** guide. It is not a Cosmere reading-order chart. Stormlight is planned as ten novels in two five-book arcs. *Wind and Truth* (2024) closes the first arc. Books 6–10 are later work. Do not postpone book one until you have a 40-title spreadsheet.

If you want the other Cosmere door Sanderson names, use the [Mistborn Era 1 and 2 guide](/guides/mistborn-era-1-2-reading-order/). You do not need both doors before you open one.

## The novellas

Sanderson wrote two Stormlight novellas to sit *between* the big books. They are optional in the strict sense and useful in the practical one.

**House rule:**

1. *The Way of Kings*
2. *Words of Radiance*
3. *Edgedancer* if you have it
4. *Oathbringer*
5. *Dawnshard* if you have it
6. *Rhythm of War*
7. *Wind and Truth*

If your library does not have the novellas, keep going. Do not stall a five-book arc on a 200-page bridge.

*Edgedancer* also lives in *Arcanum Unbounded*. That collection is a Cosmere sampler, not Stormlight book 3. Do not read the whole collection as homework between *Words of Radiance* and *Oathbringer*.

## Cosmere, kept in its place

Sanderson’s page is explicit: you can start the Cosmere with Stormlight, Mistborn, *Elantris*, *Warbreaker*, or *The Emperor’s Soul*. Tidbits inform other books. You do not have to notice them to follow the story you are in.

A few Stormlight names and objects make more sense if you have read *Warbreaker*. That is a bonus, not a prerequisite. **Do not read Warbreaker first in order to “prepare” for The Way of Kings.** *The Way of Kings* is the preparation.

We are not listing a Cosmere publication order here. Those charts go stale, argue with each other, and turn a first novel into a syllabus. If a later Stormlight book makes you curious about another planet, go read that series then.

## Common mistakes

- Opening a Cosmere spreadsheet and postponing *The Way of Kings*.
- Starting with *Edgedancer* because it is shorter.
- Treating *Arcanum Unbounded* as Stormlight book 3.
- Reading *Warbreaker* first because a forum said you “have to” before *Words of Radiance*.
- Waiting for books 6–10 before starting the first arc. *Wind and Truth* is a planned pause, not an unfinished sentence you must sit out.

## FAQ

**Do I need the rest of the Cosmere first?**  
No. Sanderson lists *The Way of Kings* as a start.

**Do I need Edgedancer and Dawnshard?**  
No. They help. They are not secret novels.

**Is Wind and Truth the end?**  
It ends the first five-book arc. The series is planned to continue later.

**Can I start with Mistborn instead?**  
Yes. That is the other door. It is not a Stormlight prequel.

**Which printing of The Way of Kings?**  
Whichever your shop has. The text is the book.
