**House rule:** read the three novels. *Kill Joy* is optional texture. *The Reappearance of Rachel Price* is a different Jackson book, not AGGGTM 4.

The TV adaptation is a cousin of book one. It is not a reason to skip to book three.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *As Good As Dead*.
- Treating *Kill Joy* as book one because it is “earlier.”
- Mixing in *Rachel Price* as a continuation.

## FAQ

**Do I need Kill Joy?**  
No. Start at *A Good Girl’s Guide to Murder* (2019).

**Is the trilogy finished?**  
Yes.

**TV first?**  
The show is a cousin of book one.
