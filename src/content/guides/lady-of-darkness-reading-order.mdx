The author’s current books page calls this a completed five-book series plus one novella. Her older suggested-order page lists the same five novels and puts *The Reaper* after *Lady of Embers*. Publication of those five is the first-read path.

Retailer “book 6” labels on *Winds of Darkness* are later. They are not a new on-ramp.

**House rule:** read the five. Insert *The Reaper* after *Lady of Embers* if you want Rayner’s extra. *Winds of Darkness* after *Lady of Starfire* if you want the later visit. Do not open either because a shop window numbered them first.

Bonus chapters on her extras page (*Cyrus*, *The Fire General*, *The First Meeting*) are shorts. Not novels.

## Neighboring series

*The Legacy* (beginning *Rain of Shadows and Endings*) and *Avonleya* sit in the wider Chaosverse. The author has said the series can be read on their own. They are still a poor place to meet Scarlett for the first time.

**House rule:** finish these five before you go hunting a later continent.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Reaper* because it is “earlier” for Rayner.
- Starting with *Winds of Darkness* because a retailer called it book 6 and it is the new hardcover.
- Starting with *Rain of Shadows and Endings* because Legacy looks like a cleaner door.
- Treating *Lady of Shadows* as a different series because the title sounds like a later brand.

## FAQ

**Do I need The Reaper?**  
No. Her suggested-order page puts it after book four. It is not required to reach *Lady of Starfire*.

**Is Winds of Darkness book six?**  
Some lists number it that way. Her current books page still describes a five-book series plus a novella. Either way it is not an on-ramp.

**Do I need Legacy first?**  
No.
