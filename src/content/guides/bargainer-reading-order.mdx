Four volumes: three Callie novels and one Desmond novella. Publication order is the first-read path, which also happens to be the honest chronological placement for the extra.

Retail lists sometimes file *The Emperor of Evening Stars* as book 0 or as book 3. Both labels are half true. It was published between books 2 and 3. Story time starts long before *Rhapsodic*. The last stretch of it sits after *A Strange Hymn*. That is why “read the prequel first” is the wrong instruction.

**House rule:** *Rhapsodic*, *A Strange Hymn*, *The Emperor of Evening Stars*, *Dark Harmony*. Skip the novella if you want; do not move it in front of book one.

Tandem charts that braid *Emperor* into *Rhapsodic* chapter by chapter exist. They are a reread toy. The novella was not designed as required dual-POV homework for a first visit.

The complete-series omnibus is the same four books. It is not a fifth title.

Thalassa’s other shelves (*Four Horsemen*, *Bewitched*, *Fallen World*) are different doors. They are not Bargainer 5.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Emperor of Evening Stars* because it is shorter, earlier, or labeled a prequel.
- Reading the novella after *Rhapsodic* but before *A Strange Hymn* — the ending of *Emperor* assumes you have already finished book 2.
- Starting with *Dark Harmony*.
- Mixing a Horsemen book into the numbered Bargainer column.

## FAQ

**What is the Bargainer reading order?**  
Start with *Rhapsodic* (2016), then *A Strange Hymn*, then *The Emperor of Evening Stars*, then *Dark Harmony*. The Emperor novella is not book one.

**Where does The Emperor of Evening Stars go?**  
After *A Strange Hymn*, before *Dark Harmony*. Not before *Rhapsodic*.

**Do I need the novella?**  
You can finish the novels without it. If you want Desmond’s earlier life, read it after book 2.

**Can I tandem-read Emperor with Rhapsodic?**  
You can on a reread. For a first read, novels then novella.

**Is the series finished?**  
Yes.
