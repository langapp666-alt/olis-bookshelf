## Starting point

Start with **Mistborn: The Final Empire** (often shelved as *Mistborn*). It is Era 1, book one.

Do not start with *The Alloy of Law*. Sanderson’s own Cosmere on-ramp page calls Alloy “an okay jumping in spot” and immediately warns that it has spoilers for the original trilogy. Era 2 is the same planet a few centuries later. It is written for people who already know what the first trilogy did to that world.

This page covers **Mistborn Era 1 and Era 2 only**. It is not a Cosmere reading-order guide. Stormlight, *Warbreaker*, and the secret-project novels are other shelves.

## Era 1 — the original trilogy

| # | Title | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Final Empire | 2006 |
| 2 | The Well of Ascension | 2007 |
| 3 | The Hero of Ages | 2008 |

Read these three in order. There is no contested start inside Era 1.

## Era 2 — Wax and Wayne

Set roughly three hundred years later. Four books. The era is complete.

| # | Title | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Alloy of Law | 2011 |
| 2 | Shadows of Self | 2015 |
| 3 | The Bands of Mourning | 2016 |
| 4 | The Lost Metal | 2022 |

Era 2 is shorter, more case-shaped, and more of a partnership book. It still assumes Era 1’s history. If you bounced off Era 1’s middle book, that is a taste problem. Starting Era 2 instead will not “skip the slog” without also skipping the ending Era 2 keeps talking around.

## Secret History and the extras

**Mistborn: Secret History** is a companion novella, not Era 1.5 in the bookstore sense.

Sanderson’s trilogy page is blunt: it contains huge spoilers for the original trilogy and **very minor spoilers for *The Bands of Mourning***. His current advice is to treat it as a companion after you have the Era 1 ending, with the BoM caveat attached.

**House rule for a first read:**

1. Finish Era 1.
2. Read Era 2 through *The Bands of Mourning*.
3. Read *Secret History*.
4. Read *The Lost Metal*.

If you are impatient and you accept a few Era 2 names leaking backward, *Secret History* can follow *The Hero of Ages*. That is the older fan habit. It is not cleaner. It is just earlier.

Other extras stay out of the numbered columns:

- **The Eleventh Metal** — short, collected in *Arcanum Unbounded*. Optional texture around Era 1.
- **Allomancer Jak and the Pits of Eltania** — Era 2 magazine pastiche in the same collection. Skip on a first pass.
- **Era 3** — planned; not a reading-order problem yet. Do not wait for it before reading 1 and 2.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Alloy of Law* because it is shorter and “more modern.”
- Reading *Secret History* before *The Hero of Ages*.
- Treating *Arcanum Unbounded* as Mistborn book 4.
- Opening a Cosmere spreadsheet and postponing Era 1 until you have a 40-book plan.
- Stopping after Era 1 because “the trilogy is the complete story” without knowing Era 2 is a finished second argument, not a teaser.

## FAQ

**Can I start with Era 2?**  
Sanderson allows it as a Cosmere jump. For *Mistborn*, start with Era 1. Alloy spoils the first trilogy’s outcome.

**Do I need Secret History?**  
No. Era 2 is readable without it. Era 2 is *richer* if you have it before *The Lost Metal*.

**Is this the whole Cosmere?**  
No. This page stops at Mistborn Eras 1 and 2 on purpose.

**Where does The Lost Metal end the story?**  
It ends Era 2. It does not end the planet. Era 3 is a later project.

**Which cover says Mistborn vs The Final Empire?**  
Same book. Search either.
