Nine major novels are out. A tenth, *A Blessing for a Warrior Going Out*, is listed on the author’s chronology page as forthcoming. Do not invent a date, and do not wait for it before starting.

The television series is a cousin. Season order follows the early novels more closely than most adaptations, then compresses. It is still not a substitute for book one.

There is no contested novel sequence. People who argue about Outlander order are usually arguing about Lord John, the graphic novel, or the shorts.

## Lord John — after Voyager, not before Outlander

The Lord John novels and novellas are part of the larger series. Gabaldon says they stand alone *and* that they reference people and situations from the main novels. Her “what to read when” note: you can read them once you have finished *Voyager*, because they sit in the middle of that book’s timeline.

If you want the Lord John pieces in the author’s listed story order, it runs: “Hellfire Club”; *Private Matter*; “Succubus”; *Brotherhood of the Blade*; “Haunted Soldier”; “Custom of the Army”; *The Scottish Prisoner*; “A Plague of Zombies.” The three middle shorts are in *Hand of Devils*. You do not need this list on a first Outlander pass.

**House rule:** novels 1–3, then optional Lord John, then novels 4–9. Or novels only. Do not open the series with a Grey mystery because it looks shorter.

## Other extras

Keep these out of the numbered novel column:

- **The Exile** — graphic novel retelling early material. Not an on-ramp.
- **A Leaf on the Wind of All Hallows**, **The Space Between**, **Virgins**, and other novellas — Gabaldon has a chronology for them. First-read: after the novel they sit beside, or not at all.
- Companion cookbooks and guides — reference, not book 6.5.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with Lord John because the hardcovers are thinner.
- Using a full chronological chart as book one through thirty.
- Treating the graphic novel as a shortcut around *Outlander*.
- Waiting for book 10 before starting. Nine novels are already a sequence.
- Assuming the show’s later seasons are a table of contents for books you have not bought yet.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
The major novels, yes. Gabaldon says so. Start at *Outlander* (1991).

**When can I read Lord John?**  
After *Voyager*, if you want the author’s stated safe point.

**Is the tenth book out?**  
Not when last checked. The author’s chronology names it. No on-sale date is used here.

**Can I start with the show?**  
You can watch first. Still start the books at *Outlander*.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No.
