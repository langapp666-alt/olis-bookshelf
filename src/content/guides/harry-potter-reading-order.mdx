Short version of the door: [Where to start Harry Potter](/questions/where-to-start-harry-potter/). This page is the seven, plus extras labeled.

There is no honest debate about the seven novels. Publication order is the story order. The clutter is everything that sits beside them: screenplays, Hogwarts “textbooks,” a stage script, and illustrated extras that retailers file next to book one.

This page keeps the seven in a numbered column and labels the rest **optional**.

**House rule:** read the seven. Stop. If you want more of the same desk, the Beedle tales are the least confusing extra. The Fantastic Beasts *films* are a prequel cousin; the published screenplays are scripts, not the next Harry novel. *Cursed Child* is a play that continues characters after book seven. It is widely treated as extra-canonical by readers who want the seven to end where they end. This page does not make you read it.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with a Fantastic Beasts screenplay because the film is “first chronologically.”
- Treating *Cursed Child* as Harry Potter book 8.
- Buying *Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them* (the slim textbook) thinking it is the movie story.
- Waiting for a new Hogwarts novel before opening *Philosopher’s Stone*.
- Mixing US and UK titles and thinking *Sorcerer’s Stone* is a different first book.

## FAQ

**Philosopher’s Stone or Sorcerer’s Stone?**  
Same book. Search either.

**Do I need the Hogwarts textbooks?**  
No.

**Is Cursed Child required?**  
No. It is a play script published after the seven.

**Should I watch Fantastic Beasts before reading Harry?**  
No. The seven novels do not assume the films.

**Illustrated editions?**  
Same text, different pictures. Start with book one either way.
