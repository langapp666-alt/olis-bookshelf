This is the **full Holly path**: three Hodges novels, then the books where she is the investigator you came for. Publication order matches her working life. There is no chronological reshuffle to argue about.

If you only want the original trilogy, the [Bill Hodges page](/guides/bill-hodges-reading-order/) stops at *End of Watch*.

**House rule:** read 1–3 if you want her origin. If you insist on meeting her as lead, the least damaging skip is still not *Holly* first — it is *The Outsider* after a blunt warning that you will lack the Hodges years. This house prefers *Mr. Mercedes*.

*If It Bleeds* is a four-story collection. You only need the title novella for this path. The other stories are King extras, not secret Holly novels.

The HBO *Outsider* series and the *Mr. Mercedes* show are cousins. They do not agree with each other, and they are not a reading order.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Holly* because that is the name you searched.
- Starting with *Never Flinch* because it is newest.
- Treating *The Outsider* as a standalone with no Holly cost.
- Skipping *If It Bleeds* and then wondering why *Holly* talks as if a job already happened.

## FAQ

**Can I start with Holly (2023)?**  
You can follow that case. You will not have the years that book is using. Start with *Mr. Mercedes*.

**Do I need the whole If It Bleeds collection?**  
You need the title novella for the path.

**Is Never Flinch a reboot?**  
No. It is the next Holly novel.

**Is this horror or crime?**  
The Hodges books begin as crime. Later volumes admit King’s other tools. The order does not change.
