Nine novels. Publication order is the only path. The war moves. So does the partnership.

Novik’s later school trilogy is a different shelf. See [Scholomance](/guides/scholomance-reading-order/). *Uprooted* and *Spinning Silver* are standalones. They are not Temeraire 0.

Read them in this order. The first three arrived in one year. That is a publishing fact, not a reason to start at book three.

*Golden Age and Other Stories* (2017) is a later collection. Optional. Not Temeraire 10, and not an on-ramp.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *League of Dragons* because it is the finale on a list.
- Starting with *Throne of Jade* because China is the part you heard about.
- Treating this as a Scholomance extra.
- Searching “Temeraire” and picking up the UK book-one title as if it were a different series.

## What to read next

1. **[The Scholomance](/guides/scholomance-reading-order/) (Naomi Novik)** — If you want her other finished trilogy, which is a school, not a dragon transport.
2. **[His Dark Materials](/guides/his-dark-materials-reading-order/) (Philip Pullman)** — If you want another partnership that is also an argument about the world.
3. **[The Inheritance Cycle](/guides/inheritance-cycle-reading-order/) (Christopher Paolini)** — If you want another dragon-and-rider ladder, younger and finished in four.
4. **[Roots of Chaos](/guides/roots-of-chaos-reading-order/) (Samantha Shannon)** — If you want another complete dragon-and-state argument, with a prequel that should stay a prequel. Short door: [where to start Roots of Chaos / Priory](/questions/where-to-start-roots-of-chaos/).
5. **Stop after League of Dragons.** The nine are a complete visit.

## FAQ

**Is the series complete?**  
Yes. *League of Dragons* is the ninth and last novel.

**His Majesty’s Dragon or Temeraire?**  
Same book one. Search either.

**Do I need Scholomance first?**  
No.

**Are the short stories required?**  
No.
