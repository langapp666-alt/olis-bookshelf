**House rule:** read the four. *Midnight Sun* is aftercare or a reread toy. Starting there hands you book one’s events with a different narrator and none of the original door.

The films follow the four more closely than most adaptations. Film order is still not a reason to skip *New Moon*.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Midnight Sun* because it is the newest thick book.
- Starting with *Breaking Dawn* because of a film still.
- Treating *Life and Death* as Twilight 5.

## FAQ

The original four are finished.

**Which Twilight edition?**
