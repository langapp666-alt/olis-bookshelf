Short version of the door: [Where to start Rivers of London](/questions/where-to-start-rivers-of-london/). This page is the novels, plus novellas and comics labeled.

Do not start with a novella. Do not start with the comics. Do not start with *The Masquerades of Spring* because it is earlier in Nightingale’s life. That book is a 1920s jazz-age side story. It will not teach you the Folly as a modern police job.

The series is still growing. Novellas, comics, and a short-story collection sit between the numbered novels and confuse first-timers more than the novels do.

Aaronovitch’s own British-editions page, when opened, listed through *The Masquerades of Spring* and had not yet added *Stone and Sky*. Gollancz published that tenth novel in July 2025. If a shop page is stale, trust the most recent Gollancz/DAW novel, not the merch grid.

## Novellas (optional, not extra novels)

These are real books. They are not Peter Grant #6.5 in the spine sense. Read them after the novel they followed into shops, or save them for later.

Aaronovitch’s [chronology page](https://www.benaaronovitch.com/chronology/) interleaves novels, novellas, shorts, and comics. It is useful as a map of *when stories sit*, not as a first-read syllabus. It also still looks pre–*Stone and Sky*. Publication order for the novels remains the honest on-ramp.

*The Home Crowd Advantage* is set during the 2012 Olympics. The author has said it contains anachronisms because the series chronology had not firmed up. Do not use it to “fix” the timeline.

## Shorts and comics

**Tales from the Folly** (2020) collects shorts with author notes that say which novel each one sits beside. Use it as a cleanup volume after you already like the voice. Do not open the series with it.

The comics (*Body Work*, *Night Witch*, *Black Mould*, and the later graphic novels) are canon enough that the author’s chronology lists them among the books. They are still optional. A first-time reader who only does the novels will not be lost. A first-time reader who starts with *Body Work* will be starting in the middle of Peter’s job.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Masquerades of Spring* or *The October Man* because they look like gentler on-ramps.
- Treating *Midnight Riot* as a different series. Same first novel, US title.
- Numbering every novella into the Peter Grant count.
- Reading the comics first because they are shorter.
- Using the author’s chronology page as book one through thirty-eight.

## FAQ

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No. They add other desks (Abigail, Winter, Reynolds, 1920s Nightingale) and one Metropolitan-line case. The novels carry Peter’s job.

**Which title do I search in the US?**  
*Midnight Riot* or *Rivers of London*. Same book one.

**Is The Masquerades of Spring a prequel on-ramp?**  
It is earlier. It is not an on-ramp.

**Where do the comics go?**  
Optional, after you already know Peter’s job.
