This is a contested page, so here is the house position in one paragraph.

Polish magazine publication, Polish book publication, and English publication are three different histories. *Sword of Destiny* appeared as a Polish book before the final *Last Wish* collection. English readers got *The Last Wish*, then jumped to *Blood of Elves*, and only later received *Sword of Destiny*. **The first-read path is not “pick up whichever paperback says Book One.”** It is the two collections, then the five-novel saga, then the later standalones.

Games and Netflix are cousins. They rearrange, invent, and skip. They are not a table of contents.

*Sword of Destiny* is listed second even though its Polish book date is 1992. That is the usual English reading sequence, and it is the sequence that introduces Ciri before the novels spend her. Lists that stamp it “Book 0” because of the copyright page are doing chronology of *editions*, not a first-read syllabus.

## The two later books

**Season of Storms** (2013) sits, in story time, among the early short-story years. It also knows the saga exists. Read it after *The Lady of the Lake*. Treating it as a missing chapter between *The Last Wish* stories is how people meet later furniture early.

**Crossroads of Ravens** (Polish 2024; English 2025) is a young-Geralt prequel. Some publisher copy will call any prequel a valid start. For this series, a first-time reader who starts there is starting with a late book about an earlier calendar. **House rule:** ninth, or not at all, on a first pass.

## What the games and the show are not

The CD Projekt games begin after the saga and invent their own continuation. They are not Witcher books 8–10.

Netflix draws on short stories and then on the novels, out of book order, with original material. If you watched first, still start with *The Last Wish*. You will recognize incidents. You will not be holding a season guide.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Blood of Elves* because it is the first novel.
- Skipping *Sword of Destiny* because English published it late.
- Reading *Season of Storms* or *Crossroads of Ravens* first because they are “earlier.”
- Using a game quest list as a novel order.
- Treating the short-story collections as optional flavor. The saga expects them.

## FAQ

**Can I skip the short stories?**  
You can. The novels will assume you did not.

**Why is Sword of Destiny year 1992 if I read it second?**  
Polish book publication. English readers still read it after *The Last Wish*.

**Is Blood of Elves book one?**  
It is saga novel one. It is not the start of the Witcher books.

**Should I read Crossroads of Ravens first?**  
No.

**Do I need the games to understand the books?**  
No. The books do not continue into the games.
