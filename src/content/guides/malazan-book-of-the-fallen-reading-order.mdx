This page is honest about difficulty. *Gardens of the Moon* drops you into a campaign already in motion. Names, gods, and armies arrive without a tour. That is the method, not a printing error. If you bounce, that is information. Starting at a later “easier” book will not fix it; it will only strand you in a different continent with the same expectation that you keep up.

The ten Book of the Fallen novels are one argument. Esslemont’s Novels of the Malazan Empire, the Path to Ascendancy prequels, the Kharkanas trilogy, and the later Witness books are other shelves. They can wait.

## The ten novels

Publication order is the first-read path. Erikson has said the order the books were written matters more than an internal timeline.

*Dust of Dreams* and *The Crippled God* are a split ending. Read them in order, back to back if you can.

*Deadhouse Gates* and *Midnight Tides* look like side trips. They are not optional. The later books spend capital those novels earn on other continents.

## What not to do with other Malazan books

Ian C. Esslemont’s **Novels of the Malazan Empire** (*Night of Knives* onward) overlap the ten. They are not Book of the Fallen 6.5. A common later habit is to sample Esslemont after *The Bonehunters*, or after the ten. A first-time reader who starts with *Night of Knives* is starting with a different author’s prequel energy.

The **Kharkanas** books (*Forge of Darkness*, *Fall of Light*, and the unfinished third) are deep-past prequels. They assume you already care about people the ten novels introduce as myth. **Do not start there.** A 2017 Tor article circulated a chronological list that put prequels first. Erikson later described that list as a timeline exercise, not a first-read order.

The **Witness** novels (*The God is Not Willing* and after) are a later generation. After the ten, if you want them.

Bauchelain and Korbal Broach novellas are comic-grim extras. Optional, after you already like the voice.

## Difficulty, without romance

Malazan has a reputation. Some of it is earned.

- You will not be oriented in chapter one. That is deliberate.
- Book two leaves several book-one people behind for a while. That is also deliberate.
- There is no gentler novel that teaches the same setting first.
- Publication order is the support system: each book is aimed at readers who have the previous books, not at readers who have a wiki open.

If you want a long epic that explains itself as it goes, this is the wrong shop. Try [Stormlight](/guides/stormlight-archive-reading-order/) or [Wheel of Time](/guides/wheel-of-time-reading-order/). If you want the campaign anyway, start at *Gardens* and give it more than fifty pages before you decide the method is not for you.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with a chronological chart or the 2017 Tor timeline piece.
- Starting with Kharkanas “for lore.”
- Skipping *Deadhouse Gates* or *Midnight Tides* because they change continent.
- Using Esslemont as an easier on-ramp.
- Opening a 20-book “complete Malazan” list and postponing book one until you own all of it.

## What to read next

1. **Esslemont’s Novels of the Malazan Empire, after the ten — or after *The Bonehunters* if you already know you want more of this war.** Same world, different desk.
2. **[The First Law](/guides/first-law-reading-order/) (Joe Abercrombie)** — If you want another complete grim argument that later books keep answering, in fewer volumes.
3. **[The Wheel of Time](/guides/wheel-of-time-reading-order/) (Robert Jordan)** — If you finished Erikson and want a long epic that will actually introduce the village.
4. **[The Stormlight Archive](/guides/stormlight-archive-reading-order/) (Brandon Sanderson)** — If you want campaign scale with a narrator who will teach the magic on the page.
5. **[The Culture](/guides/the-culture-reading-order/) (Iain M. Banks)** — If the gods-as-machinery aftertaste was the hook and you want it in space, with better manners and a contested start of its own.

## FAQ

**Is Gardens of the Moon a bad book one?**  
It is a hard book one. It is still book one.

**Can I start with Midnight Tides?**  
You can. You will be on the wrong continent with no reason to care yet.

**Do I need Esslemont?**  
Not for the ten. The ten close Book of the Fallen.

**Should I read Kharkanas first?**  
No.

**Is there a gentler entry?**  
Not inside this series. Pick a different series if that is the requirement.
