Throne of Glass is a finished eight-book argument if you count the novella collection. The contested pieces are **where the prequels go** and **whether to tandem-read *Empire of Storms* and *Tower of Dawn***.

ACOTAR and Crescent City are other Maas doors. Crescent City’s later books assume you already know this endgame. Finish TOG before *House of Sky and Breath* if you care about spoilers. See [ACOTAR](/guides/acotar-reading-order/) and [Crescent City](/guides/crescent-city-reading-order/).

**Contested order, labeled:**

- **Assassin’s Blade first.** Some readers want Celaena’s earlier jobs before book one. Bloomsbury says that is allowed and that publication placement after *Crown of Midnight* is the path they picture. This house follows Bloomsbury for a first read: meet the novel, then the prequels, then *Heir of Fire*.
- **Tandem read.** *Tower of Dawn* runs beside *Empire of Storms*. Reading ToD first spoils parts of EoS. Publication order (EoS then ToD) is clean. Chapter-by-chapter tandem charts exist for a reread. Do not postpone the series until you print a tandem spreadsheet.

## What is not Throne of Glass

*A Court of Thorns and Roses* is not TOG book 9. *House of Earth and Blood* is not TOG book 10. Cross-world cameos arrive later, in Crescent City. That is a reason to finish this series before those later CC novels, not a reason to shuffle TOG.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Assassin’s Blade* because it is “chronological.”
- Reading *Tower of Dawn* before *Empire of Storms*.
- Starting Crescent City book two before *Kingdom of Ash*.
- Quitting after book one because the later books are a different temperature — that change is designed, not a bait-and-switch you fix by starting on book five.

## FAQ

**Assassin’s Blade first or third?**  
Bloomsbury’s pictured path is third. First is a valid reread or a stubborn chronological taste. Not required.

**Do I have to tandem-read?**  
No. Publication order is enough.

**Is the series complete?**  
Yes.

**Can I start with ACOTAR instead?**  
Yes. That is a different door. It is not a TOG prequel.
