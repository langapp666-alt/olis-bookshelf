## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Babel* because it is the Kuang title in the window.
- Starting with *The Burning God* because the trilogy is “done.”
- Treating *The Drowning Faith* as required homework.
- Expecting a romantasy temperature because neighboring shelves are louder.

## What to read next

1. **[Broken Earth](/guides/broken-earth-reading-order/) (N. K. Jemisin)** — If you want another finished grim trilogy that earns its last book.
2. **[The First Law](/guides/first-law-reading-order/) (Joe Abercrombie)** — If you want another war that does not tidy itself, with a correct first door.
3. **[Book of the Fallen reading order](/guides/malazan-book-of-the-fallen-reading-order/) (Steven Erikson)** — If you want a much longer military-history argument. Not a light next step.
4. **[The Scholomance](/guides/scholomance-reading-order/) (Naomi Novik)** — If the school-as-weapon was the part you want in a different key.
5. **Babel (R. F. Kuang)** — If you want another Kuang novel as itself, not as a sequel.

## FAQ

**Is the trilogy complete?**  
Yes.

**Do I need The Drowning Faith?**  
No.

**Is Babel a sequel?**  
No.

**Can I stop after book one?**  
You can. You will be stopping in the middle of a designed three-book argument.
