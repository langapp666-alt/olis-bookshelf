Eight novels make Artemis’s run. They are a character ladder. The boy in book one is not the boy in book eight, and that is the point of reading them in order.

The Fowl Twins books are a later generation in the same shop. They are not Artemis 9–11, and they are not a cleaner on-ramp.

Read them in this order. There is no honest “start at book five” door. Graphic-novel adaptations of early books are the same cases in another script. They are not a shortcut around novel one.

Some editions number the titles (*Artemis Fowl 3: The Eternity Code*). Same books.

**House rule:** read the eight. If you want the younger brothers, read the Twins after *The Last Guardian*. Do not open *The Fowl Twins* because the cover looks like a fresh book one of the whole firm.

Colfer’s site keeps Artemis and the Twins on neighboring shelves. That is correct. Neighboring is not interchangeable.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with the Disney+ film and calling it the novel.
- Starting with *The Time Paradox* because time travel sounds like an origin.
- Treating the graphic novels as the reading order.
- Opening a Twins book because Artemis is “finished” and the new hardcover is in the window.

## FAQ

**Do I need the Twins books?**  
No. Artemis’s eight end.

**Graphic novels first?**  
No. Optional reread of early cases.

**Is the film a decent on-ramp?**  
It is a cousin. It is not the book order.

**Which cover says Artemis Fowl versus Artemis Fowl 1?**  
Same book. Search either.
