Short version of the fork: [Do I need Grant County before Will Trent?](/questions/will-trent-grant-county-first/). This page is both shelves, with the spoiler line marked.

Two Slaughter streets. Grant County is Sara Linton, Jeffrey Tolliver, and Lena Adams in Heartsdale. Will Trent is a GBI agent in Atlanta. They are not alternate book ones of the same series. They share a character after Grant County is already over.

The ABC/Hulu show is a cousin. Season order is not book order. The show is not a reason to skip Grant County if you want Sara’s first life intact.

*Pretty Girls*, *Cop Town*, *The Good Daughter*, *Pieces of Her*, and *We Are All Guilty Here* are other Slaughter doors. They are not Will Trent 13.

**UK title variants:** *Beyond Reach* is *Skin Privilege* in the UK and some other territories. *Undone* is *Genesis*. Same novels. Do not buy both thinking you missed a book.

**House path if you want both streets:** Grant County 1–6, then Will Trent from *Triptych*. That keeps Sara’s first life unspoiled and still lets you meet Will before the merge.

**House path if you came from the show:** *Triptych*, *Fractured*, then Grant County before *Undone*/*Genesis*. You can read the first two Will Trent novels without Grant County. The third is the line.

**Contested skip, labeled:** you *can* open *Undone* cold. You will pay a tax the Grant County six were designed to collect. This house does not recommend it.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Undone* or *Genesis* because Sara is on the later covers, or because a “complete Slaughter” list mixed the titles.
- Buying *Skin Privilege* and *Beyond Reach* as two novels.
- Starting with *This Is Why We Lied* because of the TV show’s later seasons.
- Treating *We Are All Guilty Here* as Will Trent 13.

## FAQ

**Do I need Grant County first?**  
Not before *Triptych*. Yes before *Undone*/*Genesis* if you do not want the earlier series spoiled.

**Which title is Will Trent book 3?**  
*Undone* in the US, *Genesis* in the UK. Same book.

**Which title is Grant County book 6?**  
*Beyond Reach* in the US, *Skin Privilege* in the UK. Same book.

**Is the TV show a substitute?**  
No. It is an adaptation that jumps.
