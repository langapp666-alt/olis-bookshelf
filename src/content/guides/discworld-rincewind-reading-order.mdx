If someone told you “start anywhere,” they are half right about the Disc and wrong about this coward. Rincewind’s books are a travel sequence. The Luggage accumulates. So does the joke about being qualified as a wizard.

*The Colour of Magic* and *The Light Fantastic* are a pair. Do not stop after the first tourist pamphlet and call it Discworld.

*Eric* is sometimes shelved as a story or an illustrated oddity. It is still the fourth Rincewind book, not optional homework you do before *Sourcery*.

*The Last Hero* is a Rincewind-and-Cohen book with pictures. It is the end of this line, not Watch #9 and not a coffee-table extra you skip because the type is large.

## Unseen Academicals is a different street

**Unseen Academicals** (2009) is Unseen University and football. Rincewind can appear. It is not Rincewind 8.

The Science of Discworld books are essays with a story wrapper. They are not this guide. Skip them as an on-ramp.

## Optional paths

- **Rincewind only.** *The Colour of Magic* through *The Last Hero*. This is the guide.
- **A different Discworld door** if early Rincewind feels like a parody that has not yet become a city. [Witches](/guides/discworld-witches-reading-order/), [Death](/guides/discworld-death-reading-order/), and [City Watch](/guides/discworld-city-watch-reading-order/) are the usual other first streets.
- **Full Discworld publication order** if you are collecting the whole Disc. That is a different project.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Interesting Times* because someone said the early books are “just pastiche.”
- Treating *The Last Hero* as a picture book you can ignore.
- Stuffing *Unseen Academicals* into the numbered Rincewind column.
- Opening a 41-book chart and postponing *The Colour of Magic* for a year.

## FAQ

**Is Rincewind a good Discworld starting point?**  
It is the historical one. It is not always the kindest one. Watch, Witches, and Death are the usual other doors.

**Do I need The Light Fantastic immediately?**  
Yes, if you started *The Colour of Magic*. They are a pair.

**What about Unseen Academicals?**  
Later, if you want University football. Not Rincewind 8.

**Whole Discworld?**  
Not on this page.
