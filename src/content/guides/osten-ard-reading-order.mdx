Memory, Sorrow, and Thorn is the original three. The Last King of Osten Ard is the sequel quartet, decades later in both story and print. The author bibliography lists two “prequels” under Last King. One is a bridge after the trilogy. One is deep past, published between sequel novels. Chronology charts that open on *Brothers of the Wind* are a reread map.

Otherland, Shadowmarch, and the Bobby Dollar books are different Williams shelves.

**Same book, two paperbacks:** the 1993 hardcover is one volume. US paperbacks split it into Part 1 and Part 2. UK paperbacks use *Siege* and *Storm*. Do not buy both sets. Do not treat the split as books 3 and 4.

*The Burning Man* (1998) is a novelette, first in the *Legends* anthology. Optional after the trilogy. Not book one.

The sequel was announced as a trilogy and published as four novels. Read them in this order. *The Witchwood Crown* assumes the original three are settled history.

## Brothers of the Wind — deep past, not the door

  author="Tad Williams"

**Contested slot, labeled:** the author files *Brothers of the Wind* as Last King prequel 2, which puts it next to *Heart* on series pages and tempts a chronology-first start. In print it arrived in 2021, after *Empire of Grass* and before *Into the Narrowdark*. **House rule:** do not open Osten Ard on it. Read it after Memory, Sorrow, and Thorn at the earliest. The publication slot (after *Empire of Grass*) is the default place if you want it inside the sequel run. Reading it just after *To Green Angel Tower* or after *Heart* is allowed. Reading it before *The Dragonbone Chair* is not.

**House rule for a first visit:** *The Dragonbone Chair* → *Stone of Farewell* → *To Green Angel Tower* → *The Heart of What Was Lost* → *The Witchwood Crown* → *Empire of Grass* → *Brothers of the Wind* → *Into the Narrowdark* → *The Navigator's Children*. Stop after *To Green Angel Tower* if you only wanted the original trilogy.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Brothers of the Wind* because a series page says prequel.
- Starting with *The Heart of What Was Lost* or *The Witchwood Crown* because they are the books in new paperback.
- Buying *Siege* and *Storm* and also Part 1 and Part 2, then treating four paperbacks as four novels.
- Mixing in *City of Golden Shadow* (Otherland) as Osten Ard book 4.

## FAQ

**Dragonbone Chair or Witchwood Crown first?**  
*The Dragonbone Chair*. *The Witchwood Crown* is the sequel quartet.

**Is The Heart of What Was Lost a prequel?**  
Only to *The Witchwood Crown*. Read it after *To Green Angel Tower*.

**Where does Brothers of the Wind go?**  
After the original trilogy. Default slot: after *Empire of Grass*. Not first.

**Are Siege and Storm extra books?**  
No. They are the UK paperback split of *To Green Angel Tower*.

**Is Otherland the same world?**  
No. *City of Golden Shadow* is a different series.
