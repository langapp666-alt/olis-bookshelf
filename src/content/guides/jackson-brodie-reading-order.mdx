Six novels. No prequel. No contested chronology. The trap is treating each Brodie book as a pure standalone because the cases change — the personal stream does not reset.

The BBC *Case Histories* adaptation covers early books and is not a substitute table of contents for the later novels.

Read them in this order. There is no chronological reorder that improves a first visit.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Big Sky* or *Death at the Sign of the Rook* because those covers are in the window.
- Using the TV series episode order as the novel list for books five and six.
- Mixing Atkinson’s standalones (*Life After Life*, *Shrines of Gaiety*) into the Brodie column.

## FAQ

**Can I start in the middle?**  
Atkinson allows it. This house still starts at *Case Histories*.

**Is there a prequel?**  
No.

**Do I need the TV series?**  
No. Cousin, not table of contents.

**Is Death at the Sign of the Rook book one?**  
No. Sixth.
