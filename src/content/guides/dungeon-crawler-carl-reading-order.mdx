Later volumes have been discussed as a finale split. They are not on this table until they are books you can hold. Do not wait for them before opening book one.

Bonus stories printed in some Ace editions are extras, not secret novels. *Operation Bounce House* is other Dinniman, not DCC 9.

## Common mistakes

- Starting mid-series because a clip was funny.
- Treating Ace editions as a reboot with a new book one.
- Mixing in other LitRPG lists as if they share a dungeon.

## FAQ

**Self-pub or Ace?**  
Same novels. Later printings may include extras.

**Is the series finished?**  
No. Eight numbered books are published.

**Can I skip to book 7?**  
No.
