**House rule:** read the three Jude novels. Insert *The Lost Sisters* if you have it. Read the Cardan extra after the trilogy. Then the Oak books if you want more Elfhame.

*The Darkest Part of the Forest* is a related standalone, not Folk of the Air book 0. Modern faerie Black books before this trilogy are other shelves.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *The Stolen Heir* because Oak is on the new hardcover.
- Starting with the Cardan extra because it is shorter.
- Treating the trilogy as optional preamble to the duology.
