Two finished six-book runs in one world. Mead’s site is blunt: Vampire Academy is finished; Bloodlines continues the same world with different narrators. Publication order across the universe is the path.

**House rule:** read Rose’s six. If you want Sydney and Adrian’s run, read Bloodlines after *Last Sacrifice*. Do not open *Bloodlines* because it says book one.

Mead has said she might return to the world someday and had no concrete plan when last checked. A later novel will not be Vampire Academy 0.

*Georgina Kincaid* is a different Mead series.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Bloodlines* because the spin-off is “more adult.”
- Starting with *Last Sacrifice* because it is the payoff.
- Using the film or the later television series as a table of contents.
- Treating the graphic novels as the reading order.

## FAQ

**Do I need Bloodlines?**  
No. Rose’s six end.

**Bloodlines first because Sydney is the new cover?**  
No.

**Is the academy series finished?**  
Yes. The spin-off is also finished.

**Movie or show first?**  
Cousins. Not a book order.
