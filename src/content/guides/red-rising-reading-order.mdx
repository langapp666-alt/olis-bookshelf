The published saga is six novels. Penguin’s series page lists them in one line: *Red Rising*, *Golden Son*, *Morning Star*, *Iron Gold*, *Dark Age*, *Light Bringer*. A seventh novel, *Red God*, is planned as the finale. It had no firm on-sale date when this page was last checked. Do not invent one.

## The original trilogy

Three books. One climb. Publication order is the only path.

If you only wanted a finished trilogy, you can stop after *Morning Star*. That is a complete story. It is not the whole saga Brown kept writing.

## The second saga

*Iron Gold* opens the wider war. Multiple cameras. A republic that has to live with the revolution. Read these after the first three, in order.

*Red God* is not a starting point, a companion, or a reason to wait before opening book one. The first six novels are already a sequence.

## Graphic extras

**Sons of Ares** is a graphic-novel prequel line about a different generation. It is optional texture. It is not Red Rising #0.5 homework, and it is a poor place to meet Darrow.

Keep picture-book recaps, RPG extras, and “complete universe” charts out of the numbered novel column.

## Common mistakes

- Starting at *Iron Gold* because the publisher copy said the story of a new world begins there. It does. It also assumes you lived through the old one.
- Treating the first trilogy as optional setup. It is the setup *and* a finished book.
- Waiting for *Red God* before starting. Six books are already on the shelf.
- Reading the comics first because they are shorter.
- Using the planned TV adaptation as a table of contents. It is not one yet, and it will not be a substitute.

## FAQ

**Can I stop after Morning Star?**  
Yes. You will have read a complete trilogy. You will not have read the saga Brown continued.

**Is Iron Gold a reboot?**  
No. It is book four.

**Do I need Sons of Ares?**  
No.

**When is Red God?**  
Not posted as a firm date when last checked. Do not wait for it before starting.

**Are they standalone?**  
No. Publication order, one through six.
