  It is book one (1970). Then the four Corwin sequels. The Merlin five come after *The Courts of Chaos*. Do not start on *Trumps of Doom*. Do not start mid-omnibus on a Merlin title.

Ten novels in two five-book cycles. Publication order is the only honest first path. The Corwin cycle is a complete visit. The Merlin cycle assumes that ending.

Short fiction and later shared-world Amber books by other writers are optional extras. They are not Amber 11 for a first Zelazny visit.

**House rule:** Corwin five first. Stop after *The Courts of Chaos* if you only wanted that argument. Merlin only if you want more Amber.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Trumps of Doom* because an omnibus spine lists Merlin first on the shelf you grabbed.
- Starting with *Sign of the Unicorn* because someone said the series “gets good there.”
- Treating every Zelazny novel as Amber continuity.
- Reading Merlin before Corwin because the later books feel more contemporary.

## FAQ

**Do I need Merlin?**  
No. Corwin closes.

**Can I start at Trumps of Doom?**  
No. It assumes the first five.

**Is there a chronological reorder?**  
Not one that helps a first reader. Publication order is the path.

**What about the short stories?**  
Optional. After the novels if you want them.
