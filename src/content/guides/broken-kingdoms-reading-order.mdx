Short version of the door: [Where to start Broken Kingdoms](/questions/where-to-start-broken-kingdoms/). This page is the nine.

Nine novels, four kingdoms, four couples. Andrews’s reading-order page lists them in one line. Publication order is the first-read path. Each new kingdom looks like a fresh series. It is not.

*The Ever Seas* is the next generation. Andrews has said you *can* start there. This house does not. *The Ever King* spends parents you have not met.

## Ever Seas — later generation

Andrews’s page lists *The Ever King*, *The Ever Queen*, *The Mist Thief*, *The Stolen Crown*, and later Ever Seas books after this set. They follow children of these couples.

**House rule:** read the nine. If you want the children, *The Ever King* after *Song of Sorrows and Fate*. Do not open *The Ever King* because TikTok said it was the better door.

*Broken Souls and Bones* and *Of Mischief and Mages* are different Andrews shelves.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Night of Masks and Knives* because the Cinderella pair is the one people talk about.
- Starting with *The Ever King* because the pirate cover is newer.
- Treating book four as Broken Kingdoms 1 of a spin-off you can read first.
- Mixing *Of Mischief and Mages* into the numbered column.

## What to read next

2. **[Lady of Darkness](/guides/lady-of-darkness-reading-order/) (Melissa K. Roehrich)** — If you want another completed dark-court shelf whose later extras look like book one. Start at *Lady of Darkness* (2021), not *The Reaper*.
3. **[Zodiac Academy](/guides/zodiac-academy-reading-order/) (Caroline Peckham and Susanne Valenti)** — If you want another long romantasy campus with neighboring series that pretend to be the door.
4. **[The Empyrean](/guides/empyrean-reading-order/) (Rebecca Yarros)** — If you want another unfinished-feeling romantasy with a strict first book.
5. **Stop after Crown of Blood and Ruin.** The first couple is a complete visit. The later kingdoms are optional appetite.

## FAQ

**Can I start at book four?**  
You can. You will spend three books standing on a war you never walked through. This house does not.

**Do I need Ever Seas?**  
No. This generation ends at *Song of Sorrows and Fate*.

**Does Andrews say Ever Seas first is fine?**  
Yes. This house still starts at *Curse of Shadows and Thorns*.
