A finished trilogy, a novella between the first two, and a later Texas spin-off. Publication order is the path.

Sanderson’s on-ramp page files Reckoners with his non-Cosmere work. *Skyward* is a different YA SF door. See the [Skyward guide](/guides/skyward-reading-order/). For Cosmere doors, use the [Cosmere starter](/guides/cosmere-starter-reading-order/).

**House rule:** *Steelheart*, insert *Mitosis* if you have it, *Firefight*, *Calamity*. *Lux* is a later team in the same Epic-haunted country. It arrived as audio first. It is not a fourth David novel, and it is not an on-ramp.

Penguin’s Reckoners series page still lists the original three as the spine. That is correct.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Lux* because it is a “new Reckoners.”
- Starting with *Calamity* because the title matches the orbiting object.
- Treating this as Cosmere homework.
- Stalling the trilogy on *Mitosis*.

## What to read next

2. **[Red Rising](/guides/red-rising-reading-order/) (Pierce Brown)** — If you want another climb out of a color-coded underclass, much longer.

5. **Stop after Calamity.** The three are a complete visit.

## FAQ

**Is Reckoners Cosmere?**  
No.

**Do I need Mitosis?**  
No.

**Is Lux book four?**  
It is a spin-off novel in the same world. After the trilogy if you want it.

**Steelheart or Skyward first?**  
Either door. They do not share homework.
