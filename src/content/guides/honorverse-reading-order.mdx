The Honorverse is several shelves that share a navy. First-time readers do not need the combined spreadsheet. They need Honor’s own fourteen, in order, until they know whether they want the rest of the war.

Weber has said publication order across the whole universe is safe. That is true and too much object for a first visit. This house gives you the Honor path, then labels the branches.

*Safehold* is a different Weber epic. It is not Honorverse 0.

## Saganami and Crown of Slaves — optional later

These are the two adult branches most readers mean by “the rest of the Honorverse.” They share the later war with Honor’s own books. They are not required before *On Basilisk Station*. They become useful around *War of Honor* / *At All Costs* if you want every fleet.

**House rule:** Honor novels first. If the wider war is the hook, add *Crown of Slaves* and *The Shadow of Saganami* after *War of Honor* (or after you finish Honor’s fourteen), then continue each branch in publication order. Do not start the Honorverse on Saganami because it says “book one.”

## Anthologies and YA — later still

Worlds of Honor anthologies (*More Than Honor*, *Worlds of Honor*, *Changer of Worlds*, *The Service of the Sword*, *In Fire Forged*, *Beginnings*, *What Price Victory?*) mix short Honorverse fiction. Some introduce names the later novels keep. They are not a hidden novel column. Optional after you already care.

## Common mistakes

- Opening a complete Honorverse spreadsheet and postponing *On Basilisk Station*.
- Starting with *A Beautiful Friendship* because it is earlier and shorter.
- Starting with *The Shadow of Saganami* or *Crown of Slaves* because they are labeled book one of a spin-off.
- Treating *Toll of Honor* as the continuation of *Uncompromising Honor*.
- Mixing *Safehold* into the Manticore column.

## FAQ

**Do I need Saganami and Crown of Slaves?**  
Not for a first Honor visit. They widen the later war. Start them after you like the main novels.

**Do I need the anthologies?**  
No. Optional texture. Some names show up again; the novels still carry Honor.

**Is the YA series book one?**  
No. Stephanie Harrington is centuries earlier. After you already like treecats, or not at all.

**Where does Toll of Honor go?**  
Later-published; earlier in Honor’s calendar. Not the first book, and not the required sequel to *Uncompromising Honor*.
