  It is the house door (US collection 2004; Ace trade soon after). Stross’s own TLDR also allows *The Rhesus Chart*, *The Nightmare Stacks*, or *Dead Lies Dreaming* as later safe starts. Do not start on *The Labyrinth Index* or a New Management middle book.

Lovecraftian bureaucracy in multiple sub-runs: early Bob Howard novels, a mid-series genre remix, then New Management novels after the Laundry itself is wrecked. Stross maintains a story-order chronology on his blog. Publication order of the main novels is enough for a first visit; his alternate doors are labeled below.

**Contested doors, labeled:** Stross lists *The Rhesus Chart* and *The Nightmare Stacks* as safe starts that do not require the early Bob books. **House rule:** *The Atrocity Archives* first if you asked for Laundry Files reading order. Use a later door only if you already know you want vampires (*Rhesus*) or the CASE NIGHTMARE stretch (*Stacks*).

*A Conventional Boy* is a short interstitial novel Stross also offers as a taster. Optional. Not a substitute for book one unless you only want a sample.

Marketing sometimes numbers New Management as “Laundry Files 10+.” Story-wise the agency is already gone. You can enter at *Dead Lies Dreaming* if that dystopia is what you want. You will not meet early Bob the same way.

Novellas (*Equoid*, *Escape from Yokai Land*, and others) sit between novels on Stross’s chronology. Optional. Do not start there.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Dead Lies Dreaming* because Amazon lists it as book 10.
- Starting with *The Labyrinth Index*.
- Treating every Stross novel (Merchant Princes, Acceleration) as Laundry continuity.
- Skipping *The Atrocity Archives* after someone said the series “gets good at Rhesus Chart” — allowed as a labeled door, not the default.

## FAQ

**Where does Stross say to start?**  
*The Atrocity Archives*, or later: *Rhesus Chart*, *Nightmare Stacks*, *Dead Lies Dreaming*, or the short *A Conventional Boy* as a taster.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No.

**Is Dead Lies Dreaming Laundry book one?**  
No. New Management door.

**Publication or story order?**  
Main novels in publication order for a first visit. Use Stross’s chronology page when you want shorts interleaved.
