Crescent City is readable as its own urban-fantasy case file. The complication is **crossover**. Bloomsbury notes that the Crescent City experience is richer if you already know ACOTAR. Later volumes go further than richness: they name other worlds.

See [ACOTAR](/guides/acotar-reading-order/) and [Throne of Glass](/guides/throne-of-glass-reading-order/).

Publication order is the only Crescent City order. There is no prequel collection hiding in front of book one.

**House rule:**

1. If Crescent City is your only Maas series, start here and keep going.
2. If you also want ACOTAR and TOG unspoiled, read those first (in either order relative to each other), then CC.
3. Do not start CC on book two because a clip said “that’s when it gets good.”

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *House of Sky and Breath*.
- Treating CC as ACOTAR book 6.
- Reading CC 2–3 before *Kingdom of Ash* and then being angry at a reading-order page.
- Waiting for a fourth CC novel before opening book one.

## FAQ

**Do I need ACOTAR first?**  
Not for book one. Yes, if you want later CC books not to step on ACOTAR.

**Do I need Throne of Glass first?**  
Same answer, especially before book two.

**Is the series finished?**  
Three novels are published. Do not treat that as a promise that the story is closed.

**Can I read CC before TOG?**  
Yes, if you accept crossover cost from book two on.
