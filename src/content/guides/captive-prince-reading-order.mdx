A finished trilogy. Publication order is the only path. The shorts are aftercare and side rooms. They are not a prequel cycle.

Early self-published parts were later issued as the three trade novels. Use the three titles below. Do not collect serial fragments as if they were extra volumes.

## Shorts — after the novels, or not at all

The collection is the stable object. It gathers *Green but for a Season*, *The Summer Palace*, *The Adventures of Charls, the Veretian Cloth Merchant*, and *Pet*. Some of those sit beside earlier novels in story time. That is why they are a later book.

**House rule:** read the three novels. If you want the shorts, read the collection after *Kings Rising*. Do not start on *Pet* because it is earlier in the court.

*Dark Rise* and *Dark Heir* are a separate trilogy. They are not Captive Prince 4–5.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with *Kings Rising* because it is the payoff.
- Starting with a short because it is free or short.
- Treating the old serial parts as extra novels.
- Mixing in *Dark Rise* as a sequel.

## FAQ

No.

No.

**Self-pub parts or the trade trilogy?**  
The three trade novels. The serial is the same story in pieces.
