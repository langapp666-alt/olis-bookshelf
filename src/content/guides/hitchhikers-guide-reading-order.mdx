Adams called it a trilogy in five parts. Publication order of the five is the first-read path. Other media — radio, TV, stage, film, text adventure — reshuffle jokes and deaths. They are not a table of contents for the novels.

Read them in this order. There is no honest “start at book four because Earth is back” door.

*Young Zaphod Plays It Safe* is a short. Optional. Not book 3.5. *The Salmon of Doubt* is a later unfinished-project collection, not Hitchhiker 6.

## The sixth — optional, labeled

**Contested extra, labeled:** Jane Belson sanctioned Colfer’s novel. Adams had talked about wanting a sixth that was less bleak than *Mostly Harmless*. Plenty of readers stop at five. That is allowed. This house lists the sixth as optional aftercare, not as required homework and not as a better book one.

If you want Colfer’s own long series, see [Artemis Fowl](/guides/artemis-fowl-reading-order/).

## Common mistakes

- Starting with the 2005 film and calling it the novel.
- Starting with the radio primary phase and skipping the books, then asking where *Mostly Harmless* goes.
- Starting with *And Another Thing…* because it is “the last one.”
- Treating *The Salmon of Doubt* as a finished sixth.

## FAQ

**Radio or novels first?**  
This page is the novels. The radio is earlier and different. Do not use it as a novel order.

**Do I need the Colfer book?**  
No.

**Is there a book six by Adams?**  
No finished one. *The Salmon of Doubt* is not that book.

**Movie order?**  
The film is a cousin of book one. It is not the series.
