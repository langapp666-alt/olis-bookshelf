## Why the new book looks standalone

The run is past sixty numbered novels. A late title can carry one case without announcing itself as book 58. The partnership and the squad still assume you have been here since *Naked in Death*. Anthology novellas (“… in Death”) sit between novels. They are not book one either.

J. D. Robb is Nora Roberts on this shelf. Other Roberts series are different doors.

## What this house does

*Naked in Death*, then the numbers. A starter table through *Witness in Death* is on the [In Death reading-order guide](/guides/in-death-reading-order/), with the rule for everything after: keep going in publication order. *Midnight in Death* is an optional novella between *Holiday in Death* and *Conspiracy in Death*. *Fury in Death* (2026) is the far end, not the start.

If you wanted a cop series with a shorter published list, [Harry Bosch](/questions/where-to-start-harry-bosch/) starts at *The Black Echo*.

## FAQ

**Can I skip to the latest In Death?**  
Not for a first read.

**Are the novellas required?**  
No.

**Is Remember When book one?**  
No. It is not an In Death number.
