## The 1968 novel

*A Wizard of Earthsea* (1968) is book one. *The Tombs of Atuan* (1971) is second. *The Farthest Shore* (1972) ends the original three. Later books assume that first naming. Publication order is the Earthsea order. The later novels change the conversation. They do not rewind it.

## What looks newer, and is not first

*Tehanu* (1990) is a later novel. Read it after the first three. *Tales from Earthsea* (2001) is stories, after *Tehanu* if you want them. *The Other Wind* (2001) closes the published novel run. None of those is an on-ramp.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Earthsea book 1?**  
*A Wizard of Earthsea* (1968), Ursula K. Le Guin. Not *Tehanu*. Not *The Other Wind*.

**Is Tehanu book one?**  
No. It is the fourth book, published years after the original three.

**Can I start with The Tombs of Atuan?**  
No. It is book two. It assumes the first naming.
