## Why the spin-off says book one

*Gunmetal Magic* is Andrea Nash’s novel. *Iron and Magic* is Hugh’s. *Blood Heir* is Julie’s. Each can wear a series number that belongs to that narrator. The world those books stand in still starts at *Magic Bites*. *Blood Heir* was published in 2021, before the Wilmington books, and the authors’ schedule still places it after *Magic Claims*.

## What this house does

*Magic Bites* first. The ten Kate novels are on the [Kate Daniels reading-order guide](/guides/kate-daniels-reading-order/). Where Andrea, Hugh, and Julie sit is on the [spin-offs guide](/guides/kate-daniels-spin-offs-reading-order/). None of the three spin-off novels is required to finish *Magic Triumphs*.

Hidden Legacy and Innkeeper are different series.

## FAQ

**After which book is Gunmetal Magic?**  
After *Magic Slays*, before *Magic Rises*.

**After which book is Blood Heir?**  
After *Magic Tides* and *Magic Claims*, not before them and not first.

**After which book is Iron and Magic?**  
After *Magic Binds*, before *Magic Triumphs*.
