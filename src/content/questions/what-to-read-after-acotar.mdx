## Stay on the Maas desk

**[Throne of Glass](/guides/throne-of-glass-reading-order/)** if you want the other long Maas fantasy before anyone mentions crossovers. Start with the novel *Throne of Glass*, not the prequel collection.

**[Crescent City](/guides/crescent-city-reading-order/)** after ACOTAR (and TOG, if you want the other half of the crossover). Book one can stand alone. Book two onward names other worlds. Do not use *House of Earth and Blood* as ACOTAR 6.

Announced later ACOTAR volumes go on the end of the [ACOTAR guide](/guides/acotar-reading-order/). They are sequels, not a new on-ramp, and not a reason to skip TOG.

## Leave Maas, keep the temperature

**[Empyrean](/guides/empyrean-reading-order/)** (*Fourth Wing*) if you want another unfinished romantasy with a strict first book. **From Blood and Ash** if you want a long romantasy that hides a prequel series as book one. **Folk of the Air** if you want faerie politics in a finished trilogy plus a later duology.

Those reasons live, with links, on the ACOTAR page’s what-to-read-next list. This page is the short version of that shelf.

## What not to do

Do not start *A Court of Silver Flames* as a “next series.” It is Nesta’s novel inside ACOTAR. Do not start a 20-title “Maas chronological order.” Those charts go stale.

## FAQ

**Is ACOTAR finished?**  
The published run through *Silver Flames* is a pause. More novels are announced. You can still leave the desk.

**Do I need Frost and Starlight before I move on?**  
No. It is a pause, not a secret war.
