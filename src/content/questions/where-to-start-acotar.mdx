## Book one is the 2015 novel

Publication order. *A Court of Mist and Fury* and *A Court of Wings and Ruin* assume that first bargain. *A Court of Frost and Starlight* is a novella pause after book three, not a secret war and not a new on-ramp. *A Court of Silver Flames* is Nesta’s novel. It is not a starting point.

Further ACOTAR novels have been announced. They are sequels. They are not a reason to wait, and they are not a reason to skip to *Silver Flames*.

## What this page is not

This is not [ACOTAR or Throne of Glass first?](/questions/acotar-or-throne-of-glass/). Either Maas door is allowed; they do not spoil each other. Start ACOTAR here, start TOG at the novel *Throne of Glass*, not *The Assassin’s Blade* — [Should I read Assassin’s Blade first?](/questions/assassins-blade-first/).

Crescent City later books assume you already know both endgames. Do not open *House of Earth and Blood* as ACOTAR 6. After the published run: [What to read after ACOTAR](/questions/what-to-read-after-acotar/).

## FAQ

**Where to start ACOTAR?**  
*A Court of Thorns and Roses* (2015). Then publication order.

**Can I start with Silver Flames?**  
No. It is Nesta’s novel inside the same series.

**Do I need Frost and Starlight?**  
No. It helps. Read it after book three if you have it.

**Do I need Throne of Glass first?**  
No.
