## The seven are the series

Publication order is the story order. Illustrated editions and adult covers are the same novels. *Sorcerer’s Stone* is not a different book one.

The films follow this spine more closely than most adaptations do. Film order is still not a reason to skip a book.

## What shops file next to book one

Hogwarts “textbooks,” Pottermore extras, Fantastic Beasts screenplays, and *Harry Potter and the Cursed Child* (a stage script) are optional. They are not novels 8–10. The Beedle tales are the least confusing extra after *Deathly Hallows*. Fantastic Beasts films are a prequel cousin; the published screenplays are scripts.

*Cursed Child* continues characters after book seven. It is widely treated as extra-canonical by readers who want the seven to end where they end. This page does not make you read it.

## FAQ

**Philosopher’s Stone or Sorcerer’s Stone?**  
Same book. Search either.

**Should I watch Fantastic Beasts before reading Harry?**  
No. The seven novels do not assume the films.

**Is Cursed Child required?**  
No.
