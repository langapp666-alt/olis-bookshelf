## The 1979 novel

*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* (1979) is book one of the novels. Then *The Restaurant at the End of the Universe*, *Life, the Universe and Everything*, *So Long, and Thanks for All the Fish*, and *Mostly Harmless*. Adams called it a trilogy in five parts. Publication order of those five is the first-read path.

## Other objects with the same name

The radio series is older and not identical. The film, the television series, the stage show, and the text adventure reshuffle jokes. They are not a table of contents for the novels. *And Another Thing…* (Eoin Colfer) is a later sixth. It is not book one, and it is not required to finish Adams's five.

## The five

The novel table, and the sixth labeled, live on the [Hitchhiker's Guide reading-order guide](/guides/hitchhikers-guide-reading-order/). This page only names book one.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Hitchhiker's Guide book 1?**  
*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* (1979), Douglas Adams. The novel. Not the film. Not *Mostly Harmless*.

**Should I start with the radio series?**  
You can listen. The novels still start at the 1979 book. The scripts are not the same text.

**Is And Another Thing book one?**  
No. It is a later continuation by another writer.
