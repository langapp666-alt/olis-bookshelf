## Nine novels. Publication order.

The war moves. So does the partnership. There is no chronological prequel hiding as a gentler door. Search *His Majesty’s Dragon* or *Temeraire*; English editions disagree on the first title, not on the first book.

## What is not this series

Novik’s later school trilogy is a different shelf. See [Scholomance](/guides/scholomance-reading-order/). *Uprooted* and *Spinning Silver* are standalones. They are not Temeraire 0.

## FAQ

**His Majesty’s Dragon or Temeraire?**  
Same first novel. UK printings often use *Temeraire*.

**Can I start at the end?**  
No. *League of Dragons* closes the run.

**Is this Scholomance?**  
No. Different shelf, same author.
