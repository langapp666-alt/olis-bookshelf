## Two streets, one spoiler line

Grant County is Sara Linton in Heartsdale: *Blindsighted* through *Beyond Reach* (UK: *Skin Privilege*). Will Trent is Atlanta GBI: *Triptych*, *Fractured*, then *Undone*.

The first two Will Trent novels do not require Grant County. The third does, if you care about the earlier ending. *Undone* is also sold as *Genesis*. Same book. Do not buy both.

The TV show is a cousin. It is not a reason to skip Grant County if you want Sara’s first life intact.

## If you want both

Grant County 1–6, then Will Trent from *Triptych*. That is the clean map.

If you came from the show: *Triptych* and *Fractured*, then the Grant County six before *Undone*/*Genesis*.

You *can* open *Undone* cold. You will pay a tax the earlier six were designed to collect.

Will Trent through *This Is Why We Lied*, Grant County, and the UK doubles live on the [Will Trent / Grant County reading-order guide](/guides/will-trent-reading-order/). *We Are All Guilty Here* is a different Slaughter door, not Will Trent 13. If you want the other long investigator with a UK title that is the same book, see [Jack Reacher](/guides/jack-reacher-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Do I need Grant County first?**  
Not before *Triptych*. Before *Undone*/*Genesis*, yes, if you do not want the spoiler.

**Skin Privilege or Beyond Reach?**  
Same Grant County novel. US: *Beyond Reach*. UK: *Skin Privilege*.

**Is the show a substitute?**  
No.
