## Trilogy, standalones, then the next generation

Abercrombie’s site groups the world the way the books were written: First Law trilogy, then three standalones, then *Sharp Ends*, then Age of Madness. Publication order is chronology for the novels that matter.

You can stop after *Last Argument of Kings* and have a complete story. You will not have the whole First Law world he kept writing. Age of Madness assumes you know what the standalones did to the map.

## What is not an on-ramp

*Best Served Cold*, *The Heroes*, and *Red Country* are standalone in the sense of a new main job. They are not standalone in the sense that they forget the trilogy. *A Little Hatred* is a sequel generation. *Sharp Ends* collects shorts from all over the timeline; that does not make it book 0.

*The Devils* (2025) is a new series. *The Shattered Sea* is a different trilogy. Leave both off this spine.

## FAQ

**Can I read Age of Madness first?**  
You can. You will be standing in a family argument you did not attend.

**Are the standalones required?**  
Required for the world as Abercrombie continued it. Not required if you only wanted the original trilogy.

**Is The Devils First Law?**  
No.
