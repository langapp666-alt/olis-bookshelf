## Trilogy first, then the wider war

Publication order is the only path: *Red Rising*, *Golden Son*, *Morning Star*, then *Iron Gold*, *Dark Age*, *Light Bringer*. You can stop after *Morning Star* and have a complete trilogy. You will not have the saga Brown kept writing.

*Red God* is a planned finale. It is not a reason to wait before opening book one. Six novels are already a sequence.

## What is not book one

*Iron Gold* opens the wider war. Multiple cameras. A republic that has to live with the revolution. It assumes you lived through the old one. **Sons of Ares** is a graphic-novel prequel line about a different generation. Optional texture. A poor place to meet Darrow.

Do not wait for a TV adaptation to become a table of contents. It is not one yet.

## FAQ

**Can I stop after Morning Star?**  
Yes. You will have read a complete trilogy. You will not have read the saga Brown continued.

**Is Iron Gold a reboot?**  
No. It is book four.

**Do I need Sons of Ares?**  
No.
