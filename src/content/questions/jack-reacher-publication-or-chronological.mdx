## Why “any order” is only half true

Most Reacher novels are a man arriving in a new town. That is why publishers say you can start anywhere. The exceptions are the point of a reading-order page.

Prequels published mid-run — *The Enemy*, *The Affair*, *Night School*, *The Secret* — jump backward. Chronological charts that begin with those titles are reread toys. They assume you already know the man who got off the bus.

A four-book chain (*61 Hours* through *Never Go Back*) follows one leftover job. Read those four in order even if you hop elsewhere later.

## Publication is how Child introduced him

Start with *Killing Floor* (1997). US book 4 is *Running Blind*; the UK title is *The Visitor*. Same book. Andrew Child co-authors from *The Sentinel* onward. That is a handoff, not a new book one.

The Prime series and the Tom Cruise films are cousins. Season order is not book order. *One Shot* is not an on-ramp because you saw a film.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
For a first pass, yes. For a reread, hop if you want — except the four-book chain, and except using prequels as doors.

**When did Andrew Child take over?**  
Co-author from *The Sentinel* (2020). Still the same numbered run.
