## The inhibitor sequence first

Reynolds says order is not critical, then immediately names *Revelation Space*, *Redemption Ark*, and *Absolution Gap* as the sequence that improves if you keep it. *Inhibitor Phase* continues that argument. That is the door.

Publication order across the whole universe is a valid history. It is not the kindest first read.

## What is not this door

*Chasm City* is a related novel that looks like a start. *The Prefect* (and later Prefect books) are a different object in the same universe. They are not inhibitor book one. Do not postpone *Revelation Space* until you own a timeline chart.

The inhibitor table, the Chasm City / Prefect flags, and the timeline note live on the [Revelation Space reading-order guide](/guides/revelation-space-reading-order/). If you want another solar-system machine war after this, [where to start The Expanse](/questions/where-to-start-the-expanse/).

## FAQ

**Can I start with Chasm City?**  
You can. You will be in the universe without the inhibitor sequence’s first argument.

**Are the Prefect books required?**  
Not for the inhibitor novels.

**Does Reynolds want chronological order?**  
He publishes a timeline. First read: *Revelation Space* onward in that sequence.
