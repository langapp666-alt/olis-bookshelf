## Start at book one, on purpose

There is no honest “start at book four because the prologue is long.” The prologue is a door. The first book is still the first book. Robert Jordan wrote 1–11. Brandon Sanderson finished 12–14 from Jordan’s notes. Those three are Wheel of Time. They are not a separate series you can detach and read first.

The series is complete. You do not wait for a shorter on-ramp. This is a long series on purpose.

## How to pace it without a syllabus

Read in publication order. Take breaks between books if you need them; do not “fix” the middle by skipping to the Sanderson jackets. Companion encyclopedias leak. The television series is a cousin that compresses early books. Season order is not book order.

If you want the prequel at all, put *New Spring* after *Lord of Chaos*, or after book 14. Reading it first hands you names and outcomes the opening novel is designed to discover.

## FAQ

**Can I start with New Spring?**  
You can. You will spend book one watching discoveries you already paid for. We do not recommend it.

**Are the last three books required?**  
Yes, if you want the ending Jordan planned.

**Is the TV show a substitute for books 1–3?**  
No.
