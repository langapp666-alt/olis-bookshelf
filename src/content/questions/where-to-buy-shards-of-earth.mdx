## One finished three

*Shards of Earth* (2021), *Eyes of the Void* (2022), *Lords of Uncreation* (2023). Publication order is the only path. There is no prequel and no contested start.

## Neighboring shelves

*Children of Time* is a different finished Tchaikovsky trilogy. A shop that files both under one author name is not a reading order. That map is [Children of Time](/guides/children-of-time-reading-order/). [Shadows of the Apt](/guides/shadows-of-the-apt-reading-order/) is the ten-book fantasy series — *Empire in Black and Gold* first. The fork between Apt and *Children of Time* is [Apt or Children of Time?](/questions/shadows-of-the-apt-or-children-of-time/).

## The trilogy table

The three novels live on the [Final Architecture reading-order guide](/guides/final-architecture-reading-order/). This page only names book one.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Final Architecture book 1?**  
*Shards of Earth* (2021), Adrian Tchaikovsky. Not *Lords of Uncreation*. Not *Children of Time*.

**Is Children of Time the prequel?**  
No. Different series.

**Can I start with Lords of Uncreation?**  
No. It closes the trilogy.
