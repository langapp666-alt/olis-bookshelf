## Hodges starts at Mr. Mercedes

*Mr. Mercedes* (2014), then *Finders Keepers* (2015), then *End of Watch* (2016). That is the trilogy. Publication order is the only path. *Finders Keepers* can look like a standalone about a manuscript. It is still book two.

Holly Gibney walks into *Mr. Mercedes* as supporting cast. The novel titled *Holly* (2023) is her name on a later spine. It is not the first book she is in.

## If you came for Holly

The trilogy is still the origin. The longer path — *The Outsider*, *If It Bleeds*, *Holly*, *Never Flinch* — is the [Holly Gibney reading order](/guides/holly-gibney-reading-order/). A first King that is not a series is the [starter path](/guides/stephen-king-starter-path/).

## The three

The Hodges novels, and the note about what comes after, live on the [Bill Hodges reading-order guide](/guides/bill-hodges-reading-order/). This page only names book one.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Bill Hodges book 1?**  
*Mr. Mercedes* (2014), Stephen King. Not *Holly*. Not *End of Watch*.

**Is the show a substitute?**  
No. The Audience Network / Peacock adaptation is a cousin. Start the books at *Mr. Mercedes*.

**Can I skip to Holly?**  
You will miss how the partnership started. This house starts at *Mr. Mercedes*.
