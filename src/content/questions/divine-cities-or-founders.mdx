## Why the shelves get merged

Both are finished Bennett trilogies, and a third series followed. An author sort on a shop page looks like one long run: *City of Miracles* into *Foundryside* into *The Tainted Cup*. They are three doors.

## What this house does

Name the series, then open its book one. Divine Cities: *City of Stairs*, *City of Blades*, *City of Miracles*. Founders: *Foundryside*, *Shorefall*, *Locklands*. Shadow of the Leviathan: *The Tainted Cup*, then *A Drop of Corruption*, then *A Trade of Blood*. Full tables on the [Divine Cities and Founders guide](/guides/divine-cities-founders-reading-order/).

If you wanted a single clear book one on a different fantasy shelf, [Osten Ard](/questions/where-to-start-osten-ard/) starts at *The Dragonbone Chair*.

## FAQ

**Do I need City of Stairs before Foundryside?**  
No.

**Is The Tainted Cup Founders book 4?**  
No.

**Are the early Bennett novels required?**  
No. They are standalones.
