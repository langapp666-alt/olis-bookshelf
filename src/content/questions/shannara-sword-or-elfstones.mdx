## Why the second book has a lobby

*The Sword of Shannara* opened the Four Lands in 1977. *The Elfstones of Shannara* (1982) is the book a lot of later readers prefer as a first taste. Brooks has also published suggested new-reader paths that do not begin on *Sword*. None of that makes *Armageddon's Children* or a Fall of Shannara finale into book one.

## What this house does

Publication through the original trilogy first. *Elfstones* first is allowed if that is the door you meant — then *Wishsong*, and only circle back to *Sword* if you care. Heritage and later cycles wait until you know you want more. Full table on the [Shannara reading-order guide](/guides/shannara-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Is Sword skippable?**  
Some readers skip it. This house still lists it as the default first title.

**Where does First King of Shannara go?**  
After the original three if you want the prequel. Not an on-ramp.
