## The novels start here

*Empire of Silence* (2018) is book one. The later novels are the same confession, in publication order, through *Shadows Upon Time* (2025). There is no second door inside the seven.

Ruocchio has published a suggested intercalary order for novellas and story collections. Those extras are optional. Do not postpone *Empire of Silence* until you own every *Tales of the Sun Eater* volume.

*The Murdered Sun* has been described as a non-canonical early version of book one. It is not a prequel and not an on-ramp.

## The seven

The novel table, and the novellas labeled optional, live on the [Sun Eater reading-order guide](/guides/sun-eater-reading-order/). This page only names book one.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Sun Eater book 1?**  
*Empire of Silence* (2018), Christopher Ruocchio. Not a novella. Not *Shadows Upon Time*.

**Do I need the novellas first?**  
No. Read the novels. Insert a novella later if you have it.

**Is The Murdered Sun book one?**  
No. It is not the published on-ramp.
