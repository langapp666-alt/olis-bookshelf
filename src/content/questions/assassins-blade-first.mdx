## Publication path (house first read)

1. *Throne of Glass*
2. *Crown of Midnight*
3. *The Assassin’s Blade*
4. Then *Heir of Fire* through *Kingdom of Ash*

Meet the novel, then the earlier jobs, then the rest. That is the publisher order this house follows.

## What “Assassin’s Blade first” actually does

The collection is earlier in Celaena’s calendar and later in the publishing history. Reading it first hands you jobs, names, and a temperature the opening novel is designed to discover. Some readers still want that. Bloomsbury says it is allowed. It is a valid reread or a stubborn chronological taste. It is not the path pictured for a first visit.

Do not start with *Kingdom of Ash*. Do not skip to *Queen of Shadows* because later books are a different temperature. That change is designed.

## The other TOG trap

*Tower of Dawn* runs beside *Empire of Storms*. Reading ToD first spoils parts of EoS. Publication order (EoS then ToD) is enough. Tandem charts are a reread toy. Details, the tandem note, and the Crescent City warning live on the [Throne of Glass reading-order guide](/guides/throne-of-glass-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Assassin’s Blade first or third?**  
Third for a first read. First if you already know you want chronology and will accept the cost.

**Do I have to tandem-read Empire of Storms and Tower of Dawn?**  
No.

**Is the series complete?**  
Yes.
