## Publication first, chronology labeled

The first-read path is three titles: *The Priory of the Orange Tree* (2019), then *A Day of Fallen Night* (2023), then *Among the Burning Flowers* (2025). Shannon’s cycle page is explicit: *Priory* is third in chronological order. *A Day of Fallen Night* is a full-length prequel five centuries earlier. *Among the Burning Flowers* is a shorter direct prequel to *Priory*, and she names it as a possible start if you want a faster read.

This house still treats *Priory* as the first visit. Shannon built later volumes to work as their own visits. That does not make the long prequel book one.

## What chronological charts do

They put *Fallen Night*, then *Burning Flowers*, then *Priory*. Coherent on a reread. For a first read, *Priory* is how the cycle was met. If you bounce off the length of *Fallen Night* as a door, that is information.

## FAQ

**What is the Roots of Chaos reading order?**  
Start with *The Priory of the Orange Tree* (2019), then *A Day of Fallen Night* (2023), then *Among the Burning Flowers* (2025). Publication first. Chronology is a later visit.

**Where to start Roots of Chaos / Priory of the Orange Tree?**  
*The Priory of the Orange Tree* (2019). It is the first published novel in the cycle.

**Can I start with A Day of Fallen Night?**  
Shannon allows more than one door. This house starts with *Priory*. Chronology is a later visit.

**Is Among the Burning Flowers book one?**  
It is a shorter prequel to *Priory*. Possible later. Not the cycle’s first published novel.

**Publication or chronological?**  
Publication first. Chronology — *Fallen Night*, *Burning Flowers*, *Priory* — is a later visit.
