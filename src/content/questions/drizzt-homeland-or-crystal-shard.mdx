## Two doors, not one wrong answer

*The Crystal Shard* introduced Drizzt to readers in 1988 with the Icewind Dale companions already on stage. *Homeland*, *Exile*, and *Sojourn* were written afterward and tell his earlier life in Menzoberranzan and on the road to the surface. Omnibus spines and “Legend of Drizzt” numbering usually put *Homeland* first.

Neither path invents a third on-ramp in the middle of Hunter’s Blades or Neverwinter.

## What this house does

Publication first: Icewind Dale three, then Dark Elf origin, then *The Legacy* onward. Chronology first: Dark Elf three, then Icewind Dale, then the same onward list. Details and the post-six handoff live on the [Drizzt reading-order guide](/guides/drizzt-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Is Homeland a prequel that spoils Crystal Shard?**  
It fills in origin. It does not replace Icewind Dale. Either order is used widely.

**Can I skip the Dark Elf trilogy?**  
Yes, if you only want the companions’ first adventure. You will meet Drizzt without his full origin.

**Where do Sellswords fit?**  
Optional side path later. Not book one.
