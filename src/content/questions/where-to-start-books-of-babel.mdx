## One climb, four novels

Publication order is the story order: *Senlin Ascends*, *Arm of the Sphinx*, *The Hod King*, *The Fall of Babel*. The first book is deliberate. Senlin is a headmaster with a guidebook. The Tower trains him out of that. Starting at book two hands you a crew and a problem you have not earned.

Some readers bounce. That is a taste problem, not a reading-order problem. The series does not have a gentler door.

## What the Orbit reprints are not

The 2018 Orbit editions of the first two books are reprints, not a reboot. Same climb. Same book one. Searching “Babel” and picking up R. F. Kuang’s *Babel* is a different tower.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
Yes. It is one climb.

**Is Senlin Ascends optional if I already know the premise?**  
No. The premise is not the book.

**Which edition should I buy?**  
Whichever printing your shop has. Orbit 2018 is the widely available first volume, not a rewrite.

**Is there a book five?**  
Not as part of this tetralogy. *The Fall of Babel* is the last novel on the author’s series page.

**Is this related to R. F. Kuang’s Babel?**  
No.
