## Why the new paperbacks look like book one

*The Heart of What Was Lost* and *Brothers of the Wind* are filed as prequels to The Last King of Osten Ard. That label means “before *The Witchwood Crown*.” It does not mean “before *The Dragonbone Chair*.” *Heart* is the bridge after *To Green Angel Tower*. *Brothers of the Wind* is deep past, published in 2021 between sequel novels.

## What this house does

*The Dragonbone Chair* → *Stone of Farewell* → *To Green Angel Tower* → *Heart* → the Last King novels, with *Brothers of the Wind* after *Empire of Grass* if you want it. Paperback *Siege* / *Storm* and Part 1 / Part 2 are the same third novel. Full tables on the [Osten Ard reading-order guide](/guides/osten-ard-reading-order/).

For another classic shelf where companion novels spoil if read first, see [Start the Belgariad with Belgarath?](/questions/belgariad-belgarath-first/).

## FAQ

**Can I start with Brothers of the Wind?**  
No. After the original trilogy at the earliest.

**Is Heart a prequel to Dragonbone Chair?**  
No. Read it after *To Green Angel Tower*.

**Witchwood Crown first?**  
Only if you have already finished Memory, Sorrow, and Thorn. It is not the door.
