## Publication, not “Book One” on the spine

Polish magazine order, Polish book order, and English order are three histories. English readers got *The Last Wish* first, jumped to *Blood of Elves*, and only later received *Sword of Destiny*. **The first-read path is the two collections, then the saga.** Lists that stamp *Blood of Elves* “Book 1” because it is the first novel are selling form, not a door.

Games and Netflix rearrange. They are cousins. They are not a table of contents. If you watched first, still start with *The Last Wish*.

## What is not an on-ramp

*Blood of Elves* is saga novel one. It expects Ciri and the shorts. *Season of Storms* sits earlier in Geralt’s calendar and later in publishing history; it also knows the saga exists. *Crossroads of Ravens* is a young-Geralt prequel published last. **House rule:** ninth, or not at all, on a first pass.

Do not skip *Sword of Destiny* because the English paperback arrived late.

## FAQ

**Can I skip the short stories?**  
You can. The novels will assume you did not.

**Is Blood of Elves book one?**  
It is saga novel one. It is not the start of the Witcher books.

**Should I read Crossroads of Ravens first?**  
No.
