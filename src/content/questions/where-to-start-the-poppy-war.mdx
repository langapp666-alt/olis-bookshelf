## A finished trilogy. Publication order.

*The Poppy War*, *The Dragon Republic*, *The Burning God*. Book two and book three assume you already know what book one cost. There is no chronological reshuffle.

*The Drowning Faith* is optional connective tissue around the end of the run. It is not a fourth novel and not an on-ramp.

## What is not this series

*Babel* and *Yellowface* are other Kuang shelves. They are not Poppy War 4 and 5. A shop that piles *Yellowface* next to *The Burning God* is doing retail gravity.
