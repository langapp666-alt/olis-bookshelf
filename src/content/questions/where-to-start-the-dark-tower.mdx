## The seven, then Keyhole

Publication order is the Tower order: *The Gunslinger* through *The Dark Tower*. There is no honest “start at book five because the movie did.” Either Gunslinger text is book one; new printings are usually the 2003 revision.

*The Wind Through the Keyhole* (2012) sits between *Wizard and Glass* and *Wolves of the Calla*. For a first read, insert it after book 4 if you already have it, or read the seven and then *Keyhole*. Do not start there. Do not postpone Roland until you own it.

## Related King books are weather

Charts that require *The Stand*, *’Salem’s Lot*, *Insomnia*, and the rest before Roland are doing spreadsheet energy in Maine. Those books reward a later reread. They are not Dark Tower 0.

## FAQ

**Revised Gunslinger or original?**  
Either is book one. New copies are usually the 2003 revision.

**Do I need The Stand first?**  
No.

**Where does Keyhole go?**  
Between 4 and 5, or after the seven. Not first.
