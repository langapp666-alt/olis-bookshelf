## Why Ember is not book one

*A Shadow in the Ember* is Flesh and Fire 1. It is not From Blood and Ash 0. The author’s FAQ says the two series are meant to be read together in release order, because the prequels explain things the Poppy books are still using as mystery.

Official first insert: after *The Crown of Gilded Bones* (FBAA 3), before *The War of Two Queens*. Then the later Ember novels continue to alternate with the later Poppy novels.

## What “just read the prequels first” costs

Chronology puts Sera’s four before Poppy. That is a complete Sera visit. It is also how people arrive at *From Blood and Ash* already holding answers the opening romantasy was designed to withhold. Armentrout calls Flesh and Fire a companion prequel, not a fully separate path.

*Visions of Flesh and Blood* is a compendium. Optional. Not before *A Fire in the Flesh*. *A Soul of Ash and Blood* is a Poppy-side look backward. Not a new door.

## The two maps

The four Ember novels and the official weave live on the [Flesh and Fire reading-order guide](/guides/flesh-and-fire-reading-order/). Poppy’s six, without the interleave, live on [From Blood and Ash](/guides/from-blood-and-ash-reading-order/). Start at FBAA either way.

## FAQ

**Flesh and Fire first?**  
No.

**Where does A Shadow in the Ember go?**  
After *The Crown of Gilded Bones* on the author’s list.

**Can I read only Poppy?**  
Yes. Use the FBAA guide. You will miss Sera’s age. You will not be reading the wrong book one.
