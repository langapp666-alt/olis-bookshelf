## The canon is nine books

Four novels and fifty-six shorts in five collections. Publication order of those nine is the first-read path: *A Study in Scarlet*, *The Sign of the Four*, *Adventures*, *Memoirs*, then *Hound*, then the return.

*Hound* and *The Valley of Fear* are set earlier than their publication dates. They were still issued to people who already knew Holmes. Chronology is a later hobby, not a first-read tool.

If you only want one novel after the meeting, *Hound* is the usual second visit — after you already know who Holmes is.

## What is not the door

Pastiches, BBC *Sherlock*, and “complete Sherlock” omnibuses that lead with a modern novel are other shelves. Doyle’s *Case-Book* is the last canon collection. You do not owe a pastiche.

A first visit can be *Study*, *Adventures*, and *Hound*. That is an introduction, not a claim that the other six books are fake.

## FAQ

**Is Hound a prequel?**  
It is set earlier than its publication. Read it fifth, or after you already know Holmes. Do not read it first.

**Do I have to read every short?**  
For “the canon,” eventually. For a first visit, no.
