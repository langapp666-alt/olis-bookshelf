## Three usual first streets

**[City Watch](/guides/discworld-city-watch-reading-order/)** — *Guards! Guards!* through *Snuff*. A true sequence. This house often starts people here because the Watch is a character ladder, not a pile of standalones.

**[Witches](/guides/discworld-witches-reading-order/)** — *Equal Rites* (some readers prefer *Wyrd Sisters* as a friendlier Granny door). Tiffany sits on that page later, not as book one.

**[Death](/guides/discworld-death-reading-order/)** — *Mort*. Short, complete, and not *Hogfather* first because of the TV film.

You do not need the other streets before you open one.

## What “start at book one” means here

*The Colour of Magic* is the first Discworld novel and the first [Rincewind](/guides/discworld-rincewind-reading-order/) book. You can start there. Early Rincewind is a different temperature from the later city. If it feels like a parody that has not yet become Ankh-Morpork, switch streets; you have not failed the Disc.

[Moist von Lipwig](/guides/discworld-moist-reading-order/) (*Going Postal*) is a later industrial door, cleaner for some modern readers, not a hidden book one.

Full Discworld publication order is a collecting project. Use the publisher list for the 41-book shelf. Do not postpone *Guards! Guards!* until you own a spreadsheet.

## FAQ

**Can Discworld be read in any order?**  
Half true of the Disc. Wrong inside a character line. Watch, Witches, Death, Rincewind, and Moist are sequences.

**Do I need Theatre of Cruelty or the picture book?**  
No. Those notes live on the Watch page.

**Is Night Watch a good first Discworld novel?**  
It is a later Watch book. People call it the best. Start with *Guards! Guards!*
