## Atlanta starts at Magic Bites

*Magic Bites* (2007) is the first Kate Daniels novel. The later Atlanta books assume that job. Ten novels run through *Magic Triumphs* (2018). *Magic Tides* and *Magic Claims* are the Wilmington Years. They spend the tenth book's ending. They are not an easier book one.

The authors have joked that you could start at *Magic Strikes*. That note is about their early books. *Magic Strikes* is still third.

## Spin-offs wear other names

*Gunmetal Magic*, *Iron and Magic*, and *Blood Heir* are Andrea, Hugh, and Julie. None of them is Kate's first novel. The fork is [Gunmetal Magic or Magic Bites?](/questions/gunmetal-magic-or-magic-bites/).

## The full Atlanta table

The ten, plus Wilmington labeled, live on the [Kate Daniels reading-order guide](/guides/kate-daniels-reading-order/). This page only names book one.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Kate Daniels book 1?**  
*Magic Bites* (2007), Ilona Andrews. Not *Magic Tides*. Not *Gunmetal Magic*.

**Can I start at Magic Strikes?**  
The authors have joked about it. This house still starts at *Magic Bites*. *Magic Strikes* is book three.

**Is Magic Tides book one?**  
No. It follows *Magic Triumphs*.
