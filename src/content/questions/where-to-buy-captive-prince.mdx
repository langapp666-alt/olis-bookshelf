## The trade novel is book one

*Captive Prince* is the first of three. The trade edition is dated 2014. Earlier serial parts were gathered into that novel. Do not collect those fragments as extra volumes. *Prince's Gambit* (2014) is book two. *Kings Rising* (2016) closes the trilogy.

## What is not this book

A short in *The Summer Palace and Other Stories* is aftercare. It is not an on-ramp. *Dark Rise* is a different Pacat trilogy.

## The full trilogy

The three novels, and the shorts labeled so they stay shorts, live on the [Captive Prince reading-order guide](/guides/captive-prince-reading-order/). This page only names book one.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Captive Prince book 1?**  
*Captive Prince*, C. S. Pacat. Trade year 2014. Not *Kings Rising*. Not a short.

**Can I start with Kings Rising?**  
No. It closes the trilogy.

**Is Dark Rise the same series?**  
No. Different trilogy.
