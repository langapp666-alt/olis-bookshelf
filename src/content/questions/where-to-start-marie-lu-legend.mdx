## Marie Lu’s Republic, not Gemmell’s Drenai

This page is Lu’s YA trilogy-plus-one. Gemmell’s *Legend* is a different book by a different author. If that is the search you meant, this shop does not have a Drenai map.

Publication order for Lu: *Legend*, *Prodigy*, *Champion*, then *Rebel* if you want the later generation.

## What chronological charts get wrong

*Life Before Legend* is a prequel novella. It assumes you already know the wanted posters. **House rule:** the three, then *Rebel*. Insert the novella after the three if you have it. *Life After Legend* pieces are short epilogue extras, not novels.

*Warcross* and *The Young Elites* are different Lu shelves.

## FAQ

**Is this David Gemmell?**  
No. Marie Lu. Different book.

**Can I start with Rebel?**  
No. It is book four, a decade later.

**Do I need Life Before Legend?**  
No. It is origin texture after you already know the Republic.
