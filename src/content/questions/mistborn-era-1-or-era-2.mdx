## Era 1 is the Mistborn door

Three books: *The Final Empire*, *The Well of Ascension*, *The Hero of Ages*. There is no contested start inside Era 1. Same first novel whether the cover says *Mistborn* or *The Final Empire*.

Era 2 (Wax and Wayne) is the same planet a few centuries later: *The Alloy of Law* through *The Lost Metal*. Shorter, more case-shaped. It still assumes Era 1’s history. Starting Era 2 to “skip the slog” skips the ending Era 2 keeps talking around.

This is not the whole Cosmere. For “which planet first?” use [Where to start the Cosmere](/questions/where-to-start-the-cosmere/). Stormlight is a different door: [Where to start Stormlight](/questions/where-to-start-stormlight-archive/).

## Secret History is not book 1.5

*Mistborn: Secret History* spoils Era 1’s ending and leaks a little Era 2. **House first read:** finish Era 1, read Era 2 through *The Bands of Mourning*, then *Secret History*, then *The Lost Metal*. The older habit of reading it right after *Hero of Ages* is allowed if you accept a few names leaking backward. Do not read it before Era 1 ends. *Arcanum Unbounded* is a collection, not Mistborn book 4.

## FAQ

**Can I start with Era 2?**  
Sanderson allows it as a Cosmere jump. For *Mistborn*, start with Era 1. Alloy spoils the first trilogy’s outcome.

**Do I need Secret History?**  
No. Era 2 is readable without it. Era 2 is richer if you have it before *The Lost Metal*.

**Is this the whole Cosmere?**  
No. This page stops at Mistborn Eras 1 and 2 on purpose.
