## Why arc-first exists

Each Foreigner trilogy is sold as a semi-encapsulated political problem. Cherryh’s universe page says you may enjoy tracking down *Foreigner* and starting at the beginning, and that arc-first is safe. That is permission, not a reason to open *Defiance* cold.

The slow colonial opening inside *Foreigner* is part of book one, not a separate prequel you must buy first.

## What this house does

*Foreigner* → *Invader* → *Inheritor*, then onward through the arcs in publication order. Full arc tables on the [Foreigner reading-order guide](/guides/foreigner-reading-order/). Stop after the first arc if you only wanted a sample.
