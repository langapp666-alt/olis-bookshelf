## Nine novels. That is the series.

*Leviathan Wakes* through *Leviathan Falls*. Publication order. The show is a cousin; characters and late-plot choices diverge. It is not a license to skip book one.

If you only do one order, do the nine. The series is finished.

## Novellas are tissue, not doors

Several shorts take place *before* or *alongside* early novels. That does not make them on-ramps. *The Churn* is Amos-beforehand. *Drive* is historically early and a poor first impression of the voice. *Memory’s Legion* collects the shorts; use it after *Leviathan Falls*, or as cleanup. Do not use it as book one.

Insert novellas after the novel they followed into bookstores, or save them all for the collection. Reordering them to “when they happen” is how people get spoiled.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read the novellas?**  
No. They add character history and a few bridges. The nine novels close the series.

**Where does Memory’s Legion go?**  
After *Leviathan Falls*, unless you are interleaving shorts as they were published.

**Is the show a substitute?**  
No.
