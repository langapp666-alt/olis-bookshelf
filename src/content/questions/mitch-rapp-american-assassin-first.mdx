## Why the film is the wrong door

The 2017 movie begins at recruitment. So do a lot of “complete Rapp” widgets, and so does the estate site’s chronology: *American Assassin*, *Kill Shot*, *Denied Access*, then *Transfer of Power*. That path is a reread map. It is not how Flynn invented the job.

*Transfer of Power* (1999) is the first published Mitch Rapp novel. Ten Flynn books, then the two young-Rapp prequels, then *The Last Man*. Kyle Mills and Don Bentley are later handoffs, not new book ones.

## What the prequels are for

*American Assassin* and *Kill Shot* were published eleventh and twelfth. *Denied Access* (2025) continues that origin stretch. *Double Tap* (2026) is another young-Rapp volume. None of them are Mitch Rapp 1 for a first-time reader.

*Term Limits* is Flynn’s debut. Rapp is not the protagonist. It is not a secret book one either.

## FAQ

**American Assassin first?**  
Not for a first read. After you already know Rapp, or not at all until the numbered list gets there.

**Do I need Term Limits?**  
No.

**When did the continuations start?**  
Mills at *The Survivor* (2015). Bentley at *Capture or Kill* (2024). Still the same run.
