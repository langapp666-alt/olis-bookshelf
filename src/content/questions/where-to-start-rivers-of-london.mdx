## The Peter Grant novels are the door

Publication order for the numbered novels, from *Rivers of London* through *Stone and Sky*. *Midnight Riot* is the US title of book one, not a different series.

Do not start with a novella. Do not start with the comics. The series is still growing; extras confuse first-timers more than the novels do.

## Chronology is a map, not a syllabus

Aaronovitch’s chronology page interleaves novels, novellas, shorts, and comics. Useful for *when stories sit*. Wrong as book one through thirty-eight.

*The Masquerades of Spring* is a 1920s jazz-age Nightingale side story. It will not teach you the Folly as a modern police job. *The October Man* is Tobias Winter in Germany — a different desk. *Tales from the Folly* is a cleanup volume after you already like the voice. Comics are optional; starting with *Body Work* starts in the middle of Peter’s job.

## FAQ

**Is Midnight Riot a different series?**  
No. Same first novel, US title.

**Should I start with the Nightingale prequel?**  
No. *The Masquerades of Spring* is earlier in his life and later on the shelf.

**Do I need the comics?**  
No. A first-time reader who only does the novels will not be lost.
