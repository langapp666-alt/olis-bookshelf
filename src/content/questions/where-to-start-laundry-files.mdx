## One series, several on-ramps

Stross’s own chronology page gives a TLDR: *The Atrocity Archives*, *The Rhesus Chart*, *The Nightmare Stacks*, or *Dead Lies Dreaming* (plus *A Conventional Boy* as a short taster). Early books are Len Deighton–flavored Laundry fieldwork. Mid books remix other fantasy subgenres. New Management novels begin after the agency is already broken.

Retail “book 10” labels on *Dead Lies Dreaming* are marketing, not a first-visit instruction.

## What this house does

*The Atrocity Archives* first. Use a later Stross-approved door only if you already know you want that stretch. Novel tables and New Management labeling live on the [Laundry Files reading-order guide](/guides/laundry-files-reading-order/). If bureaucratic magic in a different city is what you meant, see [Rivers of London](/guides/rivers-of-london-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No.

**Is Dead Lies Dreaming book one?**  
No. New Management door.

**Rhesus Chart without the early books?**  
Stross allows it. You will meet a later Bob.
