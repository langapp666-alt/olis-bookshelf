## Publication path for a first read

*The Hobbit*, then *The Fellowship of the Ring*, *The Two Towers*, *The Return of the King*. Some printings bind the three LotR volumes as one book. That is still the same novel. Do not start in the middle.

You can start at *Fellowship*. You will miss the tone it is in conversation with. *The Hobbit* is short. This house reads it first.

The Jackson films are a cousin. Film order is close for the main quest and is not a reason to skip the Hobbit book.

## Silmarillion is earlier in the calendar, later on the shelf

*The Silmarillion* was assembled after Tolkien’s death. Chronological charts put it (and sometimes *The Children of Húrin*) before *The Hobbit*. That is a reread or a lore project. For a first read it is the wrong temperature: a mythic summary written for people who already care why the later quest is expensive.

**House rule:** finish Hobbit and LotR. If you still want the First Age, open *The Silmarillion*. If you bounce, come back later. Do not “prepare” for Frodo by reading the Ainulindalë first.

## FAQ

**Can I start with The Lord of the Rings?**  
You can. *The Hobbit* is still the published on-ramp this house uses.

**Should I read The Silmarillion before The Hobbit?**  
Not for a first read. That is the contested order this page exists to label.

**Do I need Unfinished Tales?**  
No. It is a later shelf.
