## Why chronology lies here

Belgarath and Polgara cover thousands of years before Garion leaves the farm. Chronology charts therefore put them first. Both books are written from after *The Seeress of Kell*. They treat the endings of ten novels as settled history.

*Pawn of Prophecy* is Belgariad book one in print. The Malloreon five come after *Enchanters' End Game*. The companions come after that, Belgarath then Polgara, if you want them at all.

## The working order

Belgariad five → Malloreon five → companions optional. The Elenium / Tamuli is a different Eddings world. Full tables on the [Belgariad / Malloreon reading-order guide](/guides/belgariad-malloreon-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Can I skip the Malloreon?**  
Yes. The Belgariad closes.

**Is Polgara first?**  
No. After Belgarath, and only after both quintets.

**Elenium first instead?**  
Allowed as a completely separate Eddings door. Not Belgariad book one.
