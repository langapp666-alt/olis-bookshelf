## Why The Sun Trail looks first

Dawn of the Clans is set before the original books, and *The Sun Trail* is that arc’s book one. Chronology charts therefore open there. The official Warriors reading guide does not. It starts with The Prophecies Begin, the six books that opened in 2003, and it recommends Dawn of the Clans only after Omen of the Stars.

Super Editions, manga, and graphic-novel adaptations are not that door either.

## What this house does

*Into the Wild* through *The Darkest Hour*, then The New Prophecy if you want the next arc. Dawn of the Clans stays a labeled prequel. Later arcs are named, not fully tabulated, on the [Warrior Cats reading-order guide](/guides/warrior-cats-reading-order/).

For another long children’s shelf with a later cycle that looks like book one, see [Percy Jackson](/guides/percy-jackson-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Is Into the Wild the same series as The Prophecies Begin?**  
Yes. The subtitle came later. *Into the Wild* is still book one.

**Can Dawn of the Clans be read alone?**  
It stands more alone than a mid-series novel. This house still does not start there.

**What about the graphic novels?**  
Adaptations. Not a new order.
