## Why Empire looks like a cleaner start

*Daughter of the Empire*, *Servant of the Empire*, and *Mistress of the Empire* are a tidy trilogy with Janny Wurts. *Magician* is older, and the US split into *Apprentice* and *Master* makes it look like two books plus a confusing third. *A Darkness Returns* and *King of Ashes* are what a new-book table displays. None of those is the Riftwar door.

## What this house does

*Magician* (or Apprentice then Master — same novel), then *Silverthorn*, then *A Darkness at Sethanon*. Written order next is *Daughter of the Empire*, with *Prince of the Blood* between the Empire books. A block-read of the three Empire novels first is a labeled alternate, still after the Riftwar three. Later cycles are named, not fully tabulated, on the [Riftwar reading-order guide](/guides/riftwar-reading-order/).

For another 1980s epic with a false first book, see [Where to start Osten Ard?](/questions/where-to-start-osten-ard/).

## FAQ

**Are Apprentice and Master two novels?**  
No. One novel, split. Then *Silverthorn*.

**Can I skip Empire?**  
You can follow only Midkemia. Do not start there.

**Is A Darkness Returns the new book one?**  
No. Start at *Magician* for this cycle.
