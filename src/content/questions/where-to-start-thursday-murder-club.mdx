## Written order is the honest path

Penguin’s own FAQ says you can hop. The people still accumulate. For a first read: *The Thursday Murder Club*, *The Man Who Died Twice*, *The Bullet That Missed*, *The Last Devil to Die*, *The Impossible Fortune*.

The Netflix film is a cousin of book one. It is not a reason to skip to book five.

## We Solve Murders is a different desk

*We Solve Murders* starts a different Osman series. It is not Thursday Murder Club 6, a spin-off that continues Coopers Chase, or a gentler on-ramp. If a shop piles it next to *The Impossible Fortune* as the next hardcover, that is retail gravity.

## FAQ

**Do I have to read them in order?**  
For a first pass, yes. The cases are separate; the people are not.

**Is We Solve Murders book 6?**  
No.

**Film first?**  
The film is a cousin of book one.
