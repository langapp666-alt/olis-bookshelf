## The first major novel

Gabaldon's chronology page numbers the major novels and says they should be read in order. *Outlander* (1991) is the first. *Dragonfly in Amber* is second. *Voyager* is third. Lord John books become safe to visit after *Voyager*. They are not an on-ramp.

Nine major novels are out. A tenth, *A Blessing for a Warrior Going Out*, is listed as forthcoming. Do not wait for it before opening book one.

## The show is a cousin

Season order follows the early novels more closely than most adaptations, then compresses. It is still not a substitute for *Outlander*.

## The major novels

The nine, and the Lord John shelf labeled, live on the [Outlander reading-order guide](/guides/outlander-reading-order/). This page only names book one.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Outlander book 1?**  
*Outlander* (1991), Diana Gabaldon. Not a Lord John novel. Not *Dragonfly in Amber*.

**Can I start with Lord John?**  
No. Those books sit, in story time, around *Voyager*.

**Is the television series book one?**  
No. Start the novels at *Outlander*.
