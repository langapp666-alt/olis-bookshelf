## Wool is book one. Silo is the series name.

The Apple TV+ series is called *Silo*. The books were called *Wool* first. Same underground argument. If your paperback says *Wool Omnibus*, you are holding book one in the form Howey later collected.

Howey’s own series page lists *Wool*, then *Shift*, then *Dust*. Chronological charts that open with *Shift* are a reread toy. *Shift* is written for people who already have *Wool*’s questions.

## The original parts are not sequels

*Wool* began as a short and grew into five linked parts. Together they *are* the novel. If you own the omnibus, do not also buy the five parts thinking they are later books. *Shift* likewise collected three parts; the collected novel is the intended second book.

Optional Silo shorts exist. They are texture after the trilogy, not a fourth novel. *Sand* and Howey’s other shelves are not Silo 4.

## FAQ

**Where to start the Silo books?**  
*Wool*. Then *Shift*. Then *Dust*.

**Wool or Silo — same series?**  
Yes. *Wool* is the first novel. *Silo* is the series / show name.

**Should I read Shift first?**  
No. It is a prequel published second.

**Is the Apple TV+ show a substitute?**  
No. Show order is not book order.
