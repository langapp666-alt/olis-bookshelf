## Why the 1995 book is the door

*Practical Magic* was a standalone for twenty-two years. *The Rules of Magic* and *Magic Lessons* are later prequels. *The Book of Magic* is the sequel. Publication order is how the Owens curse was met: the sisters first, then the aunts as young women, then Maria in the 1600s, then the close.

Hoffman’s own series page lists both sequences and calls it your choice. That is true. Chronology is coherent. It is also how Amazon and many “complete series” widgets make *Magic Lessons* look like book one.

## What chronology actually does

Story time: *Magic Lessons*, *The Rules of Magic*, *Practical Magic*, *The Book of Magic*. You follow the family tree from Maria forward. You also postpone the novel the rest of the shelf is answering.

**House rule:** publication first. Chronology is a reread, or a stubborn family-tree taste. It is not required, and it is not what the 1995 book was designed to be.

*The Invisible Hour* is a different Hoffman novel. It is not Practical Magic 5. The 1998 film is a cousin.

## FAQ

**Publication or chronological?**  
Publication for a first read. Hoffman allows chronology. Amazon’s chronological numbering is not a command.

**Is Magic Lessons book one?**  
In story time, yes. In publishing history, no. Start with *Practical Magic*.

**Is the series finished?**  
The four Owens novels are a complete visit.
