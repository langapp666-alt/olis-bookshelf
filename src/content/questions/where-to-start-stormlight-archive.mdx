## The novels, then the novellas

Publication order is the Stormlight order: *The Way of Kings*, *Words of Radiance*, *Oathbringer*, *Rhythm of War*, *Wind and Truth*. *Wind and Truth* closes the first five-book arc. Books 6–10 are later work, not a reason to wait.

*Edgedancer* sits after book two. *Dawnshard* sits after book three. They help. They are not secret novels. If your library lacks them, keep going.

## Leave the Cosmere spreadsheet alone

Sanderson lists *The Way of Kings* as a Cosmere start. Tidbits from other planets are optional. Do not read *Warbreaker* first in order to “prepare.” *The Way of Kings* is the preparation.

For “which planet first?” use the [Cosmere starter](/guides/cosmere-starter-reading-order/). For Era 1 as a finished trilogy, use [Mistborn](/guides/mistborn-era-1-2-reading-order/). Neither is a Stormlight prequel.

## FAQ

**Do I need the rest of the Cosmere first?**  
No.

**Do I need Edgedancer and Dawnshard?**  
No. Useful, not required.

**Is Wind and Truth the end?**  
It ends the first arc. The series is planned to continue.
