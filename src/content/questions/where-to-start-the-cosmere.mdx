## Pick one Cosmere door

Mistborn Era 1 is the shorter finished trilogy. Stormlight is the long series still in motion, with a long first book. Either is honest. This house slightly prefers *The Final Empire* if you want a complete argument sooner.

You do not need both first chapters “to decide.” That is how people start neither.

## What is not a Cosmere on-ramp

*Warbreaker*, *Elantris*, and *The Emperor’s Soul* are real doors. They are not required before Mistborn or Stormlight. The secret-project novels are later treats, not Cosmere book one. *Skyward* is not Cosmere.

*Arcanum Unbounded* is a collection, not a starter pack.

## FAQ

**Do I need the whole Cosmere?**  
No. Series are sequential inside themselves. Crossovers are optional texture.

**Mistborn or Stormlight first?**  
Either. Mistborn Era 1 if you want a finish line. Stormlight if you want the large current series.

**Should I read Warbreaker before Words of Radiance?**  
No. Read it later if a trick made you curious. It is not Stormlight homework.
