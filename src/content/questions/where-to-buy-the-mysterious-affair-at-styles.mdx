## Two book ones, two detectives

Christie is not one series. *The Mysterious Affair at Styles* (1920) is the first Poirot novel. *The Murder at the Vicarage* (1930) is the first Miss Marple novel. Pick one detective and stay there for a while. This page names Styles, the earlier door. Marple's first novel is a different book.

## What is not book one

*Curtain* and *Sleeping Murder* were held back and published last. They close those detectives. They are not on-ramps. *And Then There Were None* is a famous standalone. It is not Poirot 1 and not Marple 1.

## Both paths

Poirot novels and Marple novels, in publication order, with standalones kept off the ladders, are on the [Agatha Christie reading paths](/guides/christie-reading-paths/). Each novel has its own row there, including *The Murder at the Vicarage*.

## FAQ

**Where to buy Poirot book 1?**  
*The Mysterious Affair at Styles* (1920), Agatha Christie.

**Where to buy Marple book 1?**  
*The Murder at the Vicarage* (1930). It is not Styles, and it is not *Sleeping Murder*.

**Is And Then There Were None book one?**  
No. It is a standalone. It does not open either series.

**Can I start with Curtain?**  
No. It is Poirot's last case, published late on purpose.
