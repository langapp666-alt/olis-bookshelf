## Two finished-enough Maas doors

[Throne of Glass](/guides/throne-of-glass-reading-order/) is a complete assassin-to-epic run. Start with the novel *Throne of Glass*, not *The Assassin’s Blade*.

[ACOTAR](/guides/acotar-reading-order/) is the courtly romantasy door. Start with *A Court of Thorns and Roses*, not *Silver Flames*. Further ACOTAR novels are announced; that is not a reason to wait, and it is not a reason to treat TOG as ACOTAR book zero.

There is no required “Maas chronological order” that turns book one into a syllabus.

## Crescent City is the spoiler engine

Crescent City book one can stand as its own case. From *House of Sky and Breath* onward, named crossovers arrive. If you care about that, finish TOG and get through the published ACOTAR run before Crescent City book two. See the [Crescent City guide](/guides/crescent-city-reading-order/).

If you do not care about crossovers, you can read Crescent City as urban fantasy and treat the cameos as noise. That is a taste, not a rule.

## How to choose if you only want one

Pick TOG if you want a finished long fantasy with a contested prequel. Pick ACOTAR if you want faerie courts and a novella that looks like a novel. You can read the other later. You cannot make Crescent City book two into a gentle on-ramp for either.

## FAQ

**Do I need TOG before ACOTAR?**  
No.

**Do I need ACOTAR before TOG?**  
No.

**Should I start with Crescent City?**  
Not if the other two series are on your list and spoilers bother you. Start with *House of Earth and Blood* only if this is the series you actually want.
