## Cordelia, then Miles

*Shards of Honor* (1986) is the series door this house uses. *Barrayar* was published later and sits the next day; it is not optional preamble you skip because you want Miles. The two are also sold as *Cordelia’s Honor*. Same novels.

*The Warrior’s Apprentice* is allowed if you insist on Miles first. You will meet Cordelia as history. Still read *Shards* and *Barrayar* before you are done. Do not skip ahead to *Memory* or start at *Komarr*.

## What chronology charts get wrong

Internal chronology and publication order disagree. Bujold favors chronology with adjustments. **Falling Free first** because it is earliest is the usual trap: different century, different cast. Read it later; before *Diplomatic Immunity* if you want the quaddies first.

*Ethan of Athos* is a side novel. The *Borders of Infinity* collection is the tidy extra after *The Warrior’s Apprentice*. Neither is book one.

## FAQ

**Shards or Warrior’s Apprentice first?**  
*Shards* for Bujold’s spoiler-sensitive door. *Warrior’s Apprentice* if you want Miles immediately. Not *Falling Free*.

**Do I need the novellas?**  
No. The novels carry the argument.

**Is Falling Free book one?**  
No. It is centuries earlier.
