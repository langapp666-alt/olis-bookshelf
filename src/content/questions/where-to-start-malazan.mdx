## Hard on purpose, still book one

*Gardens of the Moon* drops you into a campaign already in motion. That is the method, not a printing error. Starting at a later “easier” book will not fix it; it will strand you on a different continent with the same expectation that you keep up.

The ten Book of the Fallen novels are one argument. *Deadhouse Gates* and *Midnight Tides* look like side trips. They are not optional. *Dust of Dreams* and *The Crippled God* are a split ending — read them back to back.

## What not to open first

A circulating chronological list that puts prequels first is a timeline exercise, not a first-read syllabus. **Kharkanas** (*Forge of Darkness* onward) assumes you already care about people the ten introduce as myth. Esslemont’s Novels of the Malazan Empire overlap the ten; they are not Book of the Fallen 6.5, and *Night of Knives* is not an easier on-ramp.

If you want a long epic that explains itself as it goes, this is the wrong shop. Try [Stormlight](/questions/where-to-start-stormlight-archive/) or [Wheel of Time](/questions/where-to-start-wheel-of-time/).

## FAQ

**Is Gardens of the Moon a bad book one?**  
It is a hard book one. It is still book one.

**Can I start with Midnight Tides?**  
You can. You will be on the wrong continent with no reason to care yet.

**Should I read Kharkanas first?**  
No.
