## Book one is the 1965 novel

Publication order. There is no contested sequence inside Frank Herbert’s six. You can stop after *Dune* and have read a novel. You can stop after *Chapterhouse: Dune* and have read the series Herbert kept writing.

The Villeneuve films cover *Dune* and then *Dune Messiah*. They are cousins. Film part two is not a license to skip the 1965 novel.

## What this page is not

This is not [Should I read the Brian Herbert Dune books?](/questions/should-i-read-brian-herbert-dune/). That hub is the stop/continue question after Herbert’s six. *Hunters of Dune* and *Sandworms of Dune* are an optional later finale. Jihad and House prequels wait. None of them are book one.

## FAQ

**Dune books in order — where do I start?**  
*Dune* (1965). Then Herbert’s remaining five if you want the series he kept writing.

**Should I only read Frank Herbert?**  
For a first read, yes: the six. Brian Herbert books are optional later.

**Should I read the prequels first for “background”?**  
No. They are later books about earlier time.

**Can I start with the movies?**  
You can watch first. Start the books at *Dune*.
