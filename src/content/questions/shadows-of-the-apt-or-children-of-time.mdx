## Why one author looks like one series

*Children of Time* and *Shards of Earth* are the Tchaikovsky books most shops push. Shadows of the Apt is the earlier ten-book fantasy run. Echoes of the Fall (*The Tiger and the Wolf*) is yet another trilogy. None of them is book one of the others.

## What this house does

If you want Apt: *Empire in Black and Gold* through *Seal of the Worm*, publication order. If you want the science-fiction trilogy, use the [Children of Time guide](/guides/children-of-time-reading-order/). If you want the space-opera three, use the [Final Architecture guide](/guides/final-architecture-reading-order/) and start at *Shards of Earth*. The Apt table is on the [Shadows of the Apt reading-order guide](/guides/shadows-of-the-apt-reading-order/).

## FAQ

**Can I start Apt at The Air War or Seal of the Worm?**  
No. Book one is *Empire in Black and Gold*.

**Are the Apt short stories required?**  
No.

**Is the ten finished?**  
Yes.
