## Publication order is the only path

Bobiverse is one continuous argument: a man becomes many probes, and the many keep talking to each other. There is no chronological prequel hiding in the middle of the list.

The first three novels were written as a complete trip. Books four and five continue the same voices. They are not a new series with a new book one.

## What is not an on-ramp

*Heaven’s River* is later and longer. It assumes the copies already exist. The announced sixth book is not an excuse to wait, and it is not hiding as book one.

## FAQ

**Can I start at book four?**  
No. Four and five continue the same voices.

**Is Heaven’s River a good first Bobiverse novel?**  
It is later. Start with *We Are Legion (We Are Bob)*.

**Do I wait for book six?**  
No. Five novels are already a sequence.
