## Out of scope as primaries

Do not open a guide whose main job is competing with a thousand Harry Potter, Tolkien, Westeros, or Star Wars order pages. Mention those series only as contrasts. Standalones (for example *The Invisible Life of Addie LaRue*) are not reading-order pages.
